Holding only the stocks whose index is in BUY helps the strong parents and hurts the weak ones

The rule, and the test it had to pass

Today each AA book flips its whole parent basket into a 50/50 gold/cash sleeve when a count-weighted score of the index signals drops below 0.60. The rule tested here decides stock by stock instead. At each event, every holding is tagged with its market-cap index. It is kept only if that index's weekly supertrend is BUY, at its parent weight. The weight of every name whose index is in SELL goes to 50% gold and 50% cash.

  • Tags, point in time. Real membership from index_constituents_monthly where a snapshot exists (from 2026-04-09). Before that, the monthly market-cap rank band from mcap_based_universe: 1–100 Nifty 100, 101–250 Midcap 150, 251–500 Smallcap 250, 501–900 Microcap 250. Securities are matched on ISIN.
  • Events. Tags and targets are rebuilt only when one of the four index signals flips or the parent rebalances. Between events the book drifts, so a stock crossing a rank boundary never trades on its own.
  • Unknowns. A non-member with a rank uses its rank band. A name ranked beyond 900 reads Microcap 250. An unranked name reads Smallcap 250. Only the first case occurred: Happy Forgings (0042) and TCI (0043), 29 holding-weeks in all.

The hypothesis, its comparator and six gates were written down and hashed before anything ran. The comparator is the current whole-book rule, re-simulated on identical prices, fills, costs, sleeve and tags. D is the new rule's CAGR minus the comparator's, at 20.8 bps round trip.

Books are the four AA portfolios; their parents are 0003, 0007, 0010 and 0029. G0 is a precondition on the data rather than a test of the rule. A failed G0 does not stop the verdict, but the verdict is marked as conditional on it.

Is the edge a property of the rule, or of this history?

G4 keeps every weekly signal but slides the whole four-index matrix through time, wrapping at the ends. Each shift keeps the indices' co-movement and every episode length intact. If holding by segment is structurally better than switching the whole book, D should stay positive under most shifts. G5 keeps the signals and instead mislabels rank-sourced tags at the rate monthly ranks actually get membership wrong, to see whether D survives classification error.

Why the parent baseline does not match the published parent (G0)

A rule can only be compared with its parent if the simulated parent tracks the real one. It does on three books, within the ±1 pp/yr bar, but not on 0045. The diagnosis builds a third series, a replica: each basket held from its availableDate close at parent weights, close to close, on our prices. That splits the gap in two.

  • Index convention (published − replica). No held name moves more than ±25% in a day, so there are no split artefacts, and on a typical day the replica tracks the published NAV to 0.007%. About half to two-thirds of the gap sits on the ~39 rebalance sessions. The platform prices its basket switch at its own fills, and depending on which day's close the switch is placed at, the gap moves by ±2–3 pp/yr. It cannot be reproduced without those fills.
  • Price source. Prices come from ohlcv_eikon, joined on each security's permanent quote_id. The Kite table (merged_historical_data_with_rics) has three gaps. It loses stocks across delisting and relisting: Kirloskar Ferrous, 11–14% of 0003 in 2021–22, has no bars there before 2026-04-20. It has no rows at all for EQUITAS or SHRIRAMCIT. And it is not adjusted for demergers or special dividends: Siemens −25%, NIIT −44%, Aster DM −22% on their event days. On Kite prices 0042's gap was −1.89 pp/yr; on Eikon it is within the bar.
  • Execution cadence (replica − harness). The harness picks up a new parent basket at the next Friday review and fills the following session. That is exactly how the live AA books trade: they execute on the Monday after a review, and in 2022–23 often weeks after a parent rebalance. Every simulated strategy shares this cost, so D is unaffected. It is a real part of the gap between an AA book and its published parent.

Method and limits

Mechanics. Positions are held in units. Fills are at the next session's OHLC4, and positions are marked at forward-filled closes. Cost is charged on each position's change, half the round trip per side. Gold is GOLDBEES; cash is the dividend-reinvested LIQUIDBEES level. These are Study 2's mechanics. The v2 engine reproduces Study 2's archived scorecard exactly, and matches its simulator to on identical inputs.

Window. 2021-11-03 → 2026-08-21, weekly reviews. Every series is rebased on 2021-11-03, the last session in the published files on or before the first review. The 2021-11-04 Diwali session is missing from those files. No simulated book holds anything before its first fill on 2021-11-08.

Limits.

  • One sample. Four books over five years contain 12–14 flips per index. The same 48 events drive all four books, because parents rebalance on the same dates and the signals are global.
  • Mislabelled holdings. Monthly ranks agree with real membership on only of these books' holdings. G5 carries that error.
  • Membership tags never mattered. Every week from 2026-04-09 onward, all four signals agreed. Real-membership tags therefore changed no trade in this window.
  • Prices from Eikon. Stock prices come from ohlcv_eikon, joined on quote_id. This series is split-, bonus- and demerger-adjusted but not dividend-adjusted, so it is price return, like the Nifty indices. Every one of the 257 securities held in the window can be filled at every entry. The first versions of this report ran on Kite prices; the "Kite prices" sensitivity shows that switching source moves D by at most 0.26 pp. The switch, and two earlier repairs to the Kite panel, are logged as deviations in METHODS.md.
  • Close fallback. A session with a close but no open fills at the close. This touches 14 cells on Eikon prices.
  • Price-index benchmarks. Nifty indices exclude dividends.
  • More in METHODS.md. Formulas, denominators and source files for every chart are in AA/analysis/evidence/segment_v2/METHODS.md.
