How the three rules decide

All three switch the whole book between the parent basket and the 50/50 gold/cash sleeve once a week, filling on the next session. They differ only in which weekly supertrend readings they look at.

So N100 AND SC100 and Smallcap 100 only disagree in exactly one state: small caps trending up while large caps trend down. Everything that separates them happens in those weeks. The AND rule exits on whichever index breaks first and re-enters only when the second one turns.

Where the 2021–26 gap comes from

Two rules earn different returns only on the sessions where one holds equity and the other holds the sleeve. So the gap between them is exactly the sum, over those sessions, of equity return minus sleeve return, signed by whichever rule held equity. That pins the edge to specific weeks.

The mechanism: what each signal state predicts

This is the test of why. For every week, take the state of the two signals and look at what small caps, large caps and gold did over the following four weeks (about a month), starting from the next session. If the AND rule works for a reason, the one state where it disagrees with Smallcap 100 alone ("only Smallcap 100 BUY") should be followed by poor equity returns.

When it wins and when it loses

Year by year, the AND rule's edge over Smallcap 100 only is close to zero most of the time. It is large in a few years, positive and negative. The positive years are the ones where a rally led by small caps alone failed. The negative years are the ones where it kept going.

Why the 2021–26 curve looks so clean

How robust is it in-sample?

These are return-space runs on each book's harness parent and the 50/50 sleeve. Shift null: the joint N100/SC100 signal history is rolled through time by every offset of at least 8 weeks (236 runs), keeping the two signals' co-movement. Delay: every decision acts 1 or 2 weeks late. Best stretch: the single most helpful disagreement stretch against Smallcap 100 only.

Timing the gold inside the sleeve

Study 1 tried putting gold's own trend into the sleeve. While the book is defensive, the sleeve holds 100% gold if gold's weekly supertrend is BUY, and 100% cash if it is SELL. In 2021–26 that beat the fixed 50/50 sleeve on 3 of 4 books, and it made drawdown worse on all four. This re-tests it on the full harness, including costs on every gold/cash switch. It runs under every equity rule, in-sample and on the actual parents 2014–21, a stretch that includes four years when gold went nowhere. The gold signal is the production supertrend (5-bar weighted ATR, multiplier 2) on weekly GOLDBEES bars from Eikon, read on the same Friday as the index signals.

The key control is "fixed, same gold share". It is a constant split holding exactly as much gold, on average, as the trend version ended up holding while defensive. If the trend sleeve beats the 50/50 only because it holds more gold, it will not beat this control. If it times gold well, it will.
