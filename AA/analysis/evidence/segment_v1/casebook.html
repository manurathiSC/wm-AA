The Overlay Casebook

Two studies on the same asset-allocation overlay: what was asked, what was found, what was wrong, and what is still open.

One pattern runs through all of it: every control we added made the claim smaller. Nothing here was overturned by an outside objection — each reduction came from a test we chose to run. That is the most useful thing to take from this document, and it is why the open items at the end matter more than the headline numbers.

Study 1 — Where the overlay's returns came fromcomplete

The AA version holds exactly its parent when risk-on, so the difference between them is earned only on the days they differ. In log space that split is additive and therefore exact — it reconciles to ~1e-17, with no residual:

log(AA) − log(parent)
  = Σrisk-on excess  →  TRACKING
  + Σrisk-off excess  →  TIMING
Tracking (risk-on)Timing (risk-off)

Two books gained, two lost — and for different reasons. 0044 lost mostly to tracking, 0045 almost entirely to timing.

Tracking should have been zero — while both are in equities they hold the same basket. It was not. The cause turned out to be narrower than a general drift: the parents reconstitute quarterly, and the AA books intermittently failed to adopt a reconstitution. On 2022-09-15 parent 0010 rotated six of fifteen names and 0044 kept the old six until end-October — 40% of the book wrong for ten weeks. All four desynchronised first on the same date. That is an operational defect, not a property of the strategy.

This is the table that reframed the study. The switch did not, in general, dodge a fall — in three of four cases the parent rose while the book sat out of it. It earned a better asset instead, because gold nearly doubled. Strip gold out and the edge goes with it.

Which trend predicts gold: monotonic in market cap

Perfectly ordered by market cap. The smaller the companies whose trend you follow, the better it marks gold's good periods — and a broad cap-weighted index (N500*) lands at the 50th percentile, no better than random.

Two conclusions followed. Going broader is not the answer: Nifty 500 is ~76% Nifty 100 by weight, so its trend is a large-cap trend. And requiring two indices to agree beat every single index — Nifty 100 AND Smallcap 100 averaged +10.6%/yr and won on all four books, while signalling off each book's own largest bucket averaged +3.0 to +4.9 and was worse than what was already running.

Study 2 — Segment-level selectionin progress

If the overlay is blunt, the obvious fix is to stop flipping the whole book. Classify each holding into a market-cap bucket, hold the stocks whose own bucket is trending, and send the rest to the sleeve. Three arms were built: sleeve (freed weight to gold+cash), rotate (freed weight redistributed, stays in equity), and sleeve_n100gate (also requires Nifty 100 to be trending).

Getting a trustworthy classifier was most of the work

Point-in-time classification was the binding constraint. The membership table holds only five snapshots, all from 2026 — using it would tag 2022 holdings with 2026 membership, mis-tagging precisely the stocks that changed size. That bias sits directly on the mechanism being tested. It was resolved by finding a market-cap table carrying 412 snapshot dates back to 2005 — every month from 2021-01 to 2026-08 present — then validating the rank-band proxy against production's own bucket output rather than assuming it.

The gate passed, and found spec drift on the way

Reproducing production's own intermediates — its bucket distribution and signal score across 22 Fridays and 4 books — matched at 100% (max abs error 3.8e-05) with the signal score exact. Three corrections were needed to get there, and each is a finding.

  • The signal input set is not constant. Production's own log shows it computing the score from Nifty100 | MC100 | SC100 until 2026-04-17, then switching to Nifty100 | Midcap150 | Smallcap250 on 2026-04-24.
  • The rebalance history is not always what production ran on. On 6 book-weeks it held a basket differing from any row in the history file — adopting a reconstitution early, then reverting. The same defect class Study 1 found.
  • 8 rows are untestable, not failing — they predate the first membership snapshot, so production's own classification source no longer exists.
v2's rule (Smallcap 250)earlier rule (Smallcap 100)

Mean across the four books. The live track record was produced by the Smallcap 100 rule (98.9% overall), not by the Smallcap 250 rule in production today (89.9%).

This matters beyond bookkeeping. It means the published track record and any backtest of the current engine are not measuring the same strategy, and it retroactively explains why Study 1 found two books reconciling as pure Smallcap 100 rules. The gate was redefined around production's own intermediates precisely because reproducing history was unachievable.

The structural claim is weaker than we assumed

Segment selection was supposed to rarely sit at either extreme. It does so less than an all-or-nothing rule — but not much less:

Arm0% equityPartial100% equityAvg equity
rotate31.0%0.0%69.0%69.0%
sleeve31.0%18.0%51.0%60.0%
sleeve_n100gate41.4%11.0%47.6%51.8%

The reason is in the hold rates, which are near-identical across buckets — Largecap 57.8%, Microcap 58.8%, Midcap 61.3%, Smallcap 60.6%, Unknown 48.8% — because the bucket signals move together. The same fact made the dedicated-microcap-signal variant differ from the production convention on only 2% of weeks, which is why that arm was demoted to a sensitivity. The actionable size distinction in this data is small-versus-large, not micro-versus-small.

Corrections ledger

Six substantive errors were found and fixed across the two studies. This is the section that should calibrate how much weight to put on everything above — both because the errors were material, and because they were found by controls rather than by readers.

Parent 0003 read 93% smallcap without it and 58% microcap with it. Microcap also compounded fastest, so every benchmark was understated.

Two further items are limitations rather than errors, and both remain live: the window contains a single uninterrupted gold bull market, and the published NAV carries two known divergences from any backtest of the current engine — the equity-leg desync and the spec drift above.

Still openprovisional

Study 2's headline should not be cited as settled. One outstanding control can materially deflate it, and it has been pre-registered precisely so the conclusion cannot drift to fit the number once it exists.

  1. The n100_only control. Hold the whole parent when Nifty 100 is trending and the sleeve when it is not, with no bucket selection at all. Nifty 100's hold rate is 59.9% and the gate arm is 41% fully defensive, so the exposure profiles are nearly identical — a plain regime filter may capture most of the edge. Pre-registered: if it captures ≥70%, the finding becomes “a large-cap regime filter works” and the segment mechanic is reported as not proven.
  2. The all-cash sleeve. Cash returned ~4.99%/yr against the sleeve's 17.98%, so a 30% allocation swings from roughly +2.0pp of tailwind to −2.5pp of drag. The static component reverses sign and the arm ranking may invert, with the most defensive arm hurt worst. Actual-sleeve and all-cash will be reported as co-equal headlines.
  3. A null on the timing term. The +5.25pp still has no null beside it. Because the bucket signals move together, permuting which bucket is trending is low-powered; the primary null block-relocates the whole signal panel in time instead.
  4. Classifier distribution uncertainty. The gate validated the formula using production's own classification. Our point-in-time classifier reproduces production's exact bucket distribution on only 64% of book-weeks — per-holding accuracy is 95.7%, but the aggregate that actually feeds the signal score diverges more often. The arms rest on that classifier.
  5. Two unpriceable names. EQUITAS and SHRIRAMCIT are pre-merger entities with no rows in the price table, at 13.3% weight of one 0010 rebalance — and 0010's flip is the headline. That flip needs confirming without them.
