The pair rule wins in bear markets because small caps crack first and large caps don't chase their rallies

Out of sample, holding equity only when Nifty 100 and Smallcap 100 are both in BUY cut the worst fall from −77% to −29% on small caps. It also beat Smallcap 100's own signal in 93% of 5-year windows. It beats buy-and-hold on return only in periods with a real bear market.

Out of sample 2007-07 → 2021-10 · in-sample 2021-11 → 2026-08 shown for completeness, never as evidence · production weekly supertrend · price-return indices, LBES cash, GOLDBEES · 20.8 bps per switch · evidence: AA/analysis/evidence/why_n100sc100/deep/

  1. What it is: a strong drawdown filter, and a better small-cap timer than small caps' own signal. It is not a proven way to beat buy-and-hold on return. On the Smallcap 100 index, out of sample, it made 14.4% a year with a −28.6% worst fall. Buy-and-hold made 7.6% (−77.5%), and Smallcap 100's own signal 8.6% (−50.1%).
  2. Where the edge comes from: bear markets. In Smallcap 100 bear phases, labelled with hindsight, it added +20.9 log-points a year over the whole period. It paid back 9.5 in recoveries, by re-entering late, and 4.6 in bull phases.
  3. Why it works: the two signals make different mistakes. Small caps break first at tops: at the 12 small-cap falls of 20%+ in 2006–2020, Nifty 100 never sold first. Large caps refuse to confirm small-cap bear-market rallies: the pair's false entries were 22%, against 39% for Smallcap 100 alone. In the weeks when only Smallcap 100 said BUY, small caps fell at a 44% annualised rate. So the pair takes 29% of a fall but 45% of a rally. Smallcap 100 alone takes 52% and 47%.
  4. How sure:
    • Against Smallcap 100 alone: P = 94% in a resampling test.
    • Worst fall against buy-and-hold: shallower in 100% of 3- and 5-year windows. The −28.6% is shallower than 99.9% of 775 randomly shifted signal histories.
    • Return against buy-and-hold: only P = 80% on small caps, 63% on the blend and 37% on Nifty 100.
  5. What to expect:
    • worst falls of about −20% to −30%;
    • well ahead of buy-and-hold in years with a deep fall (+34 pp a year on average in 2008, 2011 and 2018);
    • behind in straight bull years (−22 pp);
    • far behind when small caps boom without large caps, as in 2021 (Jan–Oct: +21% against +52%).

The year pattern is the same on every index. The pair wins big in bear years, roughly ties in crash-then-V years (while beating Smallcap 100's own signal by +17 pp), and loses about 20 pp in straight bull years. On small caps it beat buy-and-hold in 8 of 14 years. Its worst fall inside the year was shallower in 11 of 14. Against Smallcap 100's own signal it was better in 7 years, equal in 3 (the signals never disagreed) and worse in 4. The worst year against both was 2021 Jan–Oct: small caps boomed while Nifty 100 stayed in SELL.

The edge survives without hindsight. Against Smallcap 100's own signal, almost all of it (+5.2 of +5.3 log-points a year) is earned when small caps are already below their 40-week average. That is where a small-cap BUY without large-cap confirmation is usually a bear-market rally. The pair also pays most in high and mid volatility, and when small caps are not leading. One idea is not supported: that the pair helps more when large and small caps move apart. The correlation terciles show no such pattern.

While "only Smallcap 100 BUY" is in force, small caps returned −44% annualised. The state is short (median 3 weeks) and is followed by the deepest interim falls. Over 13 weeks the average turns positive, because the state usually resolves into "both BUY" and the pair re-enters then. Only 28% of these weeks sit inside a hindsight bear phase, so "mostly bear-market rallies" overstates it. But that 28% is where the damage is.

At tops, small caps are the alarm. Smallcap 100's signal sold first 5 times and in the same week 3 times. Nifty 100 never sold first. In the other 4 falls Nifty 100 was already in SELL at the peak, so the pair was out before the fall began: small caps had rallied without large caps. At bottoms, large caps are the gate. The pair re-entered in the same week as Smallcap 100 alone in 6 of 12 cases, and one week later in 3. In 2008 it waited 53, 36 and 16 weeks, against 6, 3 and 2, and each wait kept it out of the next leg down.

In plain words. Nifty 100's signal, used alone to time small caps, already delivers most of the pair's return (13.9% against 14.4%), because it does not chase small-cap bear-market rallies. Smallcap 100's own signal adds the early exit, since small caps crack first, and that cuts the worst fall from −43% to −29%. "AND" keeps both strengths: leave on the first warning, come back only when the whole market confirms. "OR" keeps both weaknesses (8.1% and −50%). Any large-cap AND small-cap pair works about as well.
