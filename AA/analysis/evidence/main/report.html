2. The spread splits exactly into two parts

An AA version holds precisely its parent when risk-on, so the difference between them is earned only on the days they differ. In log space the split is additive and therefore exact — it reconciles to 3e-17 — with no residual term:

log(AA) − log(parent)
  = Σrisk-on excess  →  TRACKING
  + Σrisk-off excess  →  TIMING

Tracking should be zero: while both are in equities they hold the same basket. It is not zero, and it cost 0044 nearly 3 points a year, most of it inside one 93-session risk-on stretch in late 2022.

Comparing the AA books against the parents' own rebalance histories identifies the cause, and it is narrower than a general drift. The parents reconstitute quarterly (median gap 91 days, 19 rebalances in the window) and the AA books match them on 97–98% of holdings overall. But every AA version intermittently failed to adopt a reconstitution: on 2022-09-15 parent 0010 rotated six of fifteen names, and 0044 kept the old six until the end of October — 40% of the book wrong for ten weeks. All four desynchronised for the first time on the same date, 2022-09-19, and it recurs on 5–6% of in-equity reviews.

b. Shift the timing

Moving the realized state earlier or later shows whether the actual switch dates were well chosen.

The actual timing sits at or near the bottom of the curve for all four. Acting five weeks earlier would have been worth 150 to 236 percentage points instead of −5 to +61.

Acting earlier is not implementable — it uses information that did not exist yet — but it is diagnostic. The falls the signal was meant to avoid had largely already happened by the time it fired, and the portfolio then stayed defensive through the recovery. That is the classic lag of a trend-following filter, and it is visible in all four independently.

How to read null sd, observed sd and MDE

What the null is. Someone claims they can pick when to take holiday to get good weather — 6 trips, 45 days, great weather every time. To test it you take those same 6 trips, same lengths and slide them to random dates, 10,000 times. Same trip count, same durations, same total days off; only when changes. That is this test, applied to each book's risk-off episodes.

null sd is the spread of those 10,000 random outcomes — how much your result bounces around with no skill at all, purely from where the episodes land. 0043's is 0.247 in logs, which is large: random placement routinely lands between roughly −20% and +80%. Its observed +59.2% against a null mean of +23.9% is an edge of +28.4%, and 16 of every 100 random placements still beat it.

observed sd is how much our own measurement would swing if history had rolled differently. The total is the sum of 6 episode contributions, so we resample those 6 with replacement 2,000 times and take the spread: 0.330. The ratio matters because 0.247/0.330 = 0.749 means the statistic's own wobble is wider than the whole null distribution — so “84th percentile” is unstable, and a slightly different history could have put the same strategy at the 40th or the 99th. Below 1, the percentile overstates the evidence. All four are below 1.

MDE asks, before looking at any answer: how big would a real effect have to be for this test to reliably find it? 2.80 × null sd, where 2.80 = 1.96 (the 5% bar) + 0.84 (80% power). For 0043 that is +99.6% cumulative. Its actual edge is +28.4% — one third of the smallest effect the test can resolve. So the finding is not “no skill”; it is “this test could never have answered the question”.

Why so underpowered, and what would fix it. Six episodes. Noise falls as roughly 1/√n, so resolving the edge each book actually delivered would need about 75 episodes for 0042 (~60 years at this switching rate), 46 for 0043 (~37 years) and 854 for 0044. That is the binding constraint on the whole study, and it is why every negative here reads underpowered, not absent — and why the mechanism findings carry more weight than any significance test in this report. Section 4b quotes gold at 27.3% a year and this table at 27.90% — the same series, annualised two ways. 27.3% is CAGR on calendar time, the convention used everywhere else; 27.90% assumes 252 sessions a year where this window actually holds 247.0. This is the most favourable evidence in the study, and it splits the four cleanly. The two portfolios that outperformed held gold when gold was compounding at 38.6% and 40.1% a year against an unconditional 27.9% — an edge of 10.7 and 12.2 points. The two that underperformed held it at almost exactly average times (−0.8 and −1.8 points). So the difference between the winners and the losers is not only how much sleeve they held but when.

Two things stop this being a finding of skill. It is not significant — 84th and 88th percentile, z of 1.00 and 1.23, against an MDE of about 28 points, so an edge of 11–12 points is well inside what random placement produces. And more fundamentally, no gold data enters the rule anywhere. The signal is a supertrend on equity indices; gold's weight never varies. Whatever gold timing exists is a by-product of equity-trend weakness coinciding with gold strength.

That co-movement has a real economic basis, so some persistence is reasonable to expect. But it is an exposure the strategy happens to carry, not a capability it exercises — which matters, because the two readings imply different things about whether it repeats, and one of them invites adding a gold signal that does not currently exist.

The 60% threshold is not a tuning knob

This is the test that was impossible with the weight files alone. Sweeping the threshold on each identified blend, holding everything else fixed:

Step functions, because the underlying signals are binary per index. Levels here are not comparable with the realized attribution — this is a clean re-implementation on the parent with a fixed 50/50 gold-cash sleeve, so it carries no tracking drift and no bond leg.

The threshold is not a dial. It is a three-way choice of logic. Each index signal is binary, so a two-index blend can only produce four scores — and the threshold merely picks which of three rules you are running:

AND wins on all four books, without exception — and every threshold from 0.60 to 0.95 gives the identical answer, because they all mean AND.

So 0044 and 0045 are already at the best point available, on a plateau running 0.60 to 0.95. The step at 0.60 is a step up onto that plateau, not a precarious ledge: the risk was only ever in setting the threshold lower, which would demote them to SINGLE and cost 2.5 and 7.2 points. An earlier version of this section called that a “cliff edge” and a “genuine fragility”; that reading was wrong and is withdrawn.

The two books actually mis-set are 0042 and 0043, which sit at SINGLE and give up +0.50 and +0.04 points a year by not requiring the second index to agree.

This is the same structural finding that section 9 arrives at independently and recommends as N100 AND SC100. It was already visible here.

Fifteen rules were tested and the best reported, so some of that +10.6 is selection. Confirm on parents outside these four before changing anything.

Worth separating from the gold question: Microcap 250 is the best gold predictor but the worst standalone equity signal (+4.5 average). Value-add is avoided-equity plus sleeve-earned, and those two objectives want different indices. Don't read the gold table as a signal recommendation.

10. What this says about the strategy

Pulling the evidence together:

  1. The overlay is a risk reducer, and a good one. Every AA version cut its parent's drawdown (0042: −20.1% vs −31.9%) and every one has down-capture well below up-capture. That is a real, repeatable structural property, independent of any timing skill.
  2. The return gain came from the asset, not the timing. Gold supplied 84% of the sleeve's contribution and compounded at 27.3% a year. Replace gold with cash and every positive result collapses.
  3. The signal fires late. Consistently, across all four portfolios, five weeks of earlier action would have been worth far more. In three of four the parent actually rose during the avoided windows.
  4. Timing skill is unproven, not disproven. Against a random-placement null, none of the four clears the noise. Six to nine episodes is too few — the MDE is about +100%.
  5. The overlay is only worth adding to a weak equity book. The relationship between parent selection alpha and overlay value is nearly perfectly inverse. On 0010 and 0029, whose stock selection adds 2.4–3.0 points a year, the overlay is a tax.
  6. There is an operational cost nobody was accounting for. The AA books intermittently missed their parents' quarterly reconstitutions — 0044 ran 40% of its book wrong for ten weeks from September 2022 — costing it 3.0 points a year. Nothing to do with the signal, and the cheapest item here to fix.
  7. The best available change is structural, not a reweighting. Holding equity only when Nifty 100 and Smallcap 100 are both trending averages +10.6 points a year against +6.8 today, and beats the current rule on all four books. Choosing the signal by the book's own largest exposure — the intuitive policy — averages +3.0 to +4.9 and is worse than today on every basis tested.
  8. Gold's timing is the best evidence available, and it still is not skill. 0042 and 0043 held gold while it compounded 11–12 points a year above its own average; 0044 and 0045 held it at average times. But the effect sits at the 84th and 87th percentile of a random-placement null, and no gold input exists in the rule at all.
  9. Two of the four ran a different rule than documented, and one of them sits on a cliff. 0042 and 0043 are pure Smallcap 100 supertrend, not a market-cap-weighted blend. 0044 and 0045 do use blends at 60%, but their entire counterfactual benefit appears in the single step at 0.60 and vanishes at 0.55.
  10. 0044's signal was the wrong index, not just late. A plain Nifty 100 supertrend on its parent would have added 6.4 points a year against the +0.07 its Midcap-150-heavy blend delivered.
  11. Gold prediction runs monotonically down the size curve. Microcap 250 trend marks gold's good periods by +22.6 points a year, Smallcap 250 by +20.9, Nifty 50 by −8.4. A broad cap-weighted index — Nifty 500 included — is mostly a large-cap index and lands at zero.
  12. The filter should be less twitchy, not faster. Raising the multiplier from 2 to 2.5 cuts switches from 12 to 9 and improves every book; dropping it to 1 triples the switching and destroys the edge.

On 0043 specifically. It looks like the best overlay because it is the best overlay — its signal beats the other three on every parent. But its headline return is the lowest of the four, its edge over its parent exists mainly because that parent gave up 6.7 points a year to its own market-cap blend, and 92% of its risk-off gain came from one eight-month window in which gold rallied. The right reading is that 0043 rescued a poor stock-selection engine, not that it found a better way to time the market.

Method, and what would change these conclusions

The allocation state comes from the realized rebalance files, keyed on availableDate so nothing is used before it was live; skipped=true reviews are observations of unchanged holdings, not gaps. The defensive sleeve is reconstructed from actual per-instrument weights, which changed from gold/cash/bonds at a third each (to Apr-2023) to gold/cash at 50/50 (from Oct-2024).

Reconstruction check, restated precisely. On fully-defensive sessions the AA is the sleeve, so its published NAV is external ground truth. Excluding the three sessions either side of any state change, the reconstruction tracks it to −0.52% to −1.03% a year. Including those switch-adjacent sessions the gap is +1.30% to +4.82% a year, and essentially all of it sits in two days — 2022-02-14 and 2025-01-13, the first sessions of episodes 2 and 5, where the book transacts at fill prices while this study marks at the close. That is an execution effect, not a reconstruction error, but an earlier version of this paragraph quoted only the interior figure without saying which sessions it covered.

One known understatement in the section 4 sleeve column. build_panel.py:sleeve_return prices every defensive instrument from raw closes, and LIQUIDBEES holds a constant NAV of 999.99 because it distributes its yield — so a third of the early sleeve's weight is credited 0%. Using the adjusted total-return series instead would raise the section 4 sleeve figures by +1.43 to +1.57 percentage points cumulative. The direction is conservative for this report's conclusion, and gold's 84% share is unaffected because sleeve_leg_split uses the total-return series. Measured by validate_section4.py → validate_section4.csv.

Counterfactual arms substitute the parent for the AA's equity leg, so they measure timing alone. They use a fixed 50/50 Gold BeES / cash sleeve spanning the whole window. Cash is the adjusted LIQUIDBEES series (1000 → 2389 over 2007–2026, +4.99% a year across this window, following a realistic rate path from 2.4% in 2021 to 5.9% in 2024): the raw price history returns exactly 0.00% because the fund holds a constant NAV and distributes its yield, so using it would credit the cash leg with nothing.

Signals come from the weekly per-index W_allRebal.xlsx files, read as ST == FUB → defensive / ST == FLB → equity, applied from the session after their date. Specifications were identified by searching all one-, two- and three-index combinations and thresholds against the realized states, reaching 99.6–100% agreement.

Limitations

  • The threshold sweep is a clean re-implementation, not the realized strategy: it prices the parent rather than the AA's equity leg and uses a fixed gold-cash sleeve, so its levels are internally comparable across thresholds but not against the attribution in section 2.
  • Nifty 500 is synthetic, but validated. It is a 76 / 16 / 8 cap-weighted blend of Nifty 100, Midcap 150 and Smallcap 250 — exactly its constituents by rank — because the published series in the price database ends 2025-09-15. Checked against the real index over their 1,643 overlapping sessions it tracks at 0.9998 daily return correlation and 0.39% annualised tracking error, and its weekly supertrend agrees with the real one on 97.5% of weeks. Sound for this purpose.
  • Market-cap buckets are estimated from returns, not holdings — unavoidably, since index membership history does not exist before 2026-04. See section 8b for the bias this carries and why no conclusion here rests on it.
  • Microcap 250's history starts 2022-01, the shortest of the eight signals, and it is the selected best of eight tests. Its significance does not survive a multiple-testing correction.
  • Identified specs are fits, not documentation. At 99.6% agreement roughly five sessions in 1,185 still disagree, and several weight combinations tie on the same score, so the index sets are established more firmly than the exact weights.
  • Small sample. Six to nine episodes over 4.8 years. Every episode-level number carries that.
  • One gold regime. The window contains a single, exceptional gold bull market. The conclusion that gold drove the result is well evidenced; any expectation that it repeats is not.
  • Gross of costs and taxes, per scope; breakeven cost per round trip is given instead.
  • 0042's weight history ends 2026-06-11 while its index runs to 2026-08-21; the last 48 sessions hold its final known state.
  • Market-cap index levels are spliced at 2025-09-15 from a second source, chained on returns; the overlap disagreement is under 0.23%. This affects only the implied-blend fit, not the attribution.
  • Implied blends have R² of 0.71–0.77, so selection alpha carries residual factor exposure the three indices do not span.
