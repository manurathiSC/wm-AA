How rare is gold's 2026 fall, and what came after the last ones?

Terms on this page

Gold
MCX gold futures (MAUc1, ₹ per 10 g, weekly Friday close), 2003-11 → 2026-11-06. The two weeks after that are GOLDBEES' weekly returns, because the MAUc1 export ends on 2026-11-06.
σ (sigma), z-score
A k-week fall divided by gold's normal k-week swing, measured over the 52 weeks before the fall. A z of −2 means a fall twice as large as usual for that length of time.
Drawdown
How far gold sits below its highest weekly close so far.
Gold signal (MAUc1)
The desk's weekly supertrend on MCX gold. On SELL the 80/20 sleeve holds 20% GOLDBEES and 80% cash.
Three-index switch
IN only when Nifty 100, Midcap 100 and Smallcap 100 supertrends are all BUY. Otherwise OUT, and the book holds the gold/cash sleeve.
Event, analog
A week where the 8-week fall reached a set z (main cut: z ≤ −1.75). Later qualifying weeks within 26 weeks count as the same event.

What the gold signal did after those falls

“Weeks to BUY”: weeks until the MAUc1 signal turned BUY again. “Gold until BUY”: what gold did in those weeks, which the 80/20 sleeve mostly missed (it holds 20% gold while SELL). “Further fall”: the lowest close in the next 26 weeks against the event week.

Why gold is falling

  • A hawkish Fed. Markets now price a likely US rate rise in December. The October minutes (released 2026-11-18) and a Fed governor's comments pushed the odds up. CNBC, The National
  • A stronger dollar and higher real yields. Gold pays no interest, so higher real yields raise the cost of holding it. Reports put the dollar index at a one-year high. CNBC, GoldSilver
  • Selling to raise cash. Reports describe forced selling in global equities, with gold sold to meet margin calls because it is liquid. The National
  • A crowded trade unwinding. Gold set a record in late January 2026 (our MCX daily close peak is 2026-01-29) after the 2025 rally, which rested on rate cuts and a weak dollar. Both have reversed. Spot gold is reported roughly a third below its January record. Zee Business
  • In India, MCX gold fell about 4% on 2026-11-20 to near ₹1.22 lakh per 10 g and made a new low near ₹1.20 lakh on 2026-11-25, after our data ends. CNBC-TV18, Upstox
  • Not everyone sees a top. ING calls the move a reset, not a reversal. ING

These come from news reports gathered on 2026-11-25. They disagree on exact levels, and none of them is a test. Our own data has no dollar or yield series to check the causes against.
