## 1. Idea and references

**The idea in one sentence.** In the weeks when the three-index supertrend says SELL, keeping the SCMO_0003 stocks that pass a simple technical test (close above a moving average, or a weekly SuperTrend on BUY) and putting only the rest into the 50/50 GOLDBEES and cash sleeve earns more, after cost, than keeping a random set of the same number of stocks.

**Where it came from.** T03 showed the three-index switch times the parent better than chance (F-AA-S04, PROVISIONAL), but it goes *fully* defensive in SELL. T02, a descriptive study, kept the stocks above their 50-day EMA during SELL and got 19.9% CAGR against 21.6% for the full production overlay (SCMO_0042). T02 had no null, no pre-registration, used external data (Wm-SellRegRes) instead of the AA project's reviewed holdings matrix, and a 100% GOLDBEES sleeve. T04 asks the question properly.

**The decision this will change.** Whether the production rule should hold some equity during SELL weeks (and by which indicator), or stay fully defensive as T03's rule does.

**What is already known.**

| Ref | What it says | Relationship |
|---|---|---|
| T03 (COMPLETE 2026-11-05) | The three-index switch times SCMO_0003 better than the same spells placed at random: C1 percentile 96.0, C2 97.5 (F-AA-S04, PROVISIONAL) | depends on: T04 uses T03's regime series and engine conventions unchanged |
| T02 (descriptive, 2026-11-05) | 50-day EMA filter in SELL: 19.9% CAGR against 21.6% for the full AA overlay | contrasts: no null, external data, 100% GOLDBEES. Seen, so no result of T04 is out of sample |
| F-AA-M01 (T01) | A timing edge of 12 to 18 pp/yr is the smallest detectable with four books | depends on: §3 computes the MDE for this test |

**What this test is NOT asking.**
- Whether the three-index switch times the market (T03 answered that).
- Whether partial equity during SELL beats full defensive. That is a comparator (§3), not a null: more equity can earn more simply because equities rose.
- Whether any of this generalises to SCMO_0007, SCMO_0010 or SCMO_0029. One parent only.

## 2. Hypothesis and pre-registered criteria

**Written before any run, 2026-11-06.** What was already known:
- The five indicators were chosen by hand, not found by search, but they were chosen after T02 showed the 50-day EMA result. This is not out of sample.
- The regime series is T03's, which has been seen. T04 tests selection *within* SELL, not timing.
- T02's descriptive result (19.9% CAGR with a 50-day EMA filter, 100% GOLDBEES sleeve) has been seen. It is on different data and a different sleeve, so it does not fix any number here.
- No T04 return, drawdown, selection count or percentile of any kind was computed before this section was written.
- **Expectation, declared:** a selection edge that survives a 98th-percentile bar with 9 independent SELL spells is unlikely. The most likely result is that no indicator passes C1, and that the best indicator's CAGR is explained by holding more equity (the constant-mix comparator).

**Hypotheses** (after cost at 20.8 bps round trip; SCMO_0003 only; one pair per indicator I1 to I5):
- **H1ᵢ, selection.** Indicator Iᵢ selects stocks that earn more during SELL than a random selection of the same size. Judged on after-cost CAGR. Falsified if C1ᵢ fails.
- **H2ᵢ, protection.** Indicator Iᵢ's selections avoid deeper drawdowns than random selections of the same size. Judged on maximum drawdown. Falsified if C2ᵢ fails.

**The five indicators** (all from the stock's own close prices, point in time):

| # | Indicator | Keep a stock if | Parameters |
|---|---|---|---|
| I1 | 50-day EMA | close > EMA₅₀ | k = 2/51; EMA₀ = mean of the first 50 closes; strict inequality |
| I2 | 200-day EMA | close > EMA₂₀₀ | k = 2/201; EMA₀ = mean of the first 200 closes; strict inequality |
| I3 | 50-day SMA | close > SMA₅₀ | mean of the last 50 closes; strict inequality |
| I4 | 200-day SMA | close > SMA₂₀₀ | mean of the last 200 closes; strict inequality |
| I5 | Weekly SuperTrend | the supertrend is BUY | on the stock's own weekly candles; mul = 2.0, lookback 5 weeks, linearly weighted true range (the desk's algorithm, reimplemented in the engine) |

**Null: N_sel (selection shuffle).**

| Dimension | Randomised / held / not matched | How | Measured in §4 as |
|---|---|---|---|
| which stocks are kept on each SELL decision | **randomised** | k stocks drawn uniformly without replacement from the n in the basket | — |
| k, the number kept, on each SELL decision | held | the indicator's own count on that decision, for the basket in force | per draw and decision: kept = k (V3) |
| the regime series (BUY or SELL each week) | held | the strategy's | — |
| weights of the kept stocks | held | their basket weights, not scaled up; the rest of the weight goes to the sleeve | — |
| BUY-regime behaviour | held | the whole basket at its weights | per draw: BUY targets equal the strategy's (V3) |
| parent rebalances, calendar, sleeve split, cost, execution | held | constant for the run | — |
| the sleeve's return path | **not matched** | different random picks leave different weeks and amounts in the sleeve, so gold and cash are sampled differently | null CAGR spread |

**Draws.** 2,000 per indicator and cost. Seed `numpy.random.default_rng([4, i, draw_id])` with i = 1…5 for I1…I5 and draw_id = 0…1,999; the same draw ids for every cost and sleeve variant. The draw makes one uniform number per stock per decision; on a decision, the k stocks of the basket with the smallest numbers are kept (a uniform random k-subset). Details in §3.

**Best-of-sweep null (R5).** To guard against picking the best of five indicators after seeing them: for each draw_id, take the highest after-cost CAGR among the five indicators' draws of that id (a best-of-five random sweep). The strategy's best CAGR among I1…I5 is ranked against these 2,000 values with the same percentile rule. Pass mark 98.

**Which null decides.** N_sel, per indicator. The best-of-sweep null (R5) decides the status of any finding, not the per-indicator verdicts.

**Statistic the null is judged on.** After-cost CAGR for H1ᵢ, higher better. Maximum drawdown on daily NAV for H2ᵢ, shallower better.
- **Unit:** one realisation is indicator × cost. The percentile is computed against its own draws.
- **Percentile:** (draws worse + 0.5 × draws tied) ÷ draws × 100. Ties are equal within a relative 1e-12.
- **p-value:** (1 + draws at least as good) ÷ (draws + 1), reported beside the percentile; the pass mark is on the percentile.
- **Why CAGR answers H1ᵢ:** the null holds how many stocks are kept and when, and moves only *which* ones. If the indicator's stocks beat random same-size subsets, the percentile is high.
- **Secondary statistics**, each against the same draws: volatility of daily returns × √252 (lower better); Ulcer index (lower better); days at least 10% below the running peak (fewer better); longest time under water (shorter better); Calmar = CAGR ÷ |maximum drawdown|.

**Multiplicity.** Five indicators are tested with equal standing at a family-wise α = 0.10, Bonferroni: α/5 = 0.02 each, so the pass mark is the **98th percentile** (T03 used 90). 2,000 draws give a resolution of 0.05 points and a smallest p-value of 1/2001; (D + 1) ≥ m ÷ α = 5 ÷ 0.10 = 50 is met. Each indicator has two criteria (C1ᵢ, C2ᵢ); they are the two readings of one test of that indicator and are not further corrected.

**Pre-registered pass criteria** (after cost at 20.8 bps; per indicator):

| # | Criterion | Pass condition |
|---|---|---|
| C1ᵢ | Selection (H1ᵢ) | after-cost CAGR percentile against the N_sel null ≥ 98 |
| C2ᵢ | Protection (H2ᵢ) | maximum-drawdown percentile against the N_sel null ≥ 98 |

**Construction, in order.** Every convention below is fixed here; none is left to the engine.

1. **Calendar.** D17's dates from 2019-01-02 to 2026-10-22, then the D18 sessions after it (2026-10-23 → 2026-11-04, nine sessions; none is a special session). The eight D18 sessions D17 lacks are never read after 2019-01-02 (F-AA-D15), for prices, for indicators and for the weekly candles. Before 2019-01-02 all D18 dates are used (indicator history only).
2. **Decision dates.** T0 = 2019-01-02, then the last session of each ISO week after T0. T0 is the window's base. The decision at 2026-11-04 is not traded.
3. **Regime at decision date d.** The latest Master row of each of the three indices dated on or before d; BUY iff all three SIGN are BUY, else SELL. This is T03's rule. The decision series must equal T03's headline `decisions.csv` for SCMO_0003 on every date they share (checked and printed, not a gate).
4. **Baskets.** A basket takes effect on the first calendar session on or after its availableDate; baskets flagged `csv_only_skipped` are dropped; its weights are normalised to sum 1 over the whole basket (as in T03). A kept stock keeps its normalised weight: weights are not scaled up when stocks are dropped.
5. **Indicator values.** From each stock's own bars (never forward-filled): `close` is the last close on or before the decision date d, taken only if that bar is at most 7 calendar days before d; otherwise the stock is excluded. The EMA of a stock at bar m ≥ N (N = 50 or 200) starts at the mean of its first N closes and then EMA = k × close + (1 − k) × previous; the SMA is the mean of the last N closes. A stock with fewer than N bars, or (I5) with fewer than 5 weekly bars, cannot be computed and is **excluded** (fails the filter). Strict inequality for I1 to I4. I5: weekly candles are ISO weeks (Monday to Sunday) of the stock's own bars, high = max, low = min, close = last; true range = max(high − low, |high − previous close|, |low − previous close|) (the first candle: high − low); weighted ATR = Σ (weight × TR) over the last 5 candles, weights 1…5 divided by 15; centre = (high + low) / 2; BUB/BLB = centre ± 2 × ATR; the final bands and the trend ratchet follow `analysis/supertrend.py` (written out in the engine, not imported); BUY iff the supertrend sits on the final lower band; not yet defined or on neither band is excluded. The candle read at d is the candle containing the stock's last bar on or before d.
6. **Selection in SELL.** At a SELL decision d the indicator's pass set is the stocks that pass. The kept set is the pass set intersected with the basket in force. Its count k and the basket size n are logged (`selections.csv`). Stocks not kept have their weight in the sleeve: 50% GOLDBEES and 50% LBES cash (R1: 100% GOLDBEES). If k = 0, the whole book is in the sleeve; if k = n, the book equals the BUY book. Both are counted and reported.
7. **Target.** At any session t, the book's target is a vector of stock weights x(t) plus the sleeve. In BUY: x = the basket in force at t. In SELL: x = the basket in force at t, with stocks outside the kept set set to zero. The decision in force at t is the latest decision whose trade session is on or before t (T0's decision before the first trade). The kept set at t is the pass set of that decision intersected with the basket in force at t, so a parent rebalance during SELL re-applies the filter on the same decision's pass set to the new basket's stocks.
8. **Trades.** On the next calendar session after each decision, and on each session where a new basket takes effect, compute x(t). **A trade happens if and only if x(t) differs from the last target** (exact comparison). The last target at T0 is the T0 decision's own target. When a trade happens, at that session's fills, with V = value of all holdings at fill prices, every line is traded to its target: stock i to x_i × V, GOLDBEES and cash to (1 − Σx) × V × share (50/50; R1 100/0); traded = Σ |target − held| over every line; cost = traded × cost_bps ÷ 2 ÷ 10,000; every target is then scaled by (V − cost) ÷ V (T03's single trade rule). Fills: stocks OHLC4 of the Eikon bar (close if open, high or low is blank; the last close if the stock has no bar that session), GOLDBEES de-spiked OHLC4, LBES level. No trade happens at a session where x(t) equals the last target, so a SELL week with no change in the kept set costs nothing and the kept stocks drift.
9. **Initial position at T0 close**, NAV 1.000, no cost, in every realisation including the nulls: the basket in force at T0, as drifted since its availableDate (units from OHLC4 on the first D18 session on or after it, scaled to value 1 at T0's close), with the stocks outside T0's kept set sold at T0's close into the sleeve (50/50). In BUY the kept set is the whole basket.
10. **NAV, statistics.** Daily NAV = Σ units × close + GOLDBEES units × close + LBES units × level; no sale at the end. years = (sessions − 1) ÷ 252; CAGR = NAV_end^(1/years) − 1; maximum drawdown, Ulcer index, days at least 10% below the running peak, longest time under water, volatility, Calmar as in T03.
11. **Null draws (N_sel).** For draw_id and indicator i, `rng = default_rng([4, i, draw_id])`, `u = rng.random((J, N))` with J the number of decisions and N the number of stocks ever held (column order as in the D19 weight matrix). At a SELL decision j and a basket b the draw keeps the k_j(b) stocks of b with the smallest `u[j]`, where k_j(b) is the indicator's count of that decision for that basket (so the same u serves a parent rebalance in the same week). Everything else (targets, trades, costs) goes through steps 7 to 10 unchanged.
12. **Percentile, MDE.** Percentiles as in §2. After the run, the MDE for C1ᵢ at 80% power is (z₀.₉₈ + 0.84) × sd_null = 2.894 × the SD of after-cost CAGR across the draws, and the same for maximum drawdown, printed beside every FAIL. **Disclosed:** these numbers did not exist when this section was written; the thresholds in §2 do not depend on them. The effective independent unit is the SELL spell (T03: 9 out-spells to 2026-08, more now), not the 200-odd SELL weeks.
13. **Comparators** (benchmarks, not nulls; same calendar, cost and T0): hold the parent (decision fixed at IN; 0 cost and each cost) and the published NAV rebased; the full AA switch (T03's rule on this window: 100% sleeve in SELL); for each indicator, the constant mix: the average equity share f (the mean over decision weeks of Σx, BUY = 1) held as f × the basket and (1 − f)/2 each in GOLDBEES and cash, rebalanced on the next session after every decision date (T0's included) and at every basket change; SCMO_0042's published NAV rebased (descriptive, 2021-11-01 → 2026-08-21 only).
14. **R5.** For each draw_id d, the best of the five indicators' after-cost CAGRs of draw d. The strategy's best CAGR of the five is ranked against these 2,000. (The plan's "shared permutation seed" is read as "the same draw id".)

**How the test works, in plain words.**

- **The series.** The book's NAV is built every day from the stocks of SCMO_0003's basket. Every week the three index signals are read. On BUY the book holds the whole basket. On SELL it looks at each stock in the basket: if the stock passes the indicator it is kept at its normal weight, otherwise its share is moved into half GOLDBEES and half cash. *[the real signals, selections and NAV are printed in §4.0a]*
- **One decision.** On a SELL Friday with, say, 11 stocks, 4 pass the test: those four stay, and the weight of the other seven (their basket weights added up) goes to GOLDBEES and cash. The trade is on the next session at each instrument's (open + high + low + close) ÷ 4, paying 10.4 bps on every rupee traded. *[one real decision is printed in §4.0a]*
- **One null draw.** On the same Friday the draw also keeps 4 stocks, but chosen at random from the 11. Everything else is identical. 2,000 such draws, with the same random numbers reused across costs. *[the same Friday's real and random pick are printed in §4.0a]*
- **The ranking.** The indicator's CAGR is placed among the 2,000 draws' CAGRs: the percentile is the share of draws it beats (ties half). 98 or more is a pass for that indicator.
- **The comparators.** The full AA switch shows what no stock-picking in SELL earns. The constant mix holds the same average equity share in all weeks: it shows how much of any gain is only more equity exposure.

**Point-in-time rule.** A decision at d reads Master rows dated at or before d and bars dated at or before d (the engine asserts both and counts them). A trade is on a session after d, except a parent rebalance, which is on its availableDate session and uses the latest earlier decision's pass set. Master rows before 2021-11 are a backfill by the production code, not signals traded at the time (disclosed, not corrected).

**What the null controls for, and what it does not.** It controls for how many stocks are kept each SELL week, and so for the amount of equity exposure and the weeks in which it is held. It does not control for which weeks the sleeve is held (the sleeve is bigger in a draw that keeps fewer), nor for stock-specific effects such as survivorship in the basket itself (the basket is the parent's real holdings). Basket sizes of 5 to 15 mean that when k is near n there are few distinct picks (C(5,4) = 5); those weeks give the null little to shuffle, so §4 reports the weeks with 1 ≤ k < n.

**Known contaminants.**

| Contaminant | Handling |
|---|---|
| Five indicators, not chosen blindly; one parent | Disclosed. Bonferroni at α/5 = 0.02 per indicator; no generalisation without testing the other parents |
| The regime is T03's, already seen | Disclosed. T04 tests selection within SELL, not timing |
| Master rows before 2021-11 are a backfill by the production code | Disclosed; R4 repeats C1ᵢ on the live window |
| D11 (LBES) source unrecorded; extension (from LIQUIDCASE) about 0.5 pp/yr rich | As T03: bounded at about 0.09 pp/yr; R3 swaps in LIQUIDCASE |
| GOLDBEES fills after 2026-09-28 are not de-spiked (the MCX file ends there) | Disclosed; the raw-versus-close gap on each affected trade session is listed in `audit.json` |
| Small baskets (5 to 15 stocks) | Reportability gate and the per-decision (k, n) log |
| Indicator lookback reaches before the window | D18 starts 2014-07-01; the 200-bar indicators are computable from about 2015-04 for stocks that were already listed; stocks with fewer than 200 bars are excluded and counted |
| Eikon prices are price return, and Redington-style ordinary dividends are not corrected | As T03; the published NAV is itself price return and V4 holds the engine to G0 |
| F-AA-D15, D18's carried bars and out-of-range high/low | As T03 (calendar is D17's dates; negligible) |
| Draws re-use the same random numbers across costs and sleeve variants | By design: cost and sleeve differences are then paired, not independent |

## 5. Findings

### F-AA-S05 — During SELL weeks no stock filter keeps stocks better than a random pick of the same size on both return and drawdown; the 200-day SMA passes the return bar alone (CAGR percentile 98.3), and that edge is mostly lower turnover. SCMO_0003, 2019 → 2026-11, after 20.8 bps

**Status:** NOT ESTABLISHED (one criterion passes, and it fails the checks that decide status). All gates V1–V5 pass.
**Evidence:** `evidence/headline/` (`draws.csv`, `stats.csv`, `audit.json`), `analysis.log`, §4.1, §4.4 and the robustness table above.
**Claim:** against 2,000 random same-size selections, at the pre-registered 98th percentile: C1 passes for I4 (200-day SMA) only (CAGR 21.48% against a null mean of 18.19%, percentile 98.3, p = 0.0175); I1, I2, I3, I5 give 58.7, 94.8, 34.5, 77.9. C2 (maximum drawdown) fails for all five (15.7, 89.4, 3.0, 85.1, 87.5). No indicator passes both.
**Why it is not a finding of selection skill:**
- **Turnover.** A random pick changes every week; a moving-average filter does not. I4 trades 144 times against 217 for the average draw and pays 0.170 of NAV in cost against 0.267. At 0 bps I4's CAGR percentile is 93.7 (below 98); at 50 bps it is 99.9. The after-cost edge is largely the cost of the null's churn, which the null does not hold (§2 lists it only as "not matched"; this is the consequence).
- **Best-of-sweep (R5):** the best of the five indicators (I5, 21.61%) ranks at the 52.4th percentile of random best-of-five sweeps (p = 0.4758): FAIL.
- **Live window (R4):** I4's percentile is 85.1 on 2021-11 → 2026-11 with its own null; no indicator reaches 98 there.
- **Robustness that held:** R1 (100% GOLDBEES sleeve) 98.2 for I4; R3 (LIQUIDCASE) 98.3. These change the sleeve, not the turnover difference.

**Against the alternative (T03's full switch):** after cost the full defensive switch earns 28.45% with a worst fall of −18.4%; the five filters earn 19.6% to 21.6% with falls of −23.7% to −27.8%; holding the parent earns 16.66% with −46.3%. Keeping any stocks in SELL gave up 7 to 9 pp a year here. The constant mix at I4's average equity share (0.791) earns 15.42% and falls −37.1%.
**Falsified by:** a pre-registered rerun with a null that holds turnover (for example one that re-draws the random subset only when the indicator's own kept set changes), or on decisions that were not seen. The smallest edge detectable at 80% power is 3.9 to 4.3 pp of CAGR and 6.6 to 7.3 pp of worst fall (§4.3); with 10 SELL spells that is the effective sample.
**Cross-check:** `python tests/T04/analyse.py`; `python analysis/verify_t04.py`.
