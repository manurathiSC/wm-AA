T04: during a SELL regime, does a stock filter pick better than a random pick?

SCMO_0003 · 2019-01-02 → 2026-11-04 · five indicators, 2,000 random same-size selections each · after 20.8 bps · pre-registration sha256 47e48a835b8f

Verdict. all five gates pass. Indicators passing the pre-registered selection test C1 (CAGR percentile ≥ 98): I4; passing the protection test C2: none. Best-of-sweep null (R5): percentile 52.4, FAIL. Every indicator earns less than the full AA switch (28.4% a year after cost).

How to read this

Each week the three-index supertrend says BUY or SELL. On BUY the book holds the parent's whole basket. On SELL, the book keeps the stocks that pass an indicator (close above a moving average, or a weekly SuperTrend on BUY) and puts the rest into half GOLDBEES and half cash. The null keeps the same number of stocks each SELL week, but picks them at random. If an indicator's CAGR sits in the top 2% of those random picks it passes. Shaded bands on the curves are SELL weeks. Hover or touch any chart: the crosshair shows the x and y at the pointer and the tooltip lists every value drawn at that x.
