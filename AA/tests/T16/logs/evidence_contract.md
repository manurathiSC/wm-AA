# T16 evidence contract (what `analysis/t16_engine.py` writes, and what it means)

Written 2026-11-26 at Step 2. Evidence goes to `tests/T16/evidence/<run>/{w1,w2}/` (one run, the headline; a rerun is a new directory). Every file below exists per window. Dates are session dates; NAVs are 1.0 at the book's first session; the engine prints gates and counts only, no performance figure. **No draws, no seeds, no `draws.csv`** (T16 has no null). This contract is T15's with the changes listed in the last section.

Rules (10): `50-orig 50-half 50-liq 50-idx 50-all 80-orig 80-half 80-liq 80-idx 80-all` (T16.md 2.1). Indices: `Nifty100 MC100 SC100`. Costs: 0, 20.8 (primary), 50 bps round trip (a side is half). Books: `SCMO_0003 0007 0010 0029`. W2 calendars are per book (SCMO_0007 starts 2019-01-04). **W2 ends 2026-11-20** (W1 2019-01-02).

## Definitions the verifier and the analysis must follow

- **Decisions.** T0 and the last session of every later ISO week of the book's calendar. Signals: the latest Master row of each index and of MAUc1 dated on or before the decision date. IN iff all three SIGN are BUY. `B` = indices (order Nifty100, MC100, SC100) whose own SIGN is BUY; `k` = |B|.
- **Targets** (absolute shares of the book): `-orig`, `-half`, `-liq` as T14 (`e`, GOLDBEES `(1-e)*gw`, cash `(1-e)*(1-gw)`); `-idx`: IN parent 1; OUT-BUY the base sleeve (50/50: GOLDBEES 0.5, cash 0.5; 80/20: GOLDBEES 0.8, cash 0.2); OUT-SELL cash `1/(k+1)` and each index of B `1/(k+1)`, no parent, no GOLDBEES. **`-all`: IN parent 1; OUT-BUY the base sleeve; OUT-SELL cash 0.25 and each of Nifty 100, Midcap 100, Smallcap 100 0.25 whatever B, no parent, no GOLDBEES.**
- **Trade due** on the session after decision j (j >= 1, a session exists): T14's trigger for the six T14 rules; for `-idx` the key (IN / OUT-BUY / OUT-SELL with the set B) differs from decision j-1's; **for `-all` the key (IN / OUT-BUY / OUT-SELL) differs from decision j-1's (so a change of an index SIGN or of B inside OUT-SELL is not a trade)**.
- **A trade** at session t: V = value of every line at the session's fill prices (stocks OHLC4, GOLDBEES de-spiked OHLC4, cash at its level, each index at its own OHLC4); target value of a line = share x V; `traded` = sum of |target - held| over every line; `cost` = traded x cost_bps / 2e4; every target scaled by (V - cost) / V. Units are constant between trades; NAV at the close = sum of units x close (index close; the last close on a session without an index bar).
- **Kinds.** For every rule except `-orig`, a trade whose decision changes OUT-SELL membership is `regime_in` / `regime_out`; for `-idx`, a trade with OUT-SELL on both sides and a different B is `index_set_change`; otherwise `switch_in` / `switch_out` by the change of `e`, `sleeve_rebalance` for an unchanged `e`, and `basket_change` for a parent rebalance on its own session. `-half` (0 < e < 1): D1 partial basket trade as T14.
- **R4** (T16): on the decisions of one OUT-SELL spell the two `-all` rules hold 100% cash (`-liq`'s target); the key stays OUT-SELL, so the spell's entry and exit trade as for the real rule. **R5** multiplies each index's OHLC4 and close of session i by (1 - 0.005/252)^i; **R2** and **R3** as T14.
- **Initial position** at T0's close, no cost: the target of decision 0 (an OUT-SELL start: `-idx` holds cash and each index of B, `-all` holds cash and each of the three indices at 0.25 of NAV, at T0's closes).
- **V4(a) boundary (E17).** W1: every session and every trade. W2: sessions and trade dates on or before 2026-10-30 only, and the decisions on or before 2026-10-30 must equal T15's stored decisions; the three later decisions (2026-11-06, 11-13, 11-20) and the trade of the 2026-10-30 decision are new.
- **W2 inputs (T16.md 3):** calendar D17 `extended_2026-11-25/published_nav`, cut at the END; stock prices `extended_2026-11-25/holdings_ohlc/<p>_ohlc.csv` (W1: `data/holdings_matrix/<p>_ohlc.csv`); D03 `extended_2026-11-20/Master` (W1: `extended_2026-10-30/Master`); MAUc1 D24c `extended_2026-11-20/MAUc1_from_2003` (W1: D24b); GOLDBEES `why_gold_ohlc_eikon_extended_2026-11-23.parquet` (W1: `..._2026-11-04.parquet`), de-spike range D12 (W2) / D24b daily (W1); cash `LBES_Adjusted_Price_data_extended_2026-11-25.csv` and `liquidEtfFinal_extended_2026-11-25.csv` (W1: the 2026-11-05 files); index prices D25d `extended_2026-11-20/index_daily_2019_2026` (W1: D25). END of W2 = the earliest last SIGN date of the three D03 files and MAUc1 = 2026-11-20 (pinned, asserted).
