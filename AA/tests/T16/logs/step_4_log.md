3. **`-all` index weeks = OUT-SELL weeks** (all 49 / 54 in all spells); the `-idx` index weeks and spells are printed beside, labelled `-idx`.
4. **The k split** is a plain sum over the B stretches of each k of the ledger's `all_minus_idx_50` / `_80` (pp of period returns, not compounded); `k >= 1` = the k = 1 and k = 2 stretches; weeks per k printed beside; it does not attribute the difference to its two possible causes (the test cannot).
5. **R4 flips** are reported both ways: a headline PASS that fails when a spell is left out reads "the result rests on that spell"; a headline FAIL that passes reads "the failure rests on that spell" (T15 used the first wording for both). The R4 table marks the spell that ends at the window end (`exited_to` = window end: "open at the window end") and prints each spell's k = 0 weeks.
6. **Outcome map rows** are applied per window and base: C1 and C3 / C3 only / C3 fails (with C1 in brackets), C5 pass/fail (wording points to the k split), C6, C4, C2, R4 flips, R3 live agree or disagree, the two windows agree or disagree (C1-C6 patterns compared), a V gate fails (cannot occur in a printed result).
7. **Ties** (|difference| <= 1e-12) are listed as in T15 with the verdicts that would change if they counted as equal; the frozen unrounded rule decides. The page counts C2 and C6 ties (2 bases x 2 comparators x 4 books).
8. **Worked example picks** (fixed rules, stated on the page): SCMO_0003 (the first book), the first `regime_in` at 20.8 bps with k = 2, k = 1, k = 0, and the first `regime_out`; the audit's `B_change_not_traded` example is re-derived from the decisions (B before and after, next session) and the trade counts recounted from `trades.csv`.
