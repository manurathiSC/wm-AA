**Engine** `analysis/t16_engine.py` (a fork of `t15_engine.py`; 27 shared functions are byte-identical, checked by V4(d)). Decide on the last session of each ISO week and T0 from the latest Master rows on or before the date; trade on the next session at OHLC4 (index lines at their own OHLC4); 10.4 bps a side (20.8 round trip primary; 0 and 50 as R1); the first position is free; the last decision is not traded; units constant between trades; NAV = cash + sum of units x close (the last index close on a session with no index bar). `-all` in OUT-SELL = cash 0.25 and each of the three indices 0.25, no parent, no GOLDBEES; its target key is the regime alone, so it trades only on entering and leaving OUT-SELL. Robustness: R2 LIQUIDCASE cash leg; R3 own window (both R3 windows start in OUT-SELL); R4 leave one OUT-SELL spell out (`-all` holds cash only there); R5 index leg minus 0.5 pp a year (carried closes on the three Budget weekend sessions multiplied by that session's drag factor).

**Comparisons** (T16.md 2.3): for each window and base, C1 CAGR above `-orig`, C2 maximum drawdown shallower or equal to `-orig`'s, C3 CAGR above `-liq`, C4 CAGR above `-half`, C5 CAGR above `-idx`, C6 maximum drawdown shallower or equal to `-idx`'s, each judged by "the stated sign in at least 3 of 4 books" on the unrounded statistics after 20.8 bps; 24 in all. A pass is a description of seen weeks. Ties (|difference| <= 1e-12) are listed beside the verdicts.

**Statistics.** years = (sessions - 1) / 252; CAGR = NAV_end^(1/years) - 1; maximum drawdown = min(NAV / running peak - 1); Calmar = CAGR / |maximum drawdown|; volatility = SD of daily returns x sqrt(252). The k split of `-all` minus `-idx` is a plain sum over the B stretches of each k of the ledger's `all_minus_idx_<base>` (period returns, not compounded).

**Known limits.** The index leg is a price index (no dividends, no fund cost), not a tradable instrument; W1's parent is a rebuild with no published NAV to check it against; the four books share one market, so "3 of 4" is a consistency check; both windows and T14 / T15 were seen before the variant was proposed (post-hoc, no hold-out; the three new W2 weeks are not a hold-out); no null was run, so nothing here is a statement about chance. One verifier reading was decided after a first mismatch (R5 on sessions without an index bar) and moves 8 R5 rows by 1.5e-5 relative.
