## 2. Self-test (the corrupted copy)

A copy of the evidence with deliberate corruptions, each caught by its own check, the verifier exiting non-zero on the copy: **a B week flipped** (a `buy_<index>` and the `-idx` target changed in `decisions.csv`); **a B change not traded** (an `index_set_change` row removed from `trades.csv`); **an index leg at 1/2 where k = 2** (a `-idx` regime trade's `<index>_after` set to half of V_after); **a `-idx` regime week holding GOLDBEES** (a `-idx` trade's `gold_after` > 0 or a `GOLDBEES` row in `positions.csv`); a NAV column scaled by 1.001 in `daily_nav.csv`; **an index price shifted by one session** (the verifier's own copy of the index price series shifted, then the NAV check must fail); one trade removed from `trades.csv`; a ledger return altered; a stat (`cagr`) altered in `stats.csv`; a `robust_nav.csv` R4 NAV altered. Keep `verify_t14.py`'s corruption mechanics where they apply.
