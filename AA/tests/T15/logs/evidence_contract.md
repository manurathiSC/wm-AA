# T15 evidence contract (what `analysis/t15_engine.py` writes, and what it means)

Written 2026-11-25 at Step 2. Evidence goes to `tests/T15/evidence/<run>/{w1,w2}/` (one run, the headline; a rerun is a new directory). Every file below exists per window. Dates are session dates; NAVs are 1.0 at the book's first session; the engine prints gates and counts only, no performance figure. **No draws, no seeds, no `draws.csv`, no `draw_states.npz`** (T15 has no null).

Rules (8): `50-orig 50-half 50-liq 50-idx 80-orig 80-half 80-liq 80-idx` (T15.md 2.1). Indices: `Nifty100 MC100 SC100`. Costs: 0, 20.8 (primary), 50 bps round trip (a side is half). Books: `SCMO_0003 0007 0010 0029`. W2 calendars are per book (SCMO_0007 starts 2019-01-04).

## Definitions the verifier and the analysis must follow

- **Decisions.** T0 and the last session of every later ISO week of the book's calendar. Signals: the latest Master row of each index and of MAUc1 dated on or before the decision date. IN iff all three SIGN are BUY. `B` = indices (order Nifty100, MC100, SC100) whose own SIGN is BUY; `k` = |B|.
- **Targets** (absolute shares of the book): `-orig`, `-half`, `-liq` as T14 (`e`, GOLDBEES `(1-e)*gw`, cash `(1-e)*(1-gw)`); `-idx`: IN parent 1; OUT-BUY the base sleeve (50/50: GOLDBEES 0.5, cash 0.5; 80/20: GOLDBEES 0.8, cash 0.2); OUT-SELL cash `1/(k+1)` and each index of B `1/(k+1)`, no parent, no GOLDBEES.
- **Trade due** on the session after decision j (j >= 1, a session exists): T14's trigger for the six T14 rules; for `-idx` the key (IN / OUT-BUY / OUT-SELL with the set B) differs from decision j-1's.
- **A trade** at session t: V = value of every line at the session's fill prices (stocks OHLC4, GOLDBEES de-spiked OHLC4, cash at its level, each index at its own OHLC4); target value of a line = share x V; `traded` = sum of |target - held| over every line; `cost` = traded x cost_bps / 2e4; every target scaled by (V - cost) / V. Units are constant between trades; NAV at the close = sum of units x close (index close; the last close on a session without an index bar).
- **Kinds.** For every rule except `-orig`, a trade whose decision changes OUT-SELL membership is `regime_in` / `regime_out`; for `-idx`, a trade with OUT-SELL on both sides and a different B is `index_set_change`; otherwise `switch_in` / `switch_out` by the change of `e`, `sleeve_rebalance` for an unchanged `e`, and `basket_change` for a parent rebalance on its own session. `-half` (0 < e < 1): D1 partial basket trade as T14.
- **R4** replaces B by the empty set on the decisions of one spell (`-idx` becomes `-liq` there); **R5** multiplies each index's OHLC4 and close of session i by (1 - 0.005/252)^i; **R2** and **R3** as T14.
- **Initial position** at T0's close, no cost: the target of decision 0 (an OUT-SELL start of `-idx`: cash and each index of B at T0's closes).
