# T15 methods (how `evidence/headline/` and `evidence/report.html` were made)

Pre-registration: `tests/T15/T15.md` §1-§3 (frozen 2026-11-25, sha256 `343566dd…`, `python tests/T15/check_prereg.py`). Plan: `PLAN.md`. Reproduce everything: `bash tests/T15/run.sh` from `AA/` (about two minutes).

**Question.** When the three-index switch is OUT and the MAUc1 gold signal is SELL, does holding the liquid ETF plus each index whose own supertrend SIGN is BUY, in equal parts (`-idx`), change what the switch earns, on the 50/50 and 80/20 bases, 2014-09-01 -> 2019-01-02 (W1) and 2019-01-02 -> 2026-10-30 (W2)? Descriptive; no null and no chance claim (Manu's waiver, 2026-11-25).

**Data.** Only `APPROVED` rows of `data/APPROVED_DATA.md` (`python data/check_approved_data.py` exit 0: 70 approved rows). T14's inputs (D03 index signals, D24 / D24b MAUc1, D09 GOLDBEES with the T03 de-spike, D11 cash, D18 stock prices with the F-AA-D14 correction, D19 baskets, D05 dates, D17 published-NAV calendar in W2, D26 NSE calendar in W1) plus the daily index prices **D25 (W1) and D25c (W2, approved 2026-11-25)**. Each window's `inputs.csv` lists its files with sha256. Smallcap 100 prices before 2011-10-03 and signals before 2011-11-25 are never read.

**Engine** `analysis/t15_engine.py` (a fork of `t14_engine.py` with the null machinery removed and three index lines added). Decide on the last session of each ISO week and T0 from the latest Master rows on or before the date; trade on the next session at OHLC4 (index lines at their own OHLC4); 10.4 bps a side (20.8 round trip primary; 0 and 50 as R1); the first position is free; the last decision is not traded; units constant between trades; NAV = cash + sum of units x close (the last index close on a session with no index bar). Targets are absolute shares of the book: `-orig`, `-half`, `-liq` as T14; `-idx` in OUT-SELL = cash and each index of B (the indices whose own SIGN is BUY) at 1/(k+1) each, no parent, no GOLDBEES; a change of B is a trade. Robustness: R2 LIQUIDCASE cash leg; R3 own window (both R3 windows start in OUT-SELL); R4 leave one OUT-SELL spell out (`-idx` holds cash only there); R5 index leg minus 0.5 pp a year.

**Gates.** V1 `analysis/verify_t15.py` (a different agent; never opened the engine; own loaders and simulator): 603,123 (W1) and 954,208 (W2) comparisons, 0 failures, every planted corruption caught. V2 point in time, V3 Master `Index` = D25 / D25c close at every label used, V4 (a) the six T14 rules and hold-the-parent reproduce T14's stored NAVs and trades with a worst relative difference of 0.0, (b) `-idx` priced as cash equals `-liq`, (c) equal before the first index trade, (d) shared-code diff, (e) signals and B equal the Master files, (f) one-instrument identity and G0 / seam, V5 trade and cost identities. `analyse.py` refuses to print results if any gate fails.

**Comparisons** (T15.md 2.3): for each window and base, C1 CAGR above `-orig`, C2 maximum drawdown shallower or equal to `-orig`'s, C3 CAGR above `-liq`, C4 CAGR above `-half`, each judged by "the stated sign in at least 3 of 4 books" on the unrounded statistics after 20.8 bps. A pass is a description of seen weeks. Ties (|difference| <= 1e-12) are listed beside the verdicts.

**Statistics.** years = (sessions - 1) / 252; CAGR = NAV_end^(1/years) - 1; maximum drawdown = min(NAV / running peak - 1); Calmar = CAGR / |maximum drawdown|; volatility = SD of daily returns x sqrt(252).

**Known limits.** The index leg is a price index (no dividends, no fund cost); W1's parent is rebuilt, not validated before 2019-01-02; the four books share one market and one OUT-SELL holding; six index-holding spells per window; the variant was proposed after the windows and T14 were seen; there is no hold-out.
