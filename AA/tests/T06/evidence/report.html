Does the three-index switch still choose when to hold the parent better than chance when the random timings may use any spell lengths?

2019-01-02 → 2026-10-22 (SCMO_0007 from 2019-01-04) · four parents of the AA books · decisions every Friday, trades next session at OHLC4 · 10.4 bps a side · 2,000 draws per book, cost and null · every number is computed from evidence/headline/

The answer

Yes: the switch beats randomly timed versions of itself even when their spell lengths are free. Holding the parent only when the Nifty 100, Midcap 100 and Smallcap 100 are all BUY, and a 50/50 GOLDBEES and cash sleeve otherwise, against randomly timed versions of itself with the same number of switches and the same weeks invested, on 2019-01-02 → 2026-10-22, after 20.8 bps. Against N4.10 (free spell lengths, decides): return beats the 90th percentile in 4 of 4 books and the depth of the worst fall in 4 of 4 (pre-registered pass: 3 of 4 each). Against N4.9 (the rule's own lengths, re-run on this window): 4 of 4 and 4 of 4.

The catch: on the live decisions only (2021-11-01 → 2026-10-22) return beats the 90th percentile in 4 of 4 books against N4.10; the decisions before 2021-11 are a backfill by the production code; the rule was chosen after seeing 2014-2026 results; the bounds of N4.10 were chosen after seeing T03's draw 445; and the weeks added since the freeze of T03 (2026-08-24 → 2026-10-22) hold one switch, so every result here is in-sample and PROVISIONAL.

What to be careful about

  • The null is "same number of switches and same weeks in the market, placed at random". A pass says the rule's timing is better than random placement, not that it is better than holding the parent. It was out of the market in about half the weeks, so compare with the constant mix below.
  • Nothing is out of sample. The rule was chosen after seeing 2014-2026. Decisions before 2021-11 are the production code run backwards. On the live decisions only, return is 4 of 4 at the 90th percentile against N4.10.
  • 9 round trips per book is all the evidence there is. The smallest return edge detectable at 80% power against N4.10 is 15 to 19 pp a year.
  • The books are one market. SCMO_0007's return percentile against N4.10 is 94.1.

7. How one N4.10 draw is made. It keeps the 9 in-spells and 10 out-spells, the first state (out) and the totals (201 weeks in, 208 out), and draws every length as an integer from 2 to 46 (in) or 2 to 40 (out; 2 weeks = 14 days; the bounds are the rule's longest spells), every allowed set of lengths equally likely. With c(m, t) the number of ways to write t weeks as m lengths in the bounds, c(0, 0) = 1 and c(m, t) = Σ over x of c(m − 1, t − x), the parts are drawn one at a time: with m parts and t weeks left, P(length = x) = c(m − 1, t − x) ÷ c(m, t). The uniform number u is rng.random() and the length is the first x whose cumulative weight exceeds u × c(m, t). In-spell parts come first, then out-spell parts, from the same generator.

allowed in-tuples c(9, 201) = 7,107,131,351,925 (count_table.json: 7,107,131,351,925) allowed out-tuples c(10, 208) = 89,734,761,224,680 (count_table.json: 89,734,761,224,680)

Definitions

  • Percentile: (draws worse + 0.5 × draws tied) ÷ draws × 100, ties equal within a relative 1e-12. p-value: (1 + draws at least as good) ÷ (draws + 1).
  • CAGR: NAV_end^(252 ÷ (sessions − 1)) − 1, daily NAV, after cost. Worst fall (maximum drawdown): the most negative NAV ÷ running peak − 1.
  • N4.9 spell shuffle: the rule's in-spells reordered among themselves and its out-spells among themselves; same weeks invested, same number of switches, same spell lengths. Seed default_rng([3, id]).
  • N4.10 free-length random timing: the same number of in-spells and out-spells and the same totals of weeks in and out, every length an integer from 2 to 46 (in) or 40 (out), uniform over all allowed ordered tuples, drawn exactly by a dynamic programme, no rejection. Seed default_rng([6, id]). Both nulls start in the rule's first state.
  • Weeks versus sessions: both nulls hold invested weeks exactly; a week has 3 to 5 sessions, so invested sessions differ by draw and are reported.
  • MDE: 2.8 × the SD of the draws' CAGR (or maximum drawdown). Live window: 2021-11-01 → 2026-10-22.
