## Readings chosen (most literal reading, where the plan was silent)
- **Gates in analyse.py are re-derived from the evidence files** (V3 from `draw_states.npz` and `draws.csv`; V4 decisions and NAVs against T03's evidence, V5(b) and (c) from `count_table.json` and the states), and the engine's own flags (`audit.json` v3 block, `regression_t03.json`, `sampler_selftest.json`: every boolean `pass`) must also be true. V5(a), the chi-square, can only be read from `sampler_selftest.json` (key `p` > 0.001).
- **V4(b) compares strategy, hold-the-parent and 100%-sleeve NAVs only.** The constant mix is left out: its average share f is 201/409 on the new series against T03's 199/400, so it cannot equal T03's. (The engine's `regression_t03.json` uses T03's f for its own constant-mix rows.)
- **V5(c) is read per spell position:** within a draw the mean length is exactly S/k, so the test is the mean of the j-th spell over the draws against S/k, |z| < 4 for every position (worst |z| on smoke 2.21).
- **Bounds** HI_in and HI_out are the maximum over the four books of the rule's longest spell on the full window (46, 40), applied to every book and window.
- **Live window rule:** `decisions.csv` holds the full window only, so the live window's spell counts and totals come from `count_table.json`, its first state and sorted lengths from the N4.9 live draws (which hold them), and its round trips from those.
- **Row index of `draw_states.npz` = draw_id** (checked against the `in_lengths` and `out_lengths` strings of every draws.csv row).
- **Sessions invested of the rule** = sessions between a week's trade date and the next week's, summed over IN weeks (T03's report convention); verified equal to the engine's column.
- **Outcome map:** every row that applies is printed; "C1 and C2 PASS under N4.10 but N4.9 does not pass" (not a row of the map) gets its own line.
- **Best draw** for the regime chart is the highest-CAGR N4.10 draw of SCMO_0003 at 20.8 bps, full window, LBES; its states are read from `draw_states.npz`, its curve from `draw_curves_20.8.npz`.
- `analysis.log` blocks are 4.0a, 4.0b, 4.1 to 4.5 (4.5 is R1 to R4); `fill_writeup.py` refuses to write if any block is missing.
- `count_table.json` keys are read as the smoke file writes them (`weeks_S`, `spells_k`, nested under `tables`), and also as `k`, `S` anywhere in the file.
- The LIQUIDCASE book is built with `cash="liq"` (T03's name; it works with the real engine), falling back to `"liquidcase"`.
- The As-of date of the run is read from the `inputs.csv` header comment if there is one, else `audit.json` `data.approved_data_as_of` (the smoke file has the latter only).
