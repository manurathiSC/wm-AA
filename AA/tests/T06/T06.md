**Not rerun.** The circular-shift null of T03; its result stands for T03's window only.

**Statistic the null is judged on.** After-cost CAGR for H1, higher better. Maximum drawdown on daily NAV for H2, shallower better.
- **Unit:** one realisation is book × cost × null. The percentile is computed per realisation against its own draws.
- **Percentile:** (draws worse + 0.5 × draws tied) ÷ draws × 100. Ties are equal within a relative 1e-12.
- **p-value:** (1 + draws at least as good) ÷ (draws + 1). It is reported beside the percentile; the pass mark is on the percentile.
- **Secondary statistics**, each with its own percentile against the same draws: volatility of daily returns × √252 (lower better); Ulcer index (lower better); days at least 10% below the running peak (fewer better); longest time under water (shorter better); Calmar = CAGR ÷ |maximum drawdown|. Total return is rank-equivalent to CAGR within a realisation and is not reported separately.

**Pass mark and draws.** The percentile must be at least 90. 2,000 draws give a resolution of 0.05 points and a smallest p-value of 1/2001; (D + 1) ≥ m ÷ α is met for the two primary criteria (m = 2, α = 0.10).

**Construction, in order.** Steps 1 to 10 are T03 §3 (steps 1–10) unchanged except where stated:

1. **Calendar.** The sessions are D17's dates, per book, from its first date T0 (2019-01-02; 0007 2019-01-04) to **2026-10-22**. The sessions D18 has and D17 lacks are never read (F-AA-D15).
2. **Decision dates.** T0, then the last D17 session of each ISO week after T0. T0 is the window's base. The decision at the window's last session (2026-10-22, a Thursday) is made but not traded.
3. **Signal at decision date d.** Read the latest Master row of each of the three indices dated on or before d. The decision is IN if SIGN is BUY on all three, else OUT. A week whose Master row is dated after its last D17 session uses the previous week's row, one week stale and never ahead of d. Rows before 2021-11 are a backfill by the production code.
4. **Targets.** IN: 100% parent. OUT: 50% GOLDBEES, 50% cash.
5. **Initial position.** NAV is 1.000 at T0's close; the book holds at that close what the decision at T0 says. No cost is charged for this initial position, in any realisation including the nulls.
6. **Execution.** A trade happens only when the decision changes the state, on the **next D17 session after d**, at that session's OHLC4 of each instrument (stocks: Eikon bar with the Polyplex correction; if the bar lacks an open, high or low the close is used; GOLDBEES: the de-spiked OHLC4, high and low clipped to MCX gold's range that day, scaled, plus 0.5%, and the raw OHLC4 after the MCX file ends; cash: the level on that session, as-of). Exit: sell the basket, buy GOLDBEES and cash so that each is half of the proceeds after cost. Entry: sell the sleeve, buy the basket in force at its listed weights. Cost: ow = cost_bps ÷ 2 ÷ 10,000 per side on every value traded (stock, GOLDBEES, cash).
7. **Parent rebalances.** A basket takes effect on its `availableDate`. While IN, at that session's OHLC4 the old basket is sold and the new one bought, with cost on the value traded. While OUT, no trade happens but the current basket is recorded. The last approved basket is in force to the window's end (2026-10-22); the next rebalance is expected about mid-December 2026 and is not in the data.
8. **NAV.** Cash account + Σ stock units × close + GOLDBEES units × close + cash units × level. It is 1.000 at T0's close. No sale at the end.
9. **Statistics.** Sessions run from T0 to the last session. years = (sessions − 1) ÷ 252. CAGR = NAV_end^(1/years) − 1. Maximum drawdown, Ulcer index, days at least 10% below the running peak, longest time under water (sessions), volatility = SD of daily returns × √252, Calmar = CAGR ÷ |maximum drawdown|.
10. **Comparators** (benchmarks, not nulls), on the same calendar, cost and T0: hold the parent (the engine with the decision fixed at IN, at 0 cost and at each cost, plus the published NAV rebased to 1.000); the constant mix (the strategy's average equity share f = invested weeks ÷ weeks, held as f in the parent and (1 − f)/2 each in GOLDBEES and cash, rebalanced at each decision date's next session at OHLC4 with the same cost, including after T0's decision); the 100% sleeve (50% GOLDBEES, 50% cash, held throughout).
11. **Null draws.** From the decision series s_0 … s_n (n = 408; SCMO_0007 407), cut maximal spells: 9 in-spells and 10 out-spells.
    - **N4.9 spell shuffle** (T03's function unchanged): shuffle the in-spells among themselves and the out-spells among themselves with `numpy.random.default_rng([3, draw_id])` (draw_id = 0 … 1,999), interleave starting in the rule's first state (OUT).
    - **N4.10 free-length draw.** Let k_in = 9, S_in = 201, HI_in = 46 and k_out = 10, S_out = 208 (SCMO_0007: 207), HI_out = 40, with the lower bound 2 for both. Let c(m, t) be the number of ways to write t as m integers in [lo, hi]: c(0, 0) = 1, c(0, t) = 0 for t > 0, c(m, t) = Σ_{x = lo..hi} c(m − 1, t − x). The parts are drawn one at a time: with m parts and t weeks left, P(length = x) ∝ c(m − 1, t − x) for x in [lo, hi]. This is exactly uniform over the c(k, S) ordered tuples, with no draw rejected. *Worked example (k = 3, S = 12, bounds [2, 6], 19 tuples):* the first part is 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 with probabilities 3, 4, 5, 4, 3 over 19, because c(2, 10) = 3, c(2, 9) = 4, c(2, 8) = 5, c(2, 7) = 4 and c(2, 6) = 3. The uniform number comes from `rng.random()` and a cumulative-sum search (never `Generator.choice`, so a draw is the same across numpy versions), from `numpy.random.default_rng([6, draw_id])`, in-spell lengths first and then out-spell lengths from the same generator. The two sequences are interleaved starting in the rule's first state. The tuples are exactly 7,107,131,351,925 (in) and 89,734,761,224,680 (out) for SCMO_0003; the engine prints the counts for every book and window. The bounds for the live window are the same constants; its totals are 124 (5 in-spells) and 137 (6 out-spells).
    - The same draw ids are used for every cost and for books whose series are identical (SCMO_0003, 0010, 0029); SCMO_0007 draws from its own totals with the same seeds.
    - Each draw runs through the same engine (steps 5 to 9) with the same cost. The draws' stock trades use the real baskets and real parent rebalances; only the in-or-out state differs. The states of every draw are saved (`draw_states.npz`), and `rerun_draw.py` reproduces any draw from its id.
12. **MDE.** For C1, the smallest timing edge detectable at 80% power and 5% two-sided is 2.8 × null_sd, where null_sd is the SD of after-cost CAGR across the draws of a realisation (per null). For C2 it is 2.8 × the SD of maximum drawdown. Both are printed beside every FAIL. **Disclosed:** the numbers did not exist when this section was written; the thresholds in §2 do not depend on them. T03's N4.9 values (null SD of CAGR 5.5 to 6.9 pp, MDE 15 to 19 pp) are the prior.

**How the test works, in plain words.**

- **The series.** Each parent's NAV is built every day from the stocks it holds. Every Friday, three index signals are read. If all three say BUY, the book holds the parent's stocks; otherwise half GOLDBEES and half cash. A switch is made on the next session at the (open + high + low + close) ÷ 4 of each instrument, paying 10.4 bps on every rupee traded. *[the real signals, trades and NAVs are printed in §4.0a]*
- **One decision.** On a Friday, look up the latest weekly row of the three Master files. If the three SIGN cells are all BUY, the target is the parent. The trade, if the target changed, is on the next session. *[printed in §4.0a]*
- **The result of one path.** Units of each stock, GOLDBEES and cash are fixed between trades. Each day the NAV is Σ units × close. The CAGR and drawdown come from that daily NAV. *[printed in §4.0a]*
- **One draw of the old null (N4.9).** The real path is 19 spells: 9 in and 10 out, with fixed lengths. A draw keeps the 19 lengths and shuffles which in-spell goes where and which out-spell goes where.
- **One draw of the new null (N4.10).** A draw keeps only the *number* of spells and the *total* weeks in and out. It picks nine new in-spell lengths between 2 and 46 weeks that add up to 201, and ten new out-spell lengths between 2 and 40 weeks that add up to 208, every allowed set being equally likely, and lays them out one after the other starting out. The book then holds the parent in those weeks, with the same stocks and costs. The first 20 weeks of a real, an N4.9 and an N4.10 series are printed in §4.0a.
- **The ranking.** The rule's CAGR is placed among the 2,000 draws' CAGRs: the percentile is the share of draws it beats (ties count half); the p-value is the chance of doing as well by luck. 90 or more is a pass.
- **The comparators.** Holding the parent is what the book would do with no rule. The constant mix holds the same average share in the parent all the time: it shows how much of any drawdown benefit is only less exposure. The 100% sleeve is the other end.

**Point-in-time rule.** A decision at d reads Master rows dated at or before d only (step 3). A trade is on a session after d. The engine asserts both for every decision, counts them, and writes the counts to `audit.json`. The Master rows before 2021 were produced afterwards by the production code and are not signals traded at the time; this is disclosed, not corrected.

**Execution and cost.** AGENT.md R1 (next-session OHLC4, price return, 10.4 bps a side, weights drift) with these specifics: cash trades pay the cost; the first position at T0 is free; no sale at the end; GOLDBEES uses the de-spiked fill (X02) where the MCX file covers the session.

**Effective sample size and power.** 9 round trips per book on the full window and 5 on the live window, identical across books; 409 weekly decisions. The independent unit for timing is the round trip. MDE: step 12, computed after the run from the draws.

**What the nulls control for, and what they do not.** Both control for the fraction of weeks in the parent and the number of switches. N4.9 also controls for every spell's length; N4.10 does not. Neither controls for which weeks the sleeve is held (not matched, in §2), so gold's 2024-26 rally lifts any draw that sits in the sleeve in those years; R2 and the comparators bound this. Neither controls for the stale-week and backfill issues below.
