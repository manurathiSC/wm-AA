T05: during a SELL regime, does a monthly stock filter pick better than a random monthly pick?

SCMO_0003 · 2019-01-02 → 2026-11-04 · five indicators, 2,000 random same-size selections each · after 20.8 bps · pre-registration sha256 90dc7c4dff77

Verdict. all five gates pass. 56 filter reviews in SELL (46 monthly only, 10 forced). Indicators passing the pre-registered selection test C1 (CAGR percentile ≥ 98): none; passing the protection test C2: none. Best-of-sweep null (R5): percentile 10.3, FAIL. Every indicator earns less than the full AA switch (28.4% a year after cost).

How to read this

Each week the three-index supertrend says BUY or SELL. On BUY the book holds the parent's whole basket. On SELL, at the first SELL week of a spell and then at each month-end, the book keeps the stocks that pass an indicator (close above a moving average, or a weekly SuperTrend on BUY) and puts the rest into half GOLDBEES and half cash; between those reviews the list of passers is frozen. The null keeps the same number of stocks at each review, picked at random with that review's own random numbers, and freezes them until the next review. If an indicator's CAGR sits in the top 2% of those random picks it passes; a pass counts as selection only if it also holds at 0 bps. Shaded bands on the curves are SELL weeks. Hover or touch any chart: the crosshair shows the x and y at the pointer and the tooltip lists every value drawn at that x.
