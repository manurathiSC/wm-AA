Does holding each stock only while its own market-cap index is BUY choose what to hold better than a random exclusion of the same weight, on 2014-09-01 → 2019-01-02?

2014-09-01 → 2019-01-02 (every book) · four real parents of the AA books · decisions every Friday, trades next session at OHLC4 · 10.4 bps a side · 2,000 draws per book, cost and null · every number is computed from evidence/headline/ · run on 2026-11-17 data

The answer

No: T07's rejection holds on 2014-19 too. Holding each stock a parent holds only while the index of its size band is BUY (Large: Nifty 100, Mid: Midcap 100, Small and below: Smallcap 100), and sending the weight of the rest to a 50/50 GOLDBEES and cash sleeve, against dropping a random set of the same weight at the same events (N4.6, decides), on 2014-09-01 → 2019-01-02, after 20.8 bps. C1 (return beats the 90th percentile of the null): 0 of 4 books; C2 (worst fall): 1 of 4; C3 (rule minus the whole-book switch above zero): 1 of 4 (pre-registered pass: 3 of 4 each). N4.8 (label permutation, beside it): C1 0 of 4, C2 1 of 4.

The catch: the three signals disagree in only 57 of 228 weeks, in 11 stretches shared by all four books, so the rule differs from the whole-book switch in only 11 independent places and "3 of 4" is a consistency check. On the R2 sub-window (2015-01-02 → 2019-01-02) C1 reaches the 90th percentile in 1 of 4 books and C3 is positive in 1 of 4. T12 alone cannot reverse T07, so any finding is PROVISIONAL at most.

7. One N4.6 draw. Definitions

  • Event: a trade session on which targets are rebuilt: T0; a session after a weekly decision on which the set of stocks the current tags would drop differs from the set currently dropped; the first session on or after a basket's availableDate. Tags are read at events only, as of the session before.
  • Tag: Large, Mid or Small and below, from the stock's market-cap rank on the latest month-end snapshot on or before the date (1 to 100, 101 to 250, above 250); a stock with no rank reads Small and below (unranked). Real index membership is used only from 2026-04-09, after this window.
  • Percentile: (draws worse + 0.5 × draws tied) ÷ draws × 100, ties equal within a relative 1e-12. p-value: (1 + draws at least as good) ÷ (draws + 1).
  • CAGR: NAV_end^(252 ÷ (sessions − 1)) − 1, daily NAV, after cost. Worst fall (maximum drawdown): the most negative NAV ÷ running peak − 1. Volatility: SD of daily returns × √252. Ulcer index: root mean square of the percentage drawdown. Calmar: CAGR ÷ |maximum drawdown|.
  • N4.6 size-matched random exclusion (decides): at every event a random subset of the held stocks whose total listed weight is within τ = half the smallest listed weight of the weight the rule drops, uniform over all such subsets, redrawn at every event. Seed default_rng([15, 7, draw_id, book_no]).
  • N4.8 persistent label permutation (beside it): one random score per ISIN per draw; at every event the held stocks are ranked by score and given the labels Large, Mid, Small and below in the rule's counts per band; the real signals act on the labels. Seed default_rng([15, 8, draw_id, book_no]).
  • Whole-book switch: every stock follows the single state IN iff all three signals are BUY, else 50/50 GOLDBEES and cash (T06's rule). C3: the rule's after-cost CAGR minus the whole-book switch's. MDE: 2.8 × the SD of the draws' CAGR (or maximum drawdown). R2: 2015-01-02 → 2019-01-02. Hold the parent: rebuilt from the parent's baskets and Eikon prices; no published NAV exists before 2019-01-02.
