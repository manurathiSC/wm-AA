## 1. Idea and references

**The idea in one sentence.** Tag each stock a parent holds as Large, Mid, or Small and below; hold it only while its band's weekly supertrend is BUY (Large: Nifty 100, Mid: Midcap 100, Small and below: Smallcap 100), and send the weight of the stocks whose band is SELL to a 50/50 GOLDBEES and cash sleeve; the question is whether this choice of *which stocks to drop* beats dropping a random set of the same weight at the same events, on 2014-09-01 → 2019-01-02.

So T07's rule can now be run on the 4.3 years before 2019.

**The decision this will change.** Whether T07's rejection of segment-level holding holds on an earlier stretch, or whether T07's result is specific to 2019-26. A pass here would not reverse T07: it would say the tag effect depends on the window.

- **Expectation, declared:** T07 failed on 2019-26 and Study 3 found no robust gain, so the likeliest outcome is that C1 fails in at least two books. SCMO_0003 holds over 80% Small and below, so its rule is close to the Smallcap 100 signal alone. No pass mark depends on this expectation.

**The rule** (specified in §3; one rule, no parameters): at each event, tag every stock in the basket in force as of the session before the event's trade session; keep it at its listed weight while the signal of its band is BUY; send the weight of the rest to 50% GOLDBEES and 50% cash.

**Hypotheses** (after cost at 20.8 bps round trip; one rule; judged per book):
- **H1, selection.** The stocks the rule drops are worse than a random set of the same weight dropped at the same events. Judged on CAGR against N4.6. Falsified if C1 fails.
- **H2, protection.** The rule's worst fall is shallower than that of those random exclusions. Judged on maximum drawdown against N4.6. Falsified if C2 fails.
- **H3, increment.** The rule earns more than the whole-book switch on the same prices, fills, costs and sleeve. Judged on the difference of after-cost CAGRs. Falsified if C3 fails.

**Null (primary): N4.6 size-matched random exclusion.**

| Dimension | Randomised / held / not matched | How | Measured in §4.0 as |
|---|---|---|---|
| which stocks are dropped at each event | **randomised** | a random subset of the held stocks, uniform over all subsets whose total listed weight is within τ of the weight the rule drops (τ = half the smallest listed weight in the basket); redrawn independently at every event | per event: weight dropped, drop count |
| the weight dropped | held | to within τ. With equal weights this is the same count. When the rule drops nothing or everything, the only allowed subset is the empty or the full one | dropped weight per event, null against rule (must lie within τ) |
| events: their dates, the three signals, the basket in force, the trade sessions | held | the null shares the rule's event list | event count (must be identical) |
| book, stocks, listed weights, parent rebalances, calendar, sleeve, cost, execution | held | constant for the run | — |
| whether a dropped stock stays dropped from one event to the next | **not held** | the null redraws at each event, so its turnover can differ from the rule's | trades and cost paid per draw against the rule's; the 0 bps percentile beside the 20.8 bps one |
| which band's signal a stock follows | **not used** | N4.6 does not read tags at all | — |

The rule's own drop set lies inside the allowed set at every event (its dropped weight is the target, so it differs by 0), so the rule is one allowed configuration. Gate V5 checks this.

**Null (companion, reported, not a criterion): N4.8 persistent label permutation.** One random score per ISIN per draw. At every event, rank the held stocks by their score and give the first n_L the label Large, the next n_M Mid and the rest Small and below, where n_L, n_M and n_S are the rule's counts per band at that event; the real signals then act on the labels. Held: the events, the counts per band, the signals. Randomised: which stock carries which label. The dropped weight is **not** matched exactly when weights are unequal; it is reported per event.

**Which null decides.** N4.6 decides T12's verdict. N4.8 is reported beside it. If they disagree, both are reported in the verdict and the finding's status note says so (AGENT.md N6.8).

**Statistic the nulls are judged on.** After-cost CAGR for H1, higher better. Maximum drawdown on daily NAV for H2, shallower better. For H3 the difference of after-cost CAGRs, rule minus the whole-book switch.
- **Unit:** one realisation is book × cost × null. The percentile is computed per realisation against its own draws.
- **Percentile:** (draws worse + 0.5 × draws tied) ÷ draws × 100. Ties are equal within a relative 1e-12. **p-value:** (1 + draws at least as good) ÷ (draws + 1). The pass mark is on the percentile.
- **Secondary statistics**, each with its own percentile against the same draws: volatility of daily returns × √252 (lower better); Ulcer index (lower better); days at least 10% below the running peak (fewer better); longest time under water (shorter better); Calmar = CAGR ÷ |maximum drawdown|. Trades and cost paid are reported per draw.

**Pass mark and draws.** The percentile must be at least 90. 2,000 draws give a resolution of 0.05 points and a smallest p-value of 1/2001; D + 1 ≥ m ÷ α is met for the two primary criteria (m = 2, α = 0.10).

**The tag rule** (`analysis/tag_lookup.py`, decided by Manu 2026-11-11, reviewed 2026-11-12 and, for the pre-2018 rows, 2026-11-17; unchanged). Every date of the window is before the cutoff (the first membership snapshot, 2026-04-09), so every tag is a **rank tag**: the band of the stock's rank on the latest `mcap_based_universe` snapshot on or before the date (month-end snapshots in this window): market cap descending among ordinary shares, ties by quote_id, rank 1 to 100 Large, 101 to 250 Mid, above 250 Small and below. No averaging. A stock is found by its ISIN or, through `symbology_changes` or the panel itself, by any other ISIN that shares its quote_id. A stock with no rank reads **Small and below**, source `unranked`, and is counted. Known: Hexaware (HEXT, INE093A01041) in SCMO_0007's 2018-02-28 basket and DHFL (PIRAMALFIN, INE202B01038) in SCMO_0010's 2017-06-01 and 2017-09-01 baskets; R6 reads them by their other listing.

**When tags are read.** Only at events (step 4), as of the event's information date (the D26 session before its trade session; T0's own date for T0), and never between events: a stock's band does not change by itself between events when a new month-end snapshot appears. A tag can therefore be up to about one month older than its information date. The 2015-02-28 snapshot (a Saturday Budget session that D26 does not list) is first read on the next information date on or after it.

**Caveats of the tag data, to be stated on the page** (from both reviews): (1) every market-cap row in the window was written on 2026-01-03, 8 to 12 years after its date, so a point-in-time vintage cannot be proven; the market caps checked by the reviewer look as of the date; (2) the rank rule is untested before 2026: its 92.07% three-band agreement with real membership was measured on eight 2026 snapshots, and ranks use full market cap while the indices use free float; (3) identifiers in the source are today's (HDFC Bank carries its 2019 ISIN in 2014); only market cap feeds the rank; (4) two parent holdings are unranked (above); (5) isolated data errors (Castrol missing on 2014-02-28, DHANUKA on 2016-02-29) change no held band; (6) the parents' band mix in 2014-19 differs a lot from 2019-26.

**Construction, in order.** Steps 1 to 3 follow T11 §3; steps 4 to 13 are T07 §3 steps 4 to 13, restated with T12's calendar.

1. **Calendar.** The sessions are D26's dates from **T0 = 2014-09-01** to **END = 2019-01-02** (1,070 sessions). The basket in force on 2014-07-01 (effective 2014-06-02) is not used. 2017-10-19 (the Muhurat evening) is a D26 session with no D18 bar for any stock: every stock's previous close is its valuation and fill price there (T11's stand-in), counted. 2016-10-30 (Muhurat) is a D26 session with no GOLDBEES bar: GOLDBEES's previous close stands in, counted. A session with a GOLDBEES bar and no MCX range uses the raw OHLC4, counted.
2. **Decision dates.** T0, then the last D26 session of each ISO week after T0 (228 decisions). The decision at END is made but not traded.
3. **Signals at decision date d.** Read the latest Master row of each of the three indices dated on or before d. A band is *on* if the SIGN of its index is BUY: Large reads Nifty 100, Mid reads Midcap 100, Small and below reads Smallcap 100. The *state* of d is the triple of the three SIGN cells.
4. **Events.** An event is a trade session on which targets are rebuilt: (i) **T0**, the initial position, free of cost: the basket effective on T0 (availableDate 2014-09-01) at its listed weights renormalised to 1, bought at T0's OHLC4 and scaled so NAV = 1.000 at T0's close (T11's convention), with the value of the stocks dropped at T0 (tags and state read as of T0 itself) held half in GOLDBEES and half in cash units; (ii) a **signal event**: the next D26 session after a decision date d on which the set of stocks the current tags would drop under d's state differs from the set the book currently drops. "Current tags" are those read at the last event; tags are re-read only at events. When all three bands are tied (the whole-book switch) this reduces to T06's rule that a trade happens only when the stock share changes; (iii) a **basket event**: the first D26 session on or after a non-skipped basket's availableDate, unless the book holds no stock on that session (then nothing is traded, as in T06, and the new basket's tags, read as of the session before, become the current tags for the next signal test; T07's reading). Two events on one session are one event. Between events no target changes and the book drifts. The information date of an event on session s is the D26 session immediately before s; the state (the triple of the latest decision made before s) and the tags of that event are read as of it.
5. **Tags at an event.** For every stock the basket in force holds (listed weight > 0), `tag_lookup.tag(ISIN, information date)` gives its band. A stock is **kept** if its band is on, **dropped** otherwise.
6. **Targets (T06's convention, no cash account).** Listed weights are renormalised so each basket sums to 1 (as T06 and T07; T07's amendment 2). Let V be the book's value at session s's OHLC4 before cost. Each kept stock's target value is V × w_i. GOLDBEES and cash units each get V × s ÷ 2, where s = D is the weight of the dropped stocks. All kept: s = 0 (T06's IN). All dropped: s = 1 (T06's OUT: V ÷ 2 in each of GOLDBEES and cash). Every new position is then scaled by k = (V − cost) ÷ V.
7. **Execution.** At an event, every instrument is moved from its current value to its target on session s at that session's OHLC4 (stocks: D18 bar with the Polyplex correction, the close if the bar lacks an open, high or low; GOLDBEES: OHLC4 with open, high and low clipped to MCX gold's range that day (D24b daily file) plus 0.5% of the close, the raw OHLC4 where there is no range; cash: the level on that session, as-of). Cost: cost_bps ÷ 2 ÷ 10,000 per side on the sum of the absolute differences between current and target values over every stock, GOLDBEES and cash. Kept stocks are re-set to their listed weights at every event. No trade between events.
8. **NAV.** Σ stock units × close + GOLDBEES units × close + cash units × level. 1.000 at T0's close. No sale at the end.
9. **Statistics.** As T06 §3 step 9 (years = (sessions − 1) ÷ 252; CAGR; maximum drawdown; Ulcer index; days at least 10% below the running peak; longest time under water; volatility × √252; Calmar).
10. **Comparators** (benchmarks, not nulls), on the same calendar, cost and T0: the **whole-book switch** (every stock follows the single state IN iff all three signals are BUY, else 100% sleeve; T06's rule, the engine with the three bands tied; T11's Part A on this window); hold the parent (the engine with every stock held, at 0 cost and each cost; rebuilt, no published NAV); the constant mix (the rule's average share of NAV in stocks, held as that share in the parent and half the rest in each of GOLDBEES and cash, rebalanced at each event's session at OHLC4 with the same cost); the 100% sleeve.
11. **Null draws.** From the rule's event list (dates, states, basket in force, held stocks and listed weights), per event:
    - **N4.6.** The held stocks are indexed 0 … n − 1 (n ≤ 16) in ISIN order (Python string order) and w_i is their renormalised weights. D = the weight the rule drops, τ = ½ min_i w_i. The allowed set A is every subset X with |Σ_{i∈X} w_i − D| ≤ τ (ties decided with a 1e-12 tolerance), listed in increasing order of its bitmask (bit i = stock i). The dropped subset is A[⌊u·|A|⌋] with u = `rng.random()` from `numpy.random.default_rng([15, 7, draw_id, book_no])`, one uniform per event in event order (book_no = 0 … 3 for SCMO_0003, 0007, 0010, 0029). When the rule drops nothing, A holds only the empty set, and when it drops everything A holds only the full set.
    - **N4.8.** One score per ISIN the book ever holds in the window, in ISIN order, from `numpy.random.default_rng([15, 8, draw_id, book_no])`; at each event assign labels as in §2 (ranking by descending score) and drop the stocks whose label's band is off.
    - The same draw ids for every cost. Each draw runs through the same engine (steps 5 to 9); only which stocks are dropped differs. The dropped set of every draw at every event is saved (`draw_drops.npz`, `draw_labels.npz`) and `rerun_draw.py` reproduces any draw from its id.
12. **Controls.** V6: the shifts k are drawn with `default_rng([15, 9, 100000, book_no])`, and shift i's null uses `default_rng([15, 9, i, book_no])`. V7 (oracle) is deterministic. R5: `default_rng([15, 5, draw_id, book_no])`, two uniforms per rank-sourced stock per tag read. R2 uses its own draws with the same seeds.
13. **MDE.** For C1, 2.8 × null_sd, the SD of after-cost CAGR across the draws of a realisation (per null); for C2, 2.8 × the SD of maximum drawdown. Printed beside every FAIL. With baskets of 3 to 16 stocks and 11 stretches the null's spread is limited by the number of allowed subsets, which the engine prints per event.

**How the test works, in plain words.**

- **The series.** Each parent's NAV is built every day from the stocks it held between September 2014 and January 2019. Every Friday, three index signals are read. Each stock is tagged by size (Large, Mid, or Small and below) from its market-cap rank at the latest month end, and follows the signal of its size's index. A stock whose index is BUY stays; a stock whose index is SELL is sold, and its weight goes half to GOLDBEES and half to cash. Trades happen at the next session's (open + high + low + close) ÷ 4, paying 10.4 bps on every rupee traded. *[the real signals, tags, trades and NAVs are printed in §4.0a]*
- **One event.** On a Friday where a signal flipped (or on the day a new basket starts), look up every held stock's size tag as of the session before the trade, find its index's SIGN, and set the targets as in step 6. Tags are looked up only on these days. *[printed in §4.0a, for one real event]*
- **The result of one path.** Units of each stock, GOLDBEES and cash are fixed between events. Each day the NAV is Σ units × close. The CAGR and drawdown come from that daily NAV.
- **One draw of the main null (N4.6).** Keep the same events and the same weight to drop. Instead of dropping the stocks whose index is SELL, drop a random set of stocks of about the same total weight. Everything else is identical. *[one draw is printed in §4.0a]*
- **One draw of the companion null (N4.8).** Keep the number of Large, Mid and Small stocks, but hand the size labels to stocks at random (the same random order all the way through the draw), so the real signals act on the wrong stocks.
- **The ranking.** The rule's CAGR is placed among the 2,000 draws' CAGRs: the percentile is the share of draws it beats (ties count half); the p-value is the chance of doing as well by luck. 90 or more is a pass. The rule is also compared with the whole-book switch directly (C3).
- **The comparators.** The whole-book switch sells the whole parent unless all three signals are BUY. The constant mix holds the same average share in stocks all the time: it shows how much of any gain is only less exposure.

**Point-in-time rule.** A decision at d reads Master rows dated at or before d only (step 3). A tag is read as of the information date of its event and uses the latest snapshot on or before that date (`tag_lookup.py` never uses a later snapshot; its self-test checks this). A trade is on a session after d. The engine asserts all three for every event, counts them, and writes the counts to `audit.json`. The Master rows were produced afterwards by the production code; the market-cap history was back-filled; both are disclosed, not corrected.

**Execution and cost.** AGENT.md R1 (next-session OHLC4, price return, 10.4 bps a side, weights drift) with the specifics of steps 6 and 7: cash trades pay the cost; the first position at T0 is free; no sale at the end.

**Effective sample size and power.** 228 weekly decisions; 57 disagreement weeks in 11 stretches (R2: 47 in 10); up to 17 events in disagreement weeks. The independent unit for the segment effect is the stretch. MDE: step 13, computed after the run.

**What the nulls control for, and what they do not.** N4.6 controls for the events, the weight dropped and the exposure; it does not control for how long a stock stays dropped (turnover), which R1 and R4 report. N4.8 controls for the number of stocks per label and the persistence of a label within a draw; it does not match the dropped weight exactly. Neither controls for gold's moves in the sleeve; the whole-book switch holds the same sleeve, so C3 is not affected, while the constant mix and R3 bound it. Neither controls for the tag data's limits.
