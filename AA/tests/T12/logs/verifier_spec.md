Plus the engine's evidence in `tests/T12/evidence/<run>/`.

1. **C-data.** Through-dates must cover END = 2019-01-02 (D19 and D05 exempt as before; D12 is not read). D23b's rows must be in `inputs.csv`.
2. **C-dec.** Calendar = D26 from T0 = 2014-09-01 to END = 2019-01-02 for every book (228 decisions); r2 window from the first D26 session on or after 2015-01-02.
3. **C-tag.** Your own rank lookup on the D23b panel: latest snapshot on or before the information date; the stock found by ISIN, else through another ISIN that shares its quote_id (from D22 `isin`/`new_isin` and from the panel's own isin/quote_id pairs); no rank → `Small and below`, source `unranked`. Every tag in the window is a rank tag (assert the membership cutoff is after END). You must find exactly the unranked holdings at events that the engine lists; expected from Step 1: Hexaware (INE093A01041) in SCMO_0007 around the 2018-02-28 basket, DHFL (INE202B01038) in SCMO_0010 in the 2017-06-01 and 2017-09-01 baskets. For `full_r6`, re-tag those by the given quote_ids (Hexaware 236783369047, DHFL 236798428431) and compare `r6_links.csv`.
4. **C-events / C-target / C-nav.** As T07, with T12's initial position: the basket effective 2014-09-01 (renormalised) bought at T0's OHLC4 and scaled to NAV 1.000 at T0's close, the dropped weight (tags and state as of T0) half GOLDBEES and half cash at T0's close; the stand-ins of the contract point 14; the GOLDBEES fill from the D24b daily range. Windows `full`, `r2`, `full_r6`.
5. **C-t06 is replaced by C-regress.** With your own simulator on the D17 calendar of 2019 (T0 = each book's first D17 date, end 2019-12-31, inputs cut at 2019-12-31): the rule equals `tests/T07/evidence/headline/daily_nav.csv` (run `segment`, each cost present) and the whole-book mode equals `tests/T06/evidence/headline/daily_nav.csv` on every session of that slice, relative 1e-12. If `tests/T11/evidence/headline/` exists, your whole-book and always-in runs on T12's window equal T11's Part A strategy and hold-the-parent NAVs (relative 1e-9); else report "not run". Compare with `regression.json`.
6. **C-always-in.** No G0 (no published NAV before 2019). Your always-in run at 0 cost equals the engine's `parent` run in `daily_nav.csv` (relative 1e-9).
7. **C-pit** adds: no tag snapshot dated after its information date; no Smallcap 100 Master row before 2011-11-25.
8. **C-r7.** Re-derive `r7_flagged_days.csv` from D18 (|close / previous close − 1| ≥ 0.20 on held stock-days) and compare.
9. **C-corrupt** as T07 (flip a tag, change a target, move a drop out of its allowed set, scale a NAV, delete an event) plus: change one `r6_links.csv` rank.
