## What each script does
- **analyse.py** (`--run` / `--evidence DIR --out DIR`, `--books`, `--t06`, `--t08`, `--doc`): reads only evidence (T06's and T08's stored draws only as the historical reference, omitted with a note if the files are absent). Prints the blocks `fill_writeup.py` expects (4.0a, 4.0b, 4.1-4.5). Six criteria: A1/A2 (the `strategy_fixed50` rule against F1 and F2 draws run with `sleeve_mode=fixed50`), C1/C2 (timed rule against F1/F2 timed), C3/C4 (against F3/F4); Holm over m = 6 per book and cost; MDE per null (F1-F5 and the fixed50 F1/F2); reportability per criterion (in-or-out round trips and feasibility for all, gold spells with an OUT week for C3/C4, full and R3 windows); the outcome-map rows that apply (all that match are printed; the map is not exclusive); R1 (all six at 0 and 50 bps, deciding and beside), R2 (reported as the null result it is, with the largest CAGR difference LIQUIDCASE vs LBES over the rule and every draw), R3 (A1, C1, C3 on the 2012-01-02 window), R4 (both in-or-out pairs and the gold pair, spreads; T06/T08 percentiles as reference if their files exist), R5, R6, R7 (stats.csv kinds, plus how many of the rule's sleeve trades fill at the proxy), per-year returns, comparators (hold-the-proxy, the 50/50 rule, both constant mixes, both 100% sleeves, three single indices), hypotheses line. Gates re-derived from the evidence: V1 from `verify_t09.json`; V2 (Master labels after decision, trade dates, Smallcap 100 signal first label date, `usable_from` of inputs, audit block); V3 (every saved draw of both windows against the real series with per-state bounds, every draws.csv row, length strings, sessions invested, audit flags); V4 (the engine's `regression.json` flags, plus NAV start, one-index books across costs, end NAV vs stats, NAV on every trade date vs `nav_close_after`, the four books' identical decisions, hold-the-proxy resets at all quarter starts); V5 (inclusion-exclusion for every count-table entry with per-state bounds, both chi-square p-values from `sampler_selftest.json`, mean-length z-scores); V6 (sleeve-trade identities at all costs, numbers and dates of exits, entries and rebalances against the decisions, the proxy-period flag, the reset rows: dates against the calendar and the trades, cost, sums, weights, traded). The line `Gates: {...} -> ALL PASS` is what `report.py` and `check_page.py` parse. Exits non-zero if a gate fails.
- **fill_writeup.py**: T08's script with T09 headings (CRLF kept). Writes only between the `§4 BEGIN/END` markers; proves before writing that the §1-§3 hash (computed as `check_prereg.py` does), the bytes in front of the §4 heading, the text between the END marker and `## 5.` (the amendments block) and §5-§8 are unchanged. Reading of the brief: "writes only between the markers and the §5-§8 skeleton text" is taken as "leaves the §5-§8 skeleton text as it is" (as T08's does); §5-§8 are written by hand in Step 6.
- **report.py**: T08's page with: three-index proxy language, ten curves per book (the comparators and the three single indices; no published NAV), the worked example with an exit, a gold flip, an entry and a quarterly reset (values, units before and after from `proxy_units.csv`, implied fill prices), the amendments of `T09.md` copied onto the page, six-criterion tables with Holm columns, three histogram pairs per book (A: 50/50 sleeve, C1/C2, C3/C4), per-state bounds on the spell-length and sampler charts, the regime chart (best timed F1 draw against the rule; proxy and 50/50 sleeve in the stretch table), R1-R7, two power tables, reportability table. Every histogram tooltip and spell-length tooltip names the window dates; every stretch tip begins with its start and end date. Imports nothing from the engine or the analysis. No performance figure is typed in the file.
- **check_page.py**: independent (own parse of `APPROVED_DATA.md`, own DP/inclusion-exclusion/samplers, own simulator for hold-the-proxy). Re-derives from the raw APPROVED files: the calendar, every decision (index and MAUc1, both windows), hold-the-proxy at 0 cost with the 29 quarterly resets and each single index from the D25 bars (the Smallcap 100 file read as text from the window start), the worked entry and reset re-priced at the D25 OHLC4; the inputs (APPROVED, through-date, sha256 prefix, hash of the file on disk, usable-from marker, evidence `inputs.csv` equal to the page); every number in `page-data`; the samplers (tuple counts, draw-0 replays, the first `--regen` draws of all four samplers from their seeds, the four books sharing draws); every draw of every null; every trade, sleeve-trade, reset and proxy-units row; the held/randomised tables and the amendments from `T09.md`; the captions (and that T08's parent captions are gone); verifier and gates; every hover array, histogram bin and SVG bar. Options `--expect-draws N`, `--books`, `--regen N`.
- **rerun_draw.py**: `--book --family {F1..F5} --id [--gold-id] --cost --window {full,r3} --cash {lbes,liq} --sleeve-mode {timed,fixed50} --run/--evidence`. Uses the engine's own functions (as T06's and T08's do).
- **run.sh**: `check_approved_data.py`, `check_prereg.py`, engine headline, `verify_t09.py`, `analyse.py`, `fill_writeup.py`, `check_prereg.py`, `report.py`, `check_page.py`; `set -euo pipefail`, so each non-zero exit stops it.

## Readings and decisions (where the text is silent)
1. **Outcome map.** The patterns of `T09.md` §2 overlap (for example "A1 or A2 FAIL, C1 and C2 PASS" and "C1 or C2 FAIL, C3 and C4 PASS" can both hold with different pass sets); `analyse.py` prints every row that applies and says the text does not rank them. If no row matches it says so.
2. **Reportability** is per criterion: in-or-out round trips (>= 4) and table feasibility for all six, gold spells with an OUT week (>= 4) additionally for C3 and C4. A book that is not reportable is excluded from that criterion's pass count and flagged.
3. **A1/A2 rule** is `strategy_fixed50` and A1/A2 draws are the `fixed50` F1/F2 rows at the same cost; R1 uses them at 0 and 50 bps (the engine writes them at all three).
4. **Holm** is over the six deciding p-values of a book and cost in the order A1, A2, C1, C2, C3, C4.
5. **R2** is reported for A1, C1 and C3 and labelled a null result by construction; the page and the log print the maximum CAGR difference between LIQ and LBES, not a typed figure. **R3** is A1, C1, C3 on the R3 window. **R7** is read from `stats.csv` (the engine writes no R7 curves),.
6. **Historical reference** (R4): T06's and T08's stored draws are re-ranked against their own rules and shown as reference with no pass mark; omitted with a one-line note if the evidence files do not exist.
7. **Page text** quotes no performance figure of T09 or of earlier tests. The only historical numbers on the page come from the evidence files through the scripts; the amendments block repeats the text of `T09.md` (it contains the LIQUIDCASE-LBES difference as written there).
8. **Per-year returns** (analyse.py) use calendar years of the strategy NAV file, the first and last years partial.
9. **A synthetic directory "from the contract"** was produced by running the engine on four books with 40 draws rather than writing a mock generator; the engine's smoke and 4-book output follow the contract and the verifier's mock sets (Step 3) cover the contract-only path. If the headline evidence deviates from the contract in a way the engine and the smoke run do not, `analyse.py` or `check_page.py` will fail loudly rather than pass.
