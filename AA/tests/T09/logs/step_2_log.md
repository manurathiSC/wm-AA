## What was built
`AA/analysis/t09_engine.py` (about 1,250 lines), a fork of `t08_engine.py` (copied, not imported; it does not import ST.py or any T06/T07/T08 module). Reads files only through `approved_data.ap()`; writes to `tests/T09/evidence/<run>/` per `logs/evidence_contract.md`. CLI: `python analysis/t09_engine.py --run <name> [--draws N] [--quick] [--books SCMO_0003 ...]` (`--quick` = first book only, 20 draws unless `--draws` is given). T0 is one constant (`T0 = 2011-11-25`); the spells, bounds and counts it asserts come from `tests/T09/logs/step_1_spell_counts.json` (the engine stops if that file is for another window). Nothing was run on the 2011-11-18 window: before Manu's change the only work was reading the engine and data-availability checks (sessions without a gold bar, LBES against LIQUIDCASE returns), no NAV and no smoke run.

Changes against T08:
- **Portfolio.** `Book` holds three index instruments (D25: Nifty 100, Midcap 100, Smallcap 100) at the book's typed weights; `trade()` and `simulate()` keep T08's arithmetic (checked: the five sleeve lines of `trade` are present verbatim in both files) with `Te = e·V·w` over three lines. - **Data.** D26 calendar, D25 prices (Smallcap 100 read as text from T0), the three D03 Masters (Smallcap 100 rows sliced to >= T0, `guard_window` called), D24b (MAUc1 Master, weekly csv, daily range), GOLDBEES chain D09b → D09 (fill: proxy OHLC4 before 2014-07-01, T03's de-spike on D24b's range after), cash D11. No stock, basket, published-NAV or D12 file is opened. 14 inputs on the smoke run.
- **Samplers.** T08's DP, inclusion-exclusion and sequential sampler are unchanged; `spell_shuffle`, `free_length_draw`, `v3_check`, `make_states_*` take per-state bounds (`lo = min(2, shortest)`, `hi = longest`, from the series; asserted equal to the Step 1 JSON), seeds with first element 12; the states are generated once and shared by the four books.
- **Run plan** (`run_book`): full/lbes F1–F4 at 0/20.8/50 timed, F5 at 20.8, F1 and F2 fixed50 at all three costs; full/liq and r3/lbes F1, F3, F1-fixed50 at 20.8; the headline cell also runs the strategy, `strategy_fixed50`, `hold_proxy`, both constant mixes, both 100% sleeves, the three single indices at all costs and R7a/R7b at 20.8; every cell carries `strategy_fixed50`.
- **Gates.** V2 (decision labels, trades after decisions, Smallcap 100 rule-7 checks), V3 (every draw, per-state bounds), V4 (a) one-index book, (b) hold-the-proxy against a plain-loop hand build, (c) AST comparison with `t08_engine.py`, (d) signals against the Master/weekly files at every decision, (e) the D09b → D09 splice; V5 (small case with T06/T08's seed and, beside it, seed [12, 6, ...]; DP = inclusion–exclusion; per-position means); V6 (sleeve and reset identities, expected sleeve trades and resets recomputed from the states, constant-g re-runs, worked examples). Exit code 0 only if all gates, the approved-data/guard self-test and the Step 1 counts pass.

## Readings and decisions (where the frozen text or contract is silent) — all in `audit.json['readings']` (E1–E21)
- **E7 (frozen text wrong, reported):** `T09.md` §3 step 6 says the post-cost targets "solve the fixed point value' = value − ow × traded exactly (... the engine's `trade()` solves them)". T06's and T08's `trade()` does not solve a fixed point: cost = ow × traded measured at the pre-cost targets, then every target is scaled by (V − cost)/V. The engine does exactly that (it is what V6 checks: cost = traded × ow, post-trade values = targets × (V − cost)). Not changed in the frozen text.
- **E6:** a reset trades iff the book holds the proxy at the start of the session and no decision trade falls on it; a reset on END (2019-01-02, which opens a quarter) is traded (only decisions at END are not).
- **E9:** R2 'liq' = liquidEtfFinal as-of over the whole window; its daily returns equal LBES's to 1.6e-10 in this window, so R2 is vacuous here (T08's splice at 2024-01-24 was for the later years).
- **E8/E10-type gold readings:** 3 sessions in the D09 period have no MCX bar (2014-10-23, 2015-01-01, 2015-11-11): the GOLDBEES fill is the raw OHLC4; 2016-10-30 (an NSE special session) has no gold bar in D09 or D09b: the previous close and fill are used. The 2014-07-01 → 2015-01-30 sessions are covered by D24b's range (D12 was not needed).
- **E13 (R7b):** the gold weight is 0.5 for decisions dated before 2014-07-01; the first later decision that sees a different weight trades the sleeve if OUT.
- **E14:** F1 and F2 fixed50 draws at all three costs; the r3 and liq cells also run `strategy_fixed50` and F1 fixed50.
- **E21 / V5(a):** the self-test seed is T06/T08's [6, 123456789] (same sampler code; p = 0.83 as in T08); with seed [12, 6, 123456789] the p-value was 0.0031 in the first smoke run, above 0.001 and ordinary for one test; both are reported and both must exceed 0.001.
- **Step 1 "15 while IN":** the Step 1 count of flips "while IN" (15) counts every flip at a decision whose state is IN, which includes one flip on an entry decision (the entry trade happens; no sleeve trade); the engine's `flip_counts` separates them (14 both sides + 1 on an entry decision) and the check adds them.
- **Frozen text that is inaccurate or infeasible (listed, not edited):** (1) the fixed-point sentence above; (2) §3 data paragraph and the contaminants table say D26 omits Saturday special sessions "that the index files contain": the D25 files copied from production contain none (0 rows dropped in the window; the 7 Saturday sessions are only in Manu's `INDTradingDays.xlsx`); harmless; (3) §3 step 1 says the engine asserts a GOLDBEES bar on every session: one session (2016-10-30) has none of its own, so the engine uses the previous close and fill and counts it instead of stopping; (4) §2's "15 gold flips while IN (no trade)" includes the entry-decision flip (see above); (5) R2 is vacuous in this window (E9).
