## 2. Conventions (T09.md §3 steps 1–10 and the amendment block)
- **Decision dates:** T0, then the last session of each ISO week after T0 (D26 sessions); the decision at END is made, not traded. `s_j` = 1 iff the latest Master row dated on or before the decision date is BUY in all three indices (Smallcap 100 rows from 2011-11-25 only); `g_j` = 1 iff the latest MAUc1 row on or before the date is BUY; `gw_j` = 0.8 if `g_j` = 1, else 0.2 (`fixed50`: 0.5).
- **Book weights** (Nifty 100, Midcap 100, Smallcap 100), typed in T09.md §3: SCMO_0003 (0.09626177, 0.19679459, 0.70694364); SCMO_0007 (0.04799800, 0.17801859, 0.77398341); SCMO_0010 (0.26590767, 0.21230665, 0.52178568); SCMO_0029 (0.31294405, 0.28378774, 0.40326821).
- **Position:** NAV 1.000 at T0's close; initial position free: IN = units `w_i / close_i` of each index; OUT = `gw` GOLDBEES and `1 − gw` cash of NAV 1. A decision trade is on the next session at each instrument's OHLC4 iff `s_j` differs from the current equity target, or the target is 0 and `gw_j` differs from the sleeve weight in force. Kinds: `switch_in`, `switch_out` (sleeve bought at `gw_j`), `sleeve_rebalance` (OUT on both sides, g changed), `quarterly_reset`, `mix_rebalance` (constant mixes). A g flip while IN is not a trade; an exit on a flip decision is one exit at the new weight.
- **Trade arithmetic (T06/T08 `trade()`, amendment 1):** V = value of every holding at the fill; targets `e·V·w_i` (indices), `(1−e)·gw·V` (GOLDBEES), `(1−e)(1−gw)·V` (cash); `traded = Σ|target − held|` over all instruments; `cost = traded × cost_bps/2/10⁴`; every target is then scaled by `(V − cost)/V`. Not a fixed point.
- **Quarterly reset (IN):** a session after T0 in January, April, July or October whose previous session is in a different month; it trades iff the book holds the proxy at the start of that session and no decision trade falls on it; same arithmetic with e = 1; none while OUT; none in `constant_mix*` or `index_*` runs; `hold_proxy` resets at every quarter start; a reset on END is traded.
- **GOLDBEES leg:** close = D09b before 2014-07-01 chained with D09 from 2014-07-01; fill = D09b's OHLC4 before 2014-07-01; from 2014-07-01 T03's de-spike (OHLC4 of open/high/low clipped to MCX gold's range that day from D24b's daily file, scaled, plus the 0.5% tolerance), raw OHLC4 on sessions without an MCX bar (2014-10-23, 2015-01-01, 2015-11-11); the session 2016-10-30 has no gold bar: previous close and fill. Take the de-spike arithmetic from `verify_t08.py`'s implementation (same code path as T06/T08, source D12 replaced by the D24b daily file). R7a: fills in the proxy period at the proxy close; R7b: decisions dated before 2014-07-01 use gold weight 0.5.
- **NAV and statistics:** Σ units × close; years = (sessions − 1)/252; CAGR; max drawdown; vol = SD of daily returns × √252; Ulcer; days ≥ 10% below peak; longest under water; Calmar; percentile = (worse + 0.5 tied)/draws × 100 with ties within relative 1e-12; p = (1 + at least as good)/(draws + 1); implemented twice (vectorised, loop).
- **Runs the verifier simulates** (all costs 0 / 20.8 / 50, full window, LBES): `strategy`, `strategy_fixed50`, `hold_proxy`, `constant_mix` (f = invested weeks ÷ weeks, rest 50/50, rebalanced at every decision's next session), `constant_mix_timed` (rest split by `gw_j`), `sleeve_only`, `sleeve_only_timed`, `index_Nifty100`, `index_MC100`, `index_SC100` (one index, weight 1, no resets); at 20.8: `strategy` and `strategy_fixed50` on the R3 window and on LIQUIDCASE cash, and the two R7 variants.
- **Closed form** for the strategy: end NAV = product over fixed-unit segments of (trade-day jump × segment growth), compared at 1e-9.

## 3. Nulls and counting
- **Families:** F1 = N4.10 in-or-out + real gold; F2 = N4.9 + real gold; F3 = real in-or-out + N4.10-g gold; F4 = real in-or-out + N4.9-g gold; F5 = F1's in-or-out draw + F3's gold draw, same id; `sleeve_mode` `fixed50` for F1 and F2 (gold does not enter).
- **Bounds:** per state of each series `lo = min(2, shortest spell of the state)`, `hi = longest spell`: in-or-out in [1, 32], out [2, 35]; gold BUY [2, 36], SELL [2, 27] (full window; R3 from its own series). Totals: in 172, out 200, BUY 190, SELL 182 (R3: out 195, BUY 187, SELL 180). The verifier derives these from the real series and compares with `logs/step_1_spell_counts.json`.
- **Structure per draw** (saved states, every draw): weeks, first state, spell counts, in/BUY weeks equal the real series; N4.9/N4.9-g sorted lengths equal; N4.10/N4.10-g lengths within bounds and summing to the totals.
- **Regeneration from seeds:** N4.9 `default_rng([12, 3, id])`, N4.10 `[12, 6, id]`, N4.9-g `[12, 11, 3, id]`, N4.10-g `[12, 11, 6, id]`, id = 0..N−1: N4.9 permutes the in-spells then the out-spells and interleaves from the real first state (gold: BUY first); N4.10 uses the exact-uniform sampler (DP counts c(m, t), parts one at a time, `rng.random()` and a cumulative-sum search), in-spell (BUY) lengths first, then out (SELL), interleaved from the real first state. The verifier's sampler must reproduce the saved states for every draw in the sample.
- **Counts:** allowed tuples by inclusion–exclusion Σ_j (−1)^j C(k, j) C(S − k·lo − j(hi − lo + 1) + k − 1, k − 1) for every window, state and series, compared with `count_table.json`, with a plain DP and with `step_1_spell_counts.json` (full window: in 10,354,725,991,095,786; out 480,915,680,540,493,952; BUY 76,727,187,438,249,573,981; SELL 693,114,838,376,918,505).
- **Sampler exactness:** k = 3, S = 12, [2, 6] (19 tuples), 200,000 samples, chi-square p > 0.001 (the verifier's own seed); every position's mean over the saved draws within 4 standard errors of S ÷ k.
- **Per-row bookkeeping** of every `draws.csv` row: invested weeks, spell counts and lengths, sessions invested (coverage = next decision's session index − this one's, last week 0), gold BUY weeks, BUY/SELL spell counts and lengths, `sleeve_trades` (decisions j = 1..D−2 that are OUT after an OUT decision with g changed; 0 in `fixed50`), family tags; `spell_sig` equals `strategy_sig` on F2 rows and `g_spell_sig` equals `real_g_spell_sig` on F4 rows (informational).
- **Sampled re-simulation** (`C-draw-nav`): for every group of `draws.csv` (see the contract's row list) the first 20 and 20 seeded random draw ids, each at the group's cost, all eight statistics at 1e-9; for F1 and F3 at 20.8 bps, 20 + 20 curves against `draw_curves_20.8.npz` (float32, 1e-5).

## 4. V4 identities and signals (no T06 regression: the portfolio is new)
(a) A one-index book (weight 1, 0 cost, always IN, no resets) reproduces that index's close ÷ first close to 1e-12 on the window; (b) the verifier's `hold_proxy` at 0 cost equals its own plain-loop weighted NAV with the quarterly resets, and the engine's `hold_proxy` NAV equals it to 1e-9; (c) the signals: decisions equal the Master SIGN columns (and D24b's weekly csv `signal` for gold) at every decision date, including the stale-row rule; (d) D09b chained into D09: the splice-day return equals MAUc1's own return that day, MAUc1 rows consecutive, no non-positive price, the proxy ends 2014-06-30 and GOLDBEES starts 2014-07-01; (e) Smallcap 100 restriction: no `inputs.csv` row for a restricted file has `usable_from` later than the date the engine says it read from, the Smallcap 100 decisions use only rows dated 2011-11-25 or later.

## 5. Sleeve and reset identities (V6)
`C-sleeve`: every exit and sleeve rebalance of the verifier's strategy (all costs) against `sleeve_trades.csv` (dates, kinds, g and weight before and after, GOLDBEES and cash values before and after, traded, cost, the proxy-period flag), both directions; `C-reset`: every reset of the verifier's strategy against `quarterly_resets.csv` (date, value before and after per index, traded, cost); identities on the files' own numbers: cost = traded × bps/2/10⁴; value after = value before − cost; post-trade split = target × (V − cost)/V to 1e-12 of the value; at 0 cost the NAV is continuous through each trade; a rebalance requires a change of g; none while OUT with g constant; the proxy-period flag is true exactly before 2014-07-01.

## 6. Tolerances, self-test and exit rule
NAVs, statistics, trades, units: relative 1e-9; identities 1e-12; float32 curves 1e-5; percentiles, spell structure, counts exact. The verifier exits 0 only if rows were compared, no gate check failed or is missing, no expected evidence file (the contract's list) is missing and, in the default run, **every corruption of its self-test** is applied to a copy of the evidence and detected by its own check: a decision flipped, a gold signal week flipped, a NAV scaled, an in-or-out draw out of bounds, a gold draw out of bounds, a sleeve rebalance removed, a quarterly reset removed, a Smallcap 100 `usable_from` marker lowered in `inputs.csv`. Output `verify_t09.json` with the counts.
