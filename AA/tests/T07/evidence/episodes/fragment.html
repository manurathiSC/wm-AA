Why the whole-book switch beats the segment rule, and when

Post hoc and descriptive: it is not part of the pre-registered test and changes no verdict. Window 2019-01-02 → 2026-10-30, 20.8 bps, the same runs as everything above. The whole-book switch holds the parent only while Nifty 100, Midcap 100 and Smallcap 100 are all BUY, and sits in the 50/50 gold and cash sleeve otherwise. The segment rule keeps each stock whose own index is BUY.

The two rules hold the same weights whenever the three signals agree (all BUY: both fully invested; all SELL: both in the sleeve). So the gap between them can only open in the 16 disagreement stretches, and it does: the stretches carry all of it, and what happens outside them is -0.4 to +0.5 log points in the four books against a total of -22 to -11. Every figure in this section is in log points: 100 x ln(rule value / whole-book switch value). Positive = the rule is ahead (blue), negative = the whole-book switch is ahead (red).

The reading

  • The whole-book switch wins mainly on false starts. In 5 stretches (2, 7, 8, 12, 15) one or two indices turned BUY, the third never joined, and the market fell back to all SELL. The rule had bought the stocks of the BUY indices; the whole-book switch waited in the sleeve. That costs the rule 37 to 43 log points in the four books (shown as -43 to -37 above), more than its total deficit of 11 to 22.
  • The rule wins on shake-outs, but only twice. In stretches 5, 10 all three signals were BUY, one index turned SELL, the whole-book switch left, the other two stayed BUY and the market kept rising. The rule's gain is +24 to +41 log points. Without those two stretches the rule's total would be -52 to -40 instead of -22 to -11 (table below).
  • What the rule loses in a false start is the gap between the stocks it buys and the sleeve it gives up. Across the 5 false starts the kept stocks earned, on average over the four books, -8.6 (Large), -12.7 (Mid) and -18.2 (Small and below) percentage points of the book against the sleeve. The longest false start, stretch 15 (2025-09-22 → 2026-01-27, 18 weeks), is the one where the sleeve itself returned +21% (half of it GOLDBEES) while the rule's kept stocks lagged it.
  • This is 16 stretches, not 64 observations. The four books share the signals and differ only in which stocks they hold, so a stretch is one piece of evidence; "40 of 64" counts it four times.

"Kept stocks against the sleeve" = for every stock the rule holds, its daily return minus the rule's own sleeve return, times its value the day before, summed over the stretch and divided by the rule's value at its start (percentage points of the book). This is a different quantity from the log points above: it is in percentage points of the book, and "Other (pp)" in the per-book tables is the rule's return minus the whole-book switch's return minus the kept-stocks figure (trading costs, fills against closes, the whole-book switch's own sleeve). The positions reproduce the rule's value to 4e-16 (relative), so the attribution rests on the exact holdings; before the first logged trade (2019-03-11) the book holds only the sleeve.
