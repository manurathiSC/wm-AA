## Readings (all in `audit.json`, `readings`)
The full list is in `READINGS`. The ones another step must know:
1. **E5, weights are renormalised, as T06 does (AFFECTS STEPS 3 AND 4).** T07.md §3 step 6 says listed weights are "not renormalised" and that targets "can sum to V x (1 +- 0.0005), as in T06". T06 renormalises each basket row to 1 (T06 E5) and its targets sum to V. The two statements cannot both hold with gate V4 (1e-12 against T06's NAV): with the raw weights SCMO_0003's whole-book NAV differs from T06's by 1.5e-9 (relative; tested). V4 wins. `listed_weight` in `event_stocks.csv` is the renormalised weight (`listed_weight_raw` is the matrix value), `sum_weights` is 1.0 (`sum_weights_raw` beside it), s = D, tau is half the smallest renormalised weight. The N4.6 allowed sets are homogeneous in the weights, so they do not depend on this.
2. **EV2, a basket change while the book holds no stock (AFFECTS STEP 3).** Nothing is traded and no event is written (§3 step 4(iii)), but the basket in force changes. For the next signal test the new basket needs "current tags": they are read as of the previous session (a tag read without a trade, counted in V2 and in R5's random stream), and the book "currently drops" all of them. 7-10 such skipped basket events per book and window (listed in `audit.json`).
3. **Tie tolerance (AFFECTS STEP 3).** A subset with |sum w - D| = tau exactly is allowed (the frozen text says <=); decided with a 1e-12 tolerance. SCMO_0010 on the full window has 35 such subsets in its round-weight events (`boundary_cases` in `count_table.json`); the other books 0.
4. **V6 seeds and shifts (AFFECTS STEPS 3 AND 5).** k is drawn without replacement from the distinct circular shifts 26 <= k <= n-26 (n = decisions; negative k are the same shifts), with `default_rng([9, 100000, book_no])`; shift number i uses `default_rng([9, i, book_no])`, one stream, draw d takes the next n_events uniforms. The result does not depend on `--draws`.
5. **N4.8 (AFFECTS STEP 3).** Universe = the sorted ISINs in the realisation's `event_stocks.csv` (live window has its own); ranking by score descending; `draw_labels.npz` stores the label of every row, so the verifier can replay without scores.
6. **R5.** `default_rng([5, draw_id, book_no])`, two uniforms per rank-sourced stock per tag read (`rank` and `nonmember_rank`; membership tags are not perturbed); events are rebuilt under the perturbed tags.
7. **Constant mix** uses f = the mean daily share of NAV in stocks of the rule's path at the same cost (so f depends on the cost), rebalanced at the rule's events. `invested_weeks` of the rule = sum over decisions of the weight share the current tags keep.
8. **V7 oracle** returns: start = OHLC4 at the event's trade session (the T0 close for T0), end = OHLC4 at the next event's trade session (the last session's close for the last event); log return; lowest sum of w x log return within A; ties to the lowest mask.
9. **Output details (AFFECTS STEP 4).** `cash` is `lbes` or `liq`; `stats.csv` has `published_parent` once per (window, cash, cost) block with identical values, `published_aa` (R6, column `aa_scid`, empty cost) on the live window; `daily_nav.csv` also has `run = published_parent` with empty cost; `g_engine.csv` keeps T06's two empty columns; `events.csv` has extra `decision_date`, `sum_weights_raw`; `event_stocks.csv` has `row` (the position the drops arrays use) and `listed_weight_raw`; `trades.csv` has `event_no`, `n_dropped`; `draws.csv` and `stats.csv` as in the contract.
