## What each does
- **analyse.py** writes `analysis.log` in the blocks 4.0a (one real event and one N4.6 draw), 4.0b (gates V1-V7, reportability, allowed-subset counts, dropped weight null against rule), 4.1 (C1, C2 against N4.6 and N4.8 at 0, 20.8, 50 bps; secondary statistics; C3 with the moving-block bootstrap SE), 4.2 (comparators, trades, weight by band, tag sources), 4.3 (MDE, calibration ratio, null spreads), 4.4 (verdict and outcome map), 4.5 (R1-R6). It re-derives V3 (every draw of both nulls from `draw_drops.npz`/`draw_labels.npz`/`event_stocks.csv`, plus a seeded replay of the first 100 N4.6 draws per book and window), V4 (against T06's evidence), V5(b, c) (brute force over all 2^n subsets at every event), V6 (from `controls_negative.csv`, per book) and V7 (percentile recomputed from the oracle's CAGR against `draws.csv`). Exit 1 if any gate fails, with a plain "V6 FAILED ... nothing below may be read" line. `--expect-draws` (default 2000) makes V3 fail on a different draw count.
- **fill_writeup.py** copies `analysis.log` into §4 between the markers. Tested on a copy of `T07.md`: sections 1-3 (and the header) and 5-8 byte-identical, `check_prereg.py` OK on the copy, second run idempotent, refuses (exit 1) when the END marker or the `## 5. Findings` line is missing or when the log lacks a block; CRLF kept. `T07.md` itself is unchanged.
- **report.py** writes `evidence/report.html`: answer, data (inputs with through-dates, the "As of" date, the five tag caveats read from T07.md §3, tag sources by exposure, V4 table, gates, controls V6 and V7 per book, reportability), one rule end to end (one real event of SCMO_0003, the tag of every held stock, targets, trade, NAV, one N4.6 draw replayed from its seed, the ranking), headline for both nulls, C3, comparators, secondary statistics, per-book equity and drawdown curves (rule, whole-book switch, parent, constant mix), null histograms with N4.6 and N4.8, the weight-by-band chart, the regime chart of the disagreement weeks with the table of every stretch, R1-R6, power (MDE, calibration, spreads, allowed subsets), definitions. Every chart is hoverable (crosshair, x and y chips, tooltip of everything drawn at the pointer), from JSON arrays that also draw the chart.
- **check_page.py** (582+ checks) recomputes the whole `page-data` block key for key with its own code, re-derives decisions from the Master files and published NAVs, checks every input against `APPROVED_DATA.md` and the file's sha256, re-derives the tag of every stock of the example from the D23 panels with its own lookup, replays the example N4.6 draw and the first 200 draws per book and window, checks every draw of both nulls, and recomputes every hover array and the rectangles drawn in the SVGs.
- **rerun_draw.py** calls `python -I analysis/t07_engine.py --rerun ...` as a program, compares `end_nav`, `cagr`, `max_dd` (1e-12) and the drops with `draws.csv` and `draw_drops.npz`; exit 0 only if equal, 2 if no stored row, 3 if the engine fails. `--engine` points at another program (used with a mock).
- **run.sh** as T06's with the T07 names and V1-V7.

## Places where the frozen text or the contract is silent or ambiguous
1. **AFFECTS STEP 2 (done by the engine, confirmed from smoke):** `listed_weight` is renormalised (reading E5); the page says so in the targets step. `row` in `event_stocks.csv` is checked to be 0..R-1 in file order (the drops columns).
2. **AFFECTS STEPS 2 AND 3:** bit i = stock i in increasing ISIN order, "listed in increasing order of its bitmask", the subset within tau inclusive with 1e-12 slack, one `rng.random()` per event including events with one allowed subset. `analyse.py` and `check_page.py` replay N4.6 from the seed on that reading and gate V3 on it; on the smoke run 40 replays matched.
3. **AFFECTS STEP 3:** `verify_t07.json`'s shape is not fixed. `analyse.py` accepts T06's (`exit_ok`, `gate_failures_and_missing`, `gate_compared`, `selftest.detected`) or any `compared`/`failures` pairs plus a `detected` key; a verdict it cannot read is a V1 FAIL.
4. **AFFECTS STEP 2:** `controls_oracle.json` shape is not fixed. Accepted: `{book: {...}}`, `{"books": {book: {...}}}` or a list of rows with `book`, holding `cagr` and/or `percentile`. If `cagr` is there the percentile is recomputed from `draws.csv` and must agree with the stated one. `controls_negative.csv` uses `percentile`; the share is recomputed (not read from the json); K must be at least 100.
5. **AFFECTS STEP 2:** `audit.json`: V2 is read from `v2.violations` and `v2.checked` (plus `v2.pass`); V3 from any `pass` flags and a `bad` key. The engine's `count_table.json` shape (`books -> book -> window -> events[]` with `n_allowed_subsets`) is read as in smoke.
6. **AFFECTS STEP 6:** the calibration ratio's SE is the SD over 1,000 moving-block resamples (blocks of 20 sessions, `default_rng([10, book_no])`, one `integers(0, n-19, size=(1000, ceil(n/20)))` call) of CAGR(rule) minus CAGR(whole-book) on the paired daily log returns; the frozen text says "seeded" only. The z-times-ratio percentile (N6.6) is printed as a diagnostic.
7. **AFFECTS STEP 6:** a book that fails the reportability gate on the full window cannot pass C1, C2 or C3 (counted as not passing) and is named; R2's live percentiles of a non-reportable live realisation are blank.
8. **AFFECTS STEP 6:** V4's decision regression compares T06's decisions with `all_three_buy` (T07's `state` column is not relied on) for every T06 decision except each book's last (T06's 2026-10-22 week is truncated). The NAV comparison runs to 2026-10-22 as in the frozen text.
9. **AFFECTS STEP 6:** R6 reads the D15 files directly through the APPROVED rows (not `stats.csv`'s `published_aa` rows) so the page can check them from the raw files; the AA book's CAGR is over the sessions of the same live window.
10. **AFFECTS STEP 6:** "mean weight by band" is computed from the events (event-weighted and session-weighted); the frozen §2 numbers are weekly from the baskets, so they will differ slightly. `mean_dropped_weight` in `draws.csv` is not relied on (the dropped weight per event is recomputed from the drops).
11. The page and `check_page.py` do not read `trades.csv` or `positions.csv` (the example's V, traded and cost come from `events.csv`); `stats.csv`'s `trades` and `cost_paid` are used for R1.
12. `check_page.py` does not replay N4.8 from its seed (the score's distribution is not stated; the engine stores the labels). It checks the labels against the counts and the signals for every draw. The persistence of labels within a draw is the verifier's check (C-draws), not repeated here.
