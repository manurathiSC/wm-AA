## Ambiguities and choices (prefix = who must know)
- **AFFECTS STEP 3 (from the coordinator, 2026-11-12), three clarifications adopted:** (1) listed weights are renormalised per basket row (T06's convention; V4 needs it); default `--weights auto` infers from `events.csv` `sum_weights` (exactly 1 within 1e-13 means norm); `raw` stays selectable. T07.md §3 step 6's "not renormalised ... as in T06" is wrong. (2) A basket that arrives while no stock is held trades nothing, but its tags (as of the session before) become the current tags and the book "currently drops" all of them; default on (`--literal-skipped-basket` gives the literal reading, which yields 2 extra events in SCMO_0003). (3) N4.6 allowed set uses `|sum w - D| <= tau` with a 1e-12 tolerance; exact ties are allowed. Boundary cases within 1e-9 are noted by C-count.
- **AFFECTS STEP 2 (not an engine fault, a gap in the contract):** `count_table.json` and `controls_oracle.json` layouts are not fixed; the verifier reads them tolerantly (a per-book entry, a list of per-event records with a key containing "allowed", a key containing "cagr"/"pct"). `events.csv` is keyed by (book, window, trade_date), `event_stocks.csv` by (book, window, trade_date, isin), so row order is free except for the draw arrays, which follow the engine's `event_stocks.csv` order.
- **AFFECTS STEP 2:** `basket_eff` accepted as the availableDate (contract) or the effective session; `trades.csv` `e_after` accepted as stock value share of the post-trade targets or as 1 - s; `band_signal` accepted as BUY/SELL or the index name; `stats.csv` `events` accepted as events incl. T0 or minus 1; positions' cash instrument name is not fixed (anything that is not a stock or GOLDBEES is the cash leg); trades/positions rows at T0 are ignored if present.
- **AFFECTS STEP 2:** `mean_dropped_weight` is read as the mean over all events (incl. T0) of the dropped listed weight D (renormalised), `n_events` as the events incl. T0.
- **AFFECTS STEP 2:** constant mix: f = average over all sessions of stock value / NAV of the rule's path at the same cost (matches `audit.json` S1); the verifier also accepts the 0-cost path's f and reports which matched. Oracle: lowest sum of w x log return, fill-to-fill (OHLC4 at the trade session, T0 at the close; last event to the last close), or close-to-close; the evidence's convention is reported. Both are consistent with `audit.json` V7.
- **AFFECTS STEP 2:** `g_engine.csv` `cagr_gap_pp_yr` is accepted as either the (sessions - 1) / 252 form or the calendar-year form (T06's engine uses the latter; the limit is the same).
- **AFFECTS STEP 5:** draw curves are float32, compared at a relative 1e-6 (the spec's 1e-9 applies to the end NAV in `draws.csv`, which is compared at 1e-9). The 2,000-draw count per key is required only when the evidence directory is called `headline`; `controls_oracle.json` is required only for `headline`. The corrupted copies and the mock go to the scratch directory, never to `evidence/`.
- Real-run cost: about 40 s for one book without self-test on smoke; the full headline run (4 books x 2,000 draws of both nulls, plus the persistence test of every N4.8 draw) will take several minutes; C-draws loops over every draw once.
