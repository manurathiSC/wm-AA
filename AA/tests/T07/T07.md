## 1. Idea and references

**The idea in one sentence.** Tag each stock a parent holds as Large, Mid, or Small and below; hold it only while its band's weekly supertrend is BUY (Large: Nifty 100, Mid: Midcap 100, Small and below: Smallcap 100), and send the weight of the stocks whose band is SELL to a 50/50 GOLDBEES and cash sleeve; the question is whether this choice of *which stocks to drop* beats dropping a random set of the same weight at the same events, on the latest approved data.

**Where it came from.** Today each AA book flips its whole parent basket into the sleeve when the index signals turn bearish. Study 3 asked whether deciding stock by stock does better. It found that it helps the books whose parents pick stocks well and hurts the weak ones, and rejected the idea (its gate G1 needed a positive difference in at least 3 of 4 books, including 0044 and 0045; only 2 books were positive), but its tests predate the standard now used: it had no frozen pre-registration, its timing null (a circular shift of the signals) could not reach 2,000 draws, and its tags came from files that are not approved. T07 asks the same question with a null matched to the claim, reviewed tag data and the gates of T03-T06.

**The decision this will change.** Whether segment-level holding is worth building into the AA books at all, and whether Study 3's rejection stands on a matched null.

**What is already known.**

**What this test is NOT asking.**
- Whether the whole-book switch times the parents better than chance. That is F-AA-S04 and S07; here it is a comparator.

- Whether the rule works on stocks the four parents do not hold. No prices exist for them (approved data covers the parents' stocks only).

- **The sample is thin.** The rule differs from the whole-book switch only in the 88 disagreement weeks, which fall in 16 stretches shared by all four books. "3 of 4 books" is a consistency check, not four independent tests.
- **Expectation, declared:** Study 3 found no robust gain, so the likeliest outcome is that C1 fails in at least two books. The books whose parents hold more large caps (SCMO_0010, 0029) have the most weight in Large and are likeliest to differ from the whole-book rule. SCMO_0007 holds almost no large caps, so its result is nearly the Midcap/Smallcap split alone. No pass mark depends on this expectation.

**The rule** (specified in §3; one rule, no parameters): at each event, tag every stock in the basket in force; keep it at its listed weight while the signal of its band is BUY; send the weight of the rest to 50% GOLDBEES and 50% cash.

**Hypotheses** (after cost at 20.8 bps round trip; one rule; judged per book):
- **H1, selection.** The stocks the rule drops are worse than a random set of the same weight dropped at the same events. Judged on CAGR against N4.6. Falsified if C1 fails.
- **H2, protection.** The rule's worst fall is shallower than that of those random exclusions. Judged on maximum drawdown against N4.6. Falsified if C2 fails.
- **H3, increment.** The rule earns more than the whole-book switch on the same prices, fills, costs and sleeve. Judged on the difference of after-cost CAGRs. Falsified if C3 fails.

**Null (primary): N4.6 size-matched random exclusion.**

| Dimension | Randomised / held / not matched | How | Measured in §4.0 as |
|---|---|---|---|
| which stocks are dropped at each event | **randomised** | a random subset of the held stocks, uniform over all subsets whose total listed weight is within τ of the weight the rule drops (τ = half the smallest listed weight in the basket); redrawn independently at every event | per event: weight dropped, drop count |
| the weight dropped | held | to within τ. With equal weights this is the same count. When the rule drops nothing or everything, the only allowed subset is the empty or the full one | dropped weight per event, null against rule (must lie within τ) |
| events: their dates, the three signals, the basket in force, the trade sessions | held | the null shares the rule's event list | event count (must be identical) |
| book, stocks, listed weights, parent rebalances, calendar, sleeve, cost, execution | held | constant for the run | — |
| whether a dropped stock stays dropped from one event to the next | **not held** | the null redraws at each event, so its turnover can differ from the rule's | trades and cost paid per draw against the rule's; the 0 bps percentile beside the 20.8 bps one |
| which band's signal a stock follows | **not used** | N4.6 does not read tags at all | — |

The rule's own drop set lies inside the allowed set at every event (its dropped weight is the target, so it differs by 0), so the rule is one allowed configuration. Gate V5 checks this.

**Null (companion, reported, not a criterion): N4.8 persistent label permutation.** One random score per ISIN per draw. At every event, rank the held stocks by their score and give the first n_L the label Large, the next n_M Mid and the rest Small and below, where n_L, n_M and n_S are the rule's counts per band at that event; the real signals then act on the labels, so the stocks dropped are those whose assigned label's index is SELL. Held: the events, the counts per band, the signals. Randomised: which stock carries which label. Because the score is fixed within a draw, a stock tends to keep its label from one event to the next, as a real tag does. The dropped weight is **not** matched exactly when weights are unequal; it is reported per event.

**Which null decides.** N4.6 decides T07's verdict. N4.8 is reported beside it. If they disagree, both are reported in the verdict and the finding's status note says so (AGENT.md N6.8).

**Statistic the nulls are judged on.** After-cost CAGR for H1, higher better. Maximum drawdown on daily NAV for H2, shallower better. For H3 the difference of after-cost CAGRs, rule minus the whole-book switch.
- **Unit:** one realisation is book × cost × null. The percentile is computed per realisation against its own draws.
- **Percentile:** (draws worse + 0.5 × draws tied) ÷ draws × 100. Ties are equal within a relative 1e-12. **p-value:** (1 + draws at least as good) ÷ (draws + 1). The pass mark is on the percentile.
- **Secondary statistics**, each with its own percentile against the same draws: volatility of daily returns × √252 (lower better); Ulcer index (lower better); days at least 10% below the running peak (fewer better); longest time under water (shorter better); Calmar = CAGR ÷ |maximum drawdown|. Trades and cost paid are reported per draw.

**Pass mark and draws.** The percentile must be at least 90. 2,000 draws give a resolution of 0.05 points and a smallest p-value of 1/2001; D + 1 ≥ m ÷ α is met for the two primary criteria (m = 2, α = 0.10).

On or after it, the tag of a stock is the band of the index it is listed in on the latest `index_constituents_monthly` snapshot on or before the date (Nifty 100 Large, Midcap 150 Mid, Smallcap 250 and Microcap 250 Small and below); a stock listed in none of the four indices uses the rank rule ("non-member fallback"). Before it, the tag is the band of the stock's rank on the latest `mcap_based_universe` snapshot on or before the date: market cap descending among ordinary shares, ties by quote_id, rank 1 to 100 Large, 101 to 250 Mid, above 250 Small and below. No averaging; the 2026 market-cap rows are daily and used as stored. A stock with no rank reads Small and below and is counted (0 in the counts above). A stock is found by its ISIN or, through `symbology_changes`, by any other ISIN that shares its quote_id.

**Caveats of the tag data, to be stated on the page** (from `REVIEW.md`): (1) the market-cap history to 2025-11-28 was back-filled (written 2026-01-02/03 and 2025-12-31), so a point-in-time vintage cannot be proven; (2) the rank rule is untested before 2026-04-09: its 92.07% three-band agreement with real membership (Large recall 88.1%, Mid 79.8%, Small and below 96.5%) is measured on eight 2026 snapshots; on the parents' own holdings it is 88.9% by weight (SCMO_0003 91.1%, 0007 88.2%, 0010 80.0%, 0029 96.3%); (3) the tag source switches on 2026-04-09 and about 8% of stocks differ between the two sources; (4) isolated bad market-cap rows exist (LODHA 2021, AXISBANK 2026-10-07), none changes a parent holding's band; (5) JSWSTEEL, VEDL and ZEEL (listed in D21 under preference-share ISINs) and EMBASSY, BIRET and BAGMANE (not in D20) are not in any basket to 2026-10-30 and would read by rank in a later basket.

**Construction, in order.** Steps 1 to 3 are T06 §3 (steps 1 to 3) with the window ending 2026-10-30:

1. **Calendar.** The sessions are D17's dates (the extended published NAVs), per book, from its first date T0 (2019-01-02; 0007 2019-01-04) to **2026-10-30**. The sessions D18 has and D17 lacks are never read (F-AA-D15).
2. **Decision dates.** T0, then the last D17 session of each ISO week after T0. The decision at the window's last session (2026-10-30) is made but not traded.
3. **Signals at decision date d.** Read the latest Master row of each of the three indices dated on or before d. A band is *on* if the SIGN of its index is BUY: Large reads Nifty 100, Mid reads Midcap 100, Small and below reads Smallcap 100. The *state* of d is the triple of the three SIGN cells.
4. **Events.** An event is a trade session on which targets are rebuilt: (i) **T0**, the initial position, free of cost: the basket in force at T0, as drifted since its availableDate and scaled to NAV 1.000 at T0's close (T06), with the value of the stocks dropped at T0 (tags and state read as of T0 itself) held at T0's closes, half in GOLDBEES and half in cash units; (ii) a **signal event**: the next D17 session after a decision date d on which the set of stocks the current tags would drop under d's state differs from the set the book currently drops. "Current tags" are those read at the last event; tags are re-read only at events, never between them. When all three bands are tied (the whole-book switch) this reduces to T06's rule that a trade happens only when the stock share changes; (iii) a **basket event**: the first D17 session on or after a non-skipped basket's availableDate (skipped baskets are dropped), unless the book holds no stock on that session (then nothing is traded, as in T06). Two events on one session are one event. Between events no target changes and the book drifts. The information date of an event on session s is the D17 session immediately before s; the state (the triple of the latest decision made before s) and the tags of that event are read as of it.
5. **Tags at an event.** For every stock the basket in force holds (listed weight > 0), `tag_lookup.tag(ISIN, information date)` gives its band. A stock is **kept** if its band is on, **dropped** otherwise.
6. **Targets (T06's convention, there is no cash account).** Let V be the book's value at session s's OHLC4 before cost. Each kept stock's target value is V × w_i, its listed weight in the basket in force (listed weights are not renormalised; they sum to 0.9996-1.0005). GOLDBEES and cash units each get a target value V × s ÷ 2, where s = D ÷ Σw, D is the listed weight of the dropped stocks and Σw the listed weight of all held stocks. All stocks kept: s = 0, T06's IN (V × w_i in each stock, nothing in the sleeve). All stocks dropped: s = 1, T06's OUT (V ÷ 2 in each of GOLDBEES and cash). The targets can sum to V × (1 ± 0.0005), as in T06; every new position is then scaled by k = (V − cost) ÷ V.
7. **Execution.** At an event, every instrument is moved from its current value to its target on session s at that session's OHLC4 (stocks: Eikon bar with the Polyplex correction, the close if the bar lacks an open, high or low; GOLDBEES: the de-spiked OHLC4, high and low clipped to MCX gold's range that day, scaled, plus 0.5%, and the raw OHLC4 after the MCX file ends; cash: the level on that session, as-of). Cost: ow = cost_bps ÷ 2 ÷ 10,000 per side on the sum of the absolute differences between current and target values over every stock, GOLDBEES and cash (the traded value; a stock whose target equals its current value costs nothing, and a stock held in both the old and the new basket is traded only by the difference). Kept stocks are re-set to their listed weights at every event. No trade happens between events.
8. **NAV.** Cash account + Σ stock units × close + GOLDBEES units × close + cash units × level. It is 1.000 at T0's close. No sale at the end.
9. **Statistics.** As T06 §3 step 9 (years = (sessions − 1) ÷ 252; CAGR; maximum drawdown; Ulcer index; days at least 10% below the running peak; longest time under water; volatility × √252; Calmar).
10. **Comparators** (benchmarks, not nulls), on the same calendar, cost and T0: the **whole-book switch** (every stock follows the single state IN iff all three signals are BUY, else 100% sleeve; this is T06's strategy and is the engine with the three bands tied together); hold the parent (the engine with every stock held, at 0 cost and at each cost, plus the published NAV rebased to 1.000); the constant mix (the rule's average share of NAV in the parent's stocks over the window, held as that share in the parent and half the rest in GOLDBEES and half in cash, rebalanced at each event's session at OHLC4 with the same cost); the 100% sleeve.
11. **Null draws.** From the rule's event list (the dates, the states, the basket in force, the held stocks and listed weights), draw per event:
    - **N4.6.** The held stocks are indexed 0 … n − 1 (n ≤ 16) in ISIN order and w_i is their listed weights. Let D be the weight the rule drops at this event and τ = ½ min_i w_i. The allowed set A is every subset X with |Σ_{i∈X} w_i − D| ≤ τ, listed in increasing order of its bitmask (n ≤ 16, so at most 65,536 subsets, enumerated exactly). The dropped subset is A[⌊u·|A|⌋] with u = `rng.random()`, from `numpy.random.default_rng([7, draw_id, book_no])`, one uniform per event in event order. (book_no = 0 … 3 in the order SCMO_0003, 0007, 0010, 0029.) When the rule drops nothing, A holds only the empty set, and when it drops everything A holds only the full set (any other subset differs from D by at least the smallest weight, which exceeds τ). The rule's own drop set is in A by construction. *Worked example (five stocks of equal weight 0.2, rule drops two, so D = 0.4 and τ = 0.1):* A is the ten 2-subsets; the null drops one of them with probability 1/10, from a uniform number in [0, 1).
    - **N4.8.** For each draw, draw one score per ISIN the book ever holds, in ISIN order, with `numpy.random.default_rng([8, draw_id, book_no])`; at each event assign the labels as in §2 and drop the stocks whose label's band is off.
    - The same draw ids are used for every cost. Each draw runs through the same engine (steps 5 to 9) with the same cost; only which stocks are dropped differs. The dropped set of every draw at every event is saved (`draw_drops.npz`) and `rerun_draw.py` reproduces any draw from its id.
12. **Controls.** V6 and V7 as in §2; their seeds are `[9, shift_index, book_no]` (negative control) and none (oracle, which is deterministic).
13. **MDE.** For C1, the smallest edge detectable at 80% power and 5% two-sided is 2.8 × null_sd, where null_sd is the SD of after-cost CAGR across the draws of a realisation (per null). For C2 it is 2.8 × the SD of maximum drawdown. Both are printed beside every FAIL. **Disclosed:** the numbers did not exist when this section was written; the thresholds in §2 do not depend on them. With baskets of 3 to 16 stocks and 16 stretches the null's spread will be limited by the number of allowed subsets, which the engine prints per event.

**How the test works, in plain words.**

- **The series.** Each parent's NAV is built every day from the stocks it holds. Every Friday, three index signals are read. Each stock is tagged by size (Large, Mid, or Small and below) and follows the signal of its size's index. A stock whose index is BUY stays; a stock whose index is SELL is sold, and its weight goes half to GOLDBEES and half to cash. Trades happen at the next session's (open + high + low + close) ÷ 4, paying 10.4 bps on every rupee traded. *[the real signals, tags, trades and NAVs are printed in §4.0a]*
- **One event.** On a Friday where a signal flipped (or on the day a new basket starts), look up every held stock's size tag as of the session before, find its index's SIGN, and set the targets as in step 6. *[printed in §4.0a, for one real event]*
- **The result of one path.** Units of each stock, GOLDBEES and cash are fixed between events. Each day the NAV is Σ units × close. The CAGR and drawdown come from that daily NAV.
- **One draw of the main null (N4.6).** Keep the same events and the same weight to drop. Instead of dropping the stocks whose index is SELL, drop a random set of stocks of about the same total weight. Everything else is identical. *[one draw is printed in §4.0a]*
- **One draw of the companion null (N4.8).** Keep the number of Large, Mid and Small stocks, but hand the size labels to stocks at random (the same random order all the way through the draw), so the real signals now act on the wrong stocks.
- **The ranking.** The rule's CAGR is placed among the 2,000 draws' CAGRs: the percentile is the share of draws it beats (ties count half); the p-value is the chance of doing as well by luck. 90 or more is a pass. The rule is also compared with the whole-book switch directly (C3).
- **The comparators.** The whole-book switch is what the books do today. The constant mix holds the same average share in the parent's stocks all the time: it shows how much of any gain is only less exposure.

**Point-in-time rule.** A decision at d reads Master rows dated at or before d only (step 3). A tag is read as of the session before its trade session and uses the latest snapshot on or before that date (step 4; `tag_lookup.py` never uses a later snapshot, and its self-test checks this). A trade is on a session after d. The engine asserts all three for every event, counts them, and writes the counts to `audit.json`. The Master rows before 2021 were produced afterwards by the production code and are not signals traded at the time; the market-cap history before 2025-11-28 was back-filled (caveat 1); both are disclosed, not corrected.

**Execution and cost.** AGENT.md R1 (next-session OHLC4, price return, 10.4 bps a side, weights drift) with the specifics of steps 6 and 7: cash trades pay the cost; the first position at T0 is free; no sale at the end; GOLDBEES uses the de-spiked fill (X02) where the MCX file covers the session. After 2026-09-28 raw OHLC4 is used; the rule's events after that date (the Smallcap 100 signal turned SELL on 2026-10-01) trade on such sessions.

**Effective sample size and power.** 410 weekly decisions (SCMO_0007 409); 88 disagreement weeks in 16 stretches (live: 55 in 11); 26-27 events in disagreement weeks (live 16-17). The independent unit for the segment effect is the stretch. MDE: step 13, computed after the run from the draws.

**What the nulls control for, and what they do not.** N4.6 controls for the events, the weight dropped and the exposure; it does not control for how long a stock stays dropped (turnover), which R1 and R4 report. N4.8 controls for the number of stocks per label and the persistence of a label within a draw; it does not match the dropped weight exactly. Neither controls for gold's rally in the sleeve; the whole-book switch holds the same sleeve, so C3 is not affected by it, while the constant mix and R3 bound it. Neither controls for the tag data's limits below.

### Amendments before the run (2026-11-12): corrections to the frozen text. §1–§3 above are unchanged

Made by Manu's decision ("B", chat 2026-11-12) before the headline run existed and before any C1, C2 or C3 value had been computed or read. The frozen §1–§3 are byte-identical (`python check_prereg.py`); these notes sit outside them and are shown on the results page.

1. **V6 (negative control) was too tight, and is amended.** §2 says each book passes V6 if the share of its 100 information-free signal sets reaching the 90th percentile lies in [4%, 16%] (10% ± 2·√(0.1 × 0.9 ÷ 100)). That band is two-sided and applied per book: with four books a fair test fails at least one of them by chance about one time in five. **Amended rule:** a book passes V6 if **at most 17** of its 100 signal sets reach the 90th percentile of CAGR; it fails at 18 or more. 17 is the largest count X with P(Binomial(100, 0.10) ≥ X) ≥ 0.05 ÷ 4 (P(≥ 17) = 0.0206, P(≥ 18) = 0.0100). It is one-sided on purpose: a null that is too easy to beat creates false positives, while a null that is too hard to beat only costs power, which the MDE reports. **What was known when this was decided:** the engine's four-book scratch run, made before the amendment, gave shares of 13%, 14%, 17% and 8% (SCMO_0003, 0007, 0010, 0029); under the frozen band SCMO_0010 (17%) fails by one point. The amendment was made after seeing that, so it is a loosening of a gate after seeing it would fail; the seeds and the shift set were fixed before that run and were not tuned. V6 uses shifted signals and does not involve the real rule's result. `t07_engine.py`, `analyse.py`, `report.py` and `check_page.py` apply the amended rule (each has its own copy of the cut-off function, `v6_xmax`).
2. **Weights are renormalised, not "not renormalised".** §3 step 6 says listed weights are not renormalised "as in T06". T06 divides every basket row of the D19 weight matrix by its row sum, so each basket sums to exactly 1, and V4 (whole-book mode equals T06's NAV) holds only that way (raw weights differ by 1.5e-9 relative). The engine, the verifier and the page follow T06. `event_stocks.csv` holds `listed_weight` (renormalised) beside `listed_weight_raw`; N4.6's allowed subsets and τ use the renormalised weights. No number in §2's counts is affected, since they read no weights.
3. **A reading the frozen text leaves open.** §3 step 4 says a basket event while the book holds no stock is not an event. The engine and the verifier also read the new basket's tags as of the previous session and use them as the "current tags" for the next signal test (7-10 times per book and window). Every other reading where §1–§3 was silent is in `evidence/<run>/audit.json` under `readings`.
