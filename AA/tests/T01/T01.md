## 1. Idea and references

**The idea in one sentence.** Judge a smallcase by its own index series. When its price sits in the bottom of its recent range (its momentum dying, or the series flat and slipping), move the book's weight into another asset class, gold and cash, until the series recovers.

**Where it came from.** The user (Manu), 2026-10-01, verbatim: *"i want to desgin a framework where . i need to check whether the smallcase is doing good or not based on its index price and allocate the weight to different asset class when the momentum is dying. in th e index series. or its flat. ... i was thinking of calucalting some percentule value of the index series for past 65 days and if th ecurrent value is in the bottom 25"*. It is a proposal to be tested, with no prior evidence behind the 65 / 25 numbers.

**Prior work this relates to** (these studies predate AA's finding IDs and are cited by path):

| Ref | What it says | Relationship |
|---|---|---|
| `AA/analysis/evidence/why_n100sc100/` (2026-09-25) | Whole-book timing on market-index supertrends (N100 AND SC100) has a strong drawdown edge, but its return edge depends on the regime: it lost to SC100-only on the parents 2014-21 | contrasts: market-index timing vs the smallcase's own series |
| `AA/analysis/evidence/why_n100sc100/gold_why_*` | Timing gold with its own supertrend was exposure, not timing: it lost to a fixed sleeve holding the same average gold share | the same trap could apply here; the null below is built to catch it |
| `AA/analysis/evidence/final_strategy/` | The triple rule (N100, MC100 and SC100 all BUY) on these 4 books: live 2021-26 26.6% CAGR, 2014-21 18.4%, with an 80/20 gold-timed sleeve | descriptive comparator only (§2) |

**What this test is NOT asking.**
- Whether the rule should replace the current AA rule or the triple rule. This test compares the rule with holding the smallcase and with randomly timed exits, not with those rules. They appear only as descriptive rows.
- Whether the smallcase is doing well **relative to its benchmark** (stock selection). Every rule here reads only the smallcase's absolute index path. Relative strength is listed as an untested idea in §6.
- Which asset the freed weight should go to. The sleeve is fixed at the current AA convention, 50/50 GOLDBEES / LBES, and the cash-only sleeve is a robustness check.

## 2. Hypothesis and pre-registered criteria

**Written before any run, 2026-10-01.** What was already known:
- Every series used here has been looked at before: the four parents' published NAVs 2021-26 (CAGR and drawdown in `final_strategy/summary_stats.csv`) and their 2014-21 harness rebuilds.
- No rule built on a parent's **own** index path has been computed on any of them. The reference config is the user's, not tuned.
- Small-cap Indian baskets had deep falls in 2018-19 and 2020 (harness parents' worst falls −45% to −67% in 2014-21). Any rule that is out of the market in those falls will show a drawdown gain.
- **Expectation, declared:** H2 passes; H1 fails in at least one window, because a 65-day percentile on a choppy series whipsaws; H3 is uncertain.

**Hypotheses** (all for the reference config R = `pct_w65_q25_weekly`, after cost at 20.8 bps, 50/50 sleeve):
- **H1, timing.** At identical time in the sleeve and identical switch pattern, R's exits earn more than randomly timed exits. Falsified if C1 fails.
- **H2, protection.** R's worst fall is shallower than holding the smallcase. Falsified if C2 fails.
- **H3, risk-adjusted.** R's Calmar beats holding the smallcase. Falsified if C3 fails.

**Null: a circular shift of the weekly target series.** Specification:

| Dimension | Randomised / held / not matched | How | Measured in §4.0 as |
|---|---|---|---|
| timing of the rule's targets relative to the smallcase's path | **randomised** | the sequence of weekly targets w_0 … w_n is rotated by k positions, for every k with 8 ≤ k ≤ n+1−8 | — |
| share of weeks in the sleeve, and the target levels used | held | exactly, per realisation (a rotation preserves the multiset) | strategy vs every draw: share of weeks at each target level (must be identical) |
| spell lengths and switch count | held, except at the wrap point | per realisation | switch count, strategy vs draw (within ±2) |
| book, window, prices, sleeve, cost, calendar, execution | held | constant for the run | — |
| persistence of the signal | held | it is the same series, rotated | — |
| what the sleeve earned in the weeks it is held | **not matched** | a shifted draw holds the sleeve in different weeks, so gold's path is sampled differently | null CAGR spread (null_sd) and R4 (cash sleeve) |
| the wrap: the shifted series joins the window's last weeks to its first | **not matched** | one spell per draw may be cut or merged | switch-count gap |

There is one null. B&H (holding the smallcase) is a comparator, not a null.

**Statistic the null is judged on:** after-cost CAGR. Higher is better.
- **Unit.** One realisation is book × window × cost × sleeve. The percentile is computed per realisation, against that realisation's own draws.
- **Percentile:** (count of draws below + 0.5 × count equal) ÷ draws × 100.
- **Why CAGR answers H1.** The null holds exposure and switching fixed and moves only *when* the exits happen. If the rule's exits land on the smallcase's weak stretches, it earns more than the same exits placed at random.
- **Secondary statistics, each with its own percentile vs the same draws:**
  - maximum drawdown on daily NAV (shallower is better);
  - volatility of daily returns × √252 (lower is better);
  - Sharpe = (CAGR − r_f) ÷ volatility, with r_f = 0;
  - Calmar = CAGR ÷ |maximum drawdown|.
- Total return is rank-equivalent to CAGR within a realisation, so it is not reported separately.

## 3. Methodology

**Data.**

| File | Rows | Contributes |
|---|--:|---|
| `AA/Historical Data/parentIndices.csv` | 4,744 (4 parents × 1,186 days) | published parent NAV (close only), 2021-11-01 → 2026-08-21 |
| `AA/analysis/evidence/final_strategy/nav_curves_daily.csv`, columns `before the books 2014-07 -> 2021-10\|SCMO_004x\|parent (no overlay)` | 1,807 days per parent | the harness rebuild of each parent from its real baskets, 2014-07-04 → 2021-10-29 (price return). Book → parent: 0042 → 0003, 0043 → 0007, 0044 → 0010, 0045 → 0029 |
| `AA/analysis/evidence/final_strategy/gold_daily_ohlc_eikon.csv` | 3,019 | GOLDBEES daily close (Eikon), 2014-07-01 → 2026-09-24 |
| `AA/Historical Data/LBES_Adjusted_Price_data_2026_08_10.csv` | 5,693 | LBES adjusted level (cash), 2007-06-29 → 2026-08-09; forward-filled to 2026-08-21 |
| `AA/Historical Data/frozen_2026-09-25/Master/Master_{Nifty100,MC100,SC100}.xlsx` | weekly | SIGN, for the descriptive triple-rule rows only |

**Construction, in order.**

1. **Splice each parent.**
   - P_t is the harness rebuild for t ≤ 2021-10-29.
   - For t ≥ 2021-11-01, P_t = P_{2021-10-29} × published_t ÷ published_{2021-11-01}. The 2021-10-29 → 2021-11-01 session therefore has a return of 0: the published series starts at 100 on 2021-11-01 and gives no return for that day.
   - The session calendar is the union of the two sources' dates (NSE days).
2. **Sleeve prices** go on the same calendar, with gaps carried forward: GOLDBEES close, and the LBES level.
3. **Weekly decision sessions.** The last session of each ISO week in the calendar, plus the window's base session T0. T0 is the last session on or before 2015-07-31 for W1, and 2021-11-01 for W2. Each window ends on its last session (2021-10-29, 2026-08-21).
4. **Signal at decision session d**, from P up to and including d only:
   - `pct_w{W}_q{q}`: p_d = the share of the W previous closes P_{d−W} … P_{d−1} that are strictly below P_d, × 100. Equity weight = 0 if p_d < q, else 1. W ∈ {20, 40, 65, 130, 250} sessions, q ∈ {10, 25, 40}: 15 configs.
   - `pct_w65_hyst`: exit to the sleeve when p_d < 25. Re-enter equity only when p_d ≥ 50. Otherwise keep the previous state, which starts as equity.
   - `pct_w65_step`: equity weight 1 if p_d ≥ 50, 0.5 if 25 ≤ p_d < 50, 0 if p_d < 25. This is the user's "or it's flat" case as a graded step.
   - `pct_w65_q25_daily`: as R, but every session is a decision session.
   - `sma_{n}`: weight 0 if P_d < the mean of P_{d−n+1} … P_d, else 1. n ∈ {50, 100, 200}.
   - `tsmom_{L}`: weight 0 if P_d ÷ P_{d−L} − 1 < 0, else 1. L ∈ {65, 130, 250}.
   - `ddhigh_250_x{X}`: weight 0 if P_d ÷ max(P_{d−249} … P_d) − 1 < −X%, else 1. X ∈ {7.5 (`x7p5`), 10, 15}.
   - `st_weekly`: the production supertrend on the smallcase's weekly candles.
     - Each ISO week's candle: high = max daily close, low = min daily close, close = last close.
     - Multiplier 2, 5-candle linearly weighted ATR (weights 1..5 ÷ 15), bands (H+L)/2 ± 2 × ATR, the production ratchet, starting on the 6th candle of the spliced history.
     - Weight 0 on SELL (line on the upper band), else 1.
   - All 28 configs are listed in this step. The descriptive rows `triple` and `triple_and_pct25` are extra:
     - `triple`: weight 1 if N100, MC100 and SC100 SIGN are all BUY on the latest Master week label ≤ d.
     - `triple_and_pct25`: `triple` AND p_d ≥ 25 (W = 65).
   - A config with too little history at d (fewer than W, n, L or 250 previous closes, or no supertrend value) is undefined there. The window starts are chosen so this never happens. The engine asserts it.
5. **Targets.** At decision session d, equity weight e_d, GOLDBEES (1 − e_d)/2, LBES (1 − e_d)/2. For the cash-sleeve run: GOLDBEES 0, LBES 1 − e_d.
6. **Execution** (AGENT.md §8, with the departure below).
   - A trade happens only when the new target differs from the target last traded.
   - The trade is on the next session after d, at that session's **close**.
   - Order:
     - nav_now = Σ units × close;
     - every trim first: units −= |Δ| ÷ px, cash account += |Δ| × (1 − ow);
     - then top-ups in the order equity, GOLDBEES, LBES: v = min(Δ, cash account), units += v × (1 − ow) ÷ px.
   - ow = cost_bps ÷ 2 ÷ 10,000. Weights drift between trades.
   - **The first trade** is the build on T0's next session, at T0's target, from cash, paying the buy-side cost.
   - **The last session:** everything is sold at its close, paying the sell-side cost. A target decided on the window's last session is not traded.
7. **NAV** = cash account + Σ units × close. It is 1.0 at T0's close.
8. **Statistics.**
   - Sessions run from T0 to the last session; years = (sessions − 1) ÷ 252.
   - CAGR = NAV_end^(1/years) − 1.
   - Volatility = SD of daily returns × √252. Sharpe = CAGR ÷ volatility.
   - Maximum drawdown is on daily NAV. Calmar = CAGR ÷ |maximum drawdown|.
   - B&H = P_t ÷ P_{T0}, close to close, no cost.
9. **Null draws** for each realisation:
   - the weekly target series w_0 (at T0) … w_n is rotated by k for 8 ≤ k ≤ n+1−8;
   - each draw runs through the identical engine (steps 6–8) at the same cost.
   - `pct_w65_q25_daily` is rotated in sessions, with 40 ≤ k ≤ n+1−40.
10. **Closed-form check** (V1, at zero cost, written for the verifier):
    - Between trades the units are fixed, so V_{j+1} ÷ V_j = Σ_a w_{a,j} × close_a(e_{j+1}) ÷ close_a(e_j). Here e_j is trade session j and w_{a,j} its target weights; the window's last session closes the final period.
    - V_end = Π_j of these ratios. The verifier computes it with its own code from the raw files.
11. **MDE.**
    - For C1, the minimum detectable timing edge is 2.8 × null_sd, where null_sd is the SD of after-cost CAGR across a realisation's draws. That is the 80% power, two-sided 5% shift of a normal statistic.
    - It is printed beside every FAIL.
    - The draws come from the rule's own targets rotated, so their spread does not depend on how the rule's timing lines up with the market. **Disclosed:** the number itself did not exist when this section was written.
12. **null_sd ÷ observed_se,** beside every percentile.
    - observed_se is the Newey-West (4 lags) standard error of the mean weekly after-cost return difference between the strategy and a static mix. The static mix holds the strategy's average equity weight, rebalanced weekly at no cost, and the SE is annualised × 52.

**Point-in-time rule.**
- A decision at d reads P only up to d, and the Master SIGN only up to the latest label ≤ d.
- The trade is on d's next session.
- The engine asserts both for every decision and writes the counts to `audit.json`.

**Execution and cost: the departure from AGENT.md §8.**
- **Trades are at the next session's close, not its OHLC4.** The smallcase index series has closes only. GOLDBEES and LBES are filled at their close too, for consistency.
- Cost levels: 0 and 20.8 bps round trip, charged per side on the value traded. LBES trades pay it too.

**Effective sample size.**
- W1 has about 325 weekly decisions and W2 about 250.
- The independent units for timing are the rule's sleeve spells (counted per realisation in §4), not weeks.
- The four books share one market and are not independent.

**What the null does not control for.**
- Which weeks the sleeve is held. Gold's 2024-26 rally means any draw that sits in the sleeve in those years earns more. This is controlled by R4.
- The wrap spell.

**Known contaminants.**

| Contaminant | Handling |

| 2014-21 parents are the harness rebuild (price return, no dividends; it runs 0.5–3.3 pp/yr below the published series in 2021-26) | Not handled in levels. Rule and null use the same series, so the timing comparison is unaffected. B&H is understated by roughly the dividend yield; the rule avoids that drag while in the sleeve, a bias of about 0.3 pp/yr in its favour |
