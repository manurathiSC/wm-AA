## 1. Idea and references

**The idea in one sentence.** Every week, tag each stock the parent holds as Large, Mid, or Small and below; the **FS score** is the share of those stocks (by count) whose band's weekly supertrend is BUY (Large: Nifty 100, Mid: Midcap 100, Small and below: Smallcap 100); hold the **whole** parent basket at its listed weights while FS is at least 0.60, otherwise hold 50% GOLDBEES and 50% cash; the question is how this compares, after cost, with holding the parent and with the three-index switch (parent only when all three signals are BUY).

**Where it came from.** AA's production engine `segment_v2` decided each parent's book this way: re-tag the basket at every review, compute FS = Σ over buckets of (share of stocks in the bucket) × (1 if the bucket's index is BUY), and hold the whole book if FS ≥ 0.60 (`segment_v2.py` lines 400-430; `gate_v2.py` `compute_fs`). T03-T08 tested the three-index switch (every signal BUY) and T07 tests holding each stock by its own band. T10 tests the rule in between, as it was written, on the reviewed tag data, with the conventions and gates of T06 and T07. Manu waived a null for this test.

**The decision this will change.** Whether the FS rule is worth keeping or reinstating against the three-index switch. T10 cannot say that either rule beats chance (no null); it says how they compare on one window.

## 3. Methodology

**Data.** Every file is a row of `data/APPROVED_DATA.md` (as of 2026-11-12, status APPROVED) and is opened only through the approved-data check; the engine writes `inputs.csv` from those rows and stops on a sha256 mismatch. **The files, their data-review IDs and verdicts are those of the table in `T07.md` §3, unchanged** (D17 published NAVs, D15 for R8, D18 stock OHLC with the D14 Polyplex correction, D19 basket weights, D05 skipped baskets, D03 Master signals to 2026-10-30 which sets the window end, D20-D23 tag data, D09 GOLDBEES, D12 MCX de-spike, D11 cash and its LIQUIDCASE file for R3). The calendar is D17's dates up to 2026-10-30, so no price after 2026-10-30 is read.

**The tag rule** (`analysis/tag_lookup.py`, decided by Manu 2026-11-11, reviewed 2026-11-12), as in `T07.md` §3: the cutoff is the first membership snapshot (2026-04-09). On or after it, the tag is the band of the index the stock is listed in on the latest `index_constituents_monthly` snapshot on or before the date (Nifty 100 Large, Midcap 150 Mid, Smallcap 250 and Microcap 250 Small and below), with the rank rule as the non-member fallback; before it, the band of the stock's rank on the latest `mcap_based_universe` snapshot on or before the date (1-100 Large, 101-250 Mid, above 250 Small and below). No averaging; ISINs follow `symbology_changes`. **Caveats, to be stated on the page** (from `REVIEW.md`): (1) the market-cap history to 2025-11-28 was back-filled, so a point-in-time vintage cannot be proven; (2) the rank rule is untested before 2026-04-09 (91.4% of the parents' holding-weeks use it); its three-band agreement with real membership is 92.07% (Large recall 88.1%, Mid 79.8%, Small and below 96.5%), and on the parents' own holdings 88.9% by weight (SCMO_0003 91.1%, 0007 88.2%, 0010 80.0%, 0029 96.3%); (3) the source switches on 2026-04-09 and about 8% of stocks differ between the two; (4) isolated bad market-cap rows exist (LODHA 2021, AXISBANK 2026-10-07); (5) JSWSTEEL, VEDL, ZEEL, EMBASSY, BIRET and BAGMANE are in no basket to 2026-10-30 and would read by rank in a later one. **T10 reads tags at every weekly decision**, not only at events as T07 does.

**Construction, in order.** Steps 1 to 3 and 8 to 10 are T06 §3 and T07 §3 with the window ending 2026-10-30:

1. **Calendar.** The sessions are D17's dates, per book, from its first date T0 (2019-01-02; 0007 2019-01-04) to **2026-10-30**. The sessions D18 has and D17 lacks are never read (F-AA-D15).
2. **Decision dates.** T0, then the last D17 session of each ISO week after T0. The decision at the window's last session (2026-10-30) is made but not traded.
3. **Signals at decision date d.** The latest Master row of each of the three indices dated on or before d; its SIGN is BUY or SELL. A band is *on* if the SIGN of its index is BUY: Large reads Nifty 100, Mid reads Midcap 100, Small and below reads Smallcap 100.
4. **The FS decision at d.** The basket in force at d is the latest non-skipped basket whose availableDate is on or before d (skipped baskets are dropped). Each stock with listed weight > 0 is tagged with `tag_lookup.tag(ISIN, d)`. n = the number of stocks held; k = the number whose band is on. **FS = k ÷ n**, which equals Σ over bands of (share of stocks in the band by count) × (1 if the band's index is BUY). **The state is IN iff 5k ≥ 3n** (FS ≥ 0.60 exactly, integers; FS = 0.60 is IN), else OUT. Listed weights play no part in the decision. (R5 uses other thresholds, decided the same way: k ÷ n ≥ t as fractions; R6 uses the listed weights.)
5. **Events.** An event is a trade session on which targets are rebuilt: (i) **T0**, the initial position, free of cost: IN holds the basket in force at T0, as drifted since its availableDate and scaled to NAV 1.000 at T0's close (T06), OUT holds half GOLDBEES, half cash units, at T0's closes; (ii) a **state event**: the next D17 session after a decision date d on which the state at d differs from the state the book currently holds; (iii) a **basket event**: the first D17 session on or after a non-skipped basket's availableDate, unless the book holds no stock on that session (then nothing is traded, as in T06). Two events on one session are one event. Between events no target changes and the book drifts. The state used at a basket event is that of the latest decision made before the trade session (it is not re-evaluated against the new basket until the next weekly decision). The targets at an event on session s use the basket in force on s.
6. **Targets (T06's convention; there is no cash account).** IN: each stock gets V × w_i, where w_i is its listed weight in the basket in force, renormalised to sum to 1 (T06 divides every basket row by its row sum); GOLDBEES and cash get 0. OUT: GOLDBEES and cash each get V ÷ 2. V is the book's value at session s's OHLC4 before cost; every new position is then scaled by k_c = (V − cost) ÷ V.
7. **Execution.** At an event every instrument is moved from its current value to its target on session s at that session's OHLC4 (stocks: Eikon bar with the Polyplex correction, the close if the bar lacks an open, high or low; GOLDBEES: the de-spiked OHLC4 and the raw OHLC4 after the MCX file ends; cash: the level on that session, as-of). Cost: cost_bps ÷ 2 ÷ 10,000 per side on the sum of absolute differences between current and target values over every stock, GOLDBEES and cash; a stock whose target equals its current value costs nothing. No trade happens between events.
8. **NAV.** Σ stock units × close + GOLDBEES units × close + cash units × level, 1.000 at T0's close. No sale at the end.
9. **Statistics.** As T06 §3 step 9.
10. **Comparators** (benchmarks, same calendar, cost and T0): the **three-index switch** (IN iff all three SIGN cells are BUY; T06's strategy; the engine with decision rule AND), **hold the parent** (the engine with every stock held, at 0 cost and at each cost; and the published NAV rebased to 1.000), the **100% sleeve**, and the **constant mix** (the rule's average share of NAV in the parent's stocks over the window, held as that share in the parent with half the rest in GOLDBEES and half in cash, rebalanced at each event's session at OHLC4 with the same cost). Descriptive: T07's per-stock rule (each stock kept iff its band is on, the dropped weight to the 50/50 sleeve, tags read at events only, as `T07.md` §3 step 4 and 5), run by the same engine.
11. **Controls.** None that needs a null. V3 and V4 are the regression and exact-fact gates.

**How the test works, in plain words.**

- **The series.** Each parent's NAV is built every day from the stocks it holds (Eikon prices; the published NAV is the check). GOLDBEES and a liquid-fund cash level make up the sleeve.
- **One decision.** Every Friday, look at the stocks the parent holds, tag each Large, Mid or Small and below as of that day, read the three index signals, and count how many stocks sit in a band whose index is BUY. If at least 60% of them do, hold all of the parent (every stock at its weight); if fewer, hold half GOLDBEES and half cash. The trade is at the next session's (open + high + low + close) ÷ 4, paying 10.4 bps on every rupee traded. *[one real decision is printed in §4.0a]*
- **The result of one path.** Units of each stock, GOLDBEES and cash are fixed between events. Each day the NAV is Σ units × close. The CAGR and the drawdown come from the daily NAV.
- **The comparisons.** The same weeks, prices and costs give the parent held throughout, the switch (parent only when all three signals are BUY) and the constant mix (the same average exposure held all the time). Whatever the rule earns more than the switch it earns in the extra weeks. No random draws are involved in the four criteria. *[the stretch table is printed in §4]*
- **What the page cannot say.** With no random benchmark, a better number is a difference on this window, not proof of skill.

**Point-in-time rule.** A decision at d reads Master rows dated at or before d and tags as of d with the latest snapshot on or before d (`tag_lookup.py` never uses a later snapshot, and its self-test checks this); a trade is on a session after d. The engine asserts both for every decision, counts them, and writes the counts to `audit.json`. The Master rows before 2021 were produced afterwards by the production code and are not signals traded at the time; the market-cap history before 2025-11-28 was back-filled; both are disclosed, not corrected.

**Execution and cost.** AGENT.md R1 (next-session OHLC4, price return, 10.4 bps a side, weights drift) with the specifics of steps 6 and 7: cash trades pay the cost; the first position at T0 is free; no sale at the end; GOLDBEES uses the de-spiked fill (X02) where the MCX file covers the session, the raw OHLC4 after 2026-09-28.

**Effective sample size.** 410 weekly decisions (SCMO_0007 409); 36-57 extra weeks in 8-13 stretches (live 18-33 in 5-9). The independent unit for the difference from the switch is the stretch. No MDE is computed, because there is no null; the bootstrap interval is reported instead.

**What the comparisons control for, and what they do not.** The switch is the same engine with a different decision, so C3 and C4 isolate the extra weeks. They do not control for exposure: the rule is IN whenever the switch is IN and more often besides, so the constant mix (the same average exposure held all the time) is the check, and it is descriptive. Nothing controls for chance, for the tag data's limits below, or for gold's rally in the sleeve, which both the rule and the switch hold.
