# T10 independent verifier: the spec (Step 3)

**Written 2026-11-12 from `T10.md` §2-§3 (frozen) and `logs/evidence_contract.md`.** `AA/analysis/verify_t10.py` is written from this spec by an agent that does **not** read `AA/analysis/t10_engine.py` (if it exists), `AA/analysis/t07_engine.py`, `AA/analysis/t06_engine.py` or `AA/analysis/tag_lookup.py`, and **imports nothing from them, from `approved_data.py` or from any AA analysis module**. It may read `AA/analysis/verify_t07.py`, `verify_t06.py` and `verify_t03.py` for style and structure only (they are other verifiers, not engines). Where this spec and `T10.md` differ, `T10.md` wins.

## What it is
A re-derivation of the engine's evidence from the raw approved files, with its own code. It exits 0 only if rows were compared and none failed, writes `evidence/<run>/verify_t10.json` with counts per check, and on a deliberately corrupted copy of the evidence it exits non-zero. Run as `python -I analysis/verify_t10.py --run <name>` from `AA/`.

## Inputs it may read
Only `APPROVED` rows of `AA/data/APPROVED_DATA.md` (parse the table itself; do not import `check_approved_data.py`): the D17 extended published NAVs (`Historical Data/extended_2026-11-06/published_nav/`), D03 extended Master files, D18 OHLC (`data/holdings_matrix/<p>_ohlc.csv`) and `price_corrections.csv`, D19 weight matrices / columns / sources, D09 GOLDBEES, D12 MCX de-spike, D11 cash (and the LIQUIDCASE file for R3), **D20-D22 under `Historical Data/extended_2026-11-12/` with its own tag lookup**, D23 panels only for the V5 cross-check; plus `AA/tests/T06/evidence/headline/{daily_nav,decisions}.csv` for the regression, and the engine's evidence in `AA/tests/T10/evidence/<run>/` (`logs/evidence_contract.md` lists the files and the seeds). Read D20-D23 CSVs with only the empty string as null (`keep_default_na=False, na_values=[""]`).

## Conventions of the run (all from T06 §3 / `T10.md` §3; none from any engine)
- Calendar = D17's dates per book from its first date to 2026-10-30; decision dates = T0 then the last session of each ISO week; the signals at d = the latest Master row of each index on or before d.
- Baskets: D19 weight matrix rows, baskets flagged `csv_only_skipped` in the sources file dropped; **every basket row is divided by its row sum (weights renormalised to 1)**; a basket takes effect on the first calendar session on or after its availableDate; the basket in force at decision date d is the latest one that has taken effect on or before d.
- Stock fill = OHLC4 of the Eikon bar (close if open, high or low is blank; last close if no bar), Polyplex correction applied (`price_corrections.csv`); NAV valuation at closes (forward-filled).
- Initial position at T0 (free of cost): the basket in force at T0, drifted from its availableDate (first D18 session on or after it, OHLC4) and scaled to NAV 1.000 at T0's close; if the state at T0 is OUT, the same value is held at T0's closes and converted half to GOLDBEES, half to cash units at T0's closes.
- Targets and execution as `T10.md` §3 steps 6-8: IN, stock i gets V × w_i; OUT, GOLDBEES and cash get V ÷ 2 each; traded = Σ|target − held| over every stock, GOLDBEES and cash; cost = traded × cost_bps ÷ 2 ÷ 1e4; every new position is scaled by (V − cost) ÷ V; V is the value at the trade session's fill prices (stocks OHLC4, GOLDBEES de-spiked OHLC4, cash level). GOLDBEES fill: OHLC of D09 with open, high and low clipped to MCX gold's daily range (D12) × [1 − (range ÷ close + 0.005), 1 + (…)] around the GOLDBEES close, high and low re-bounded to include open and close, then (O+H+L+C) ÷ 4; after the MCX file's last date the raw OHLC4. Cash level: the D11 extended LBES as-of; R3 LIQUIDCASE as in T06 (LBES to the session before 2024-01-24, then chained with LIQUIDCASE returns). When a precise convention is unclear, `AA/tests/T06/T06.md` §3 and `AA/tests/T07/T07.md` §3 are the references; if still unclear, report it rather than guessing, and the check C-t06 (below) is the arbiter of the NAV conventions.
- States: the rule's state at decision j = IN iff 5k ≥ 3n. Events: T0; a **state event** on the session after decision j (j ≥ 1) whose state differs from the state the book holds; a **basket event** on the first session on or after a non-skipped basket's availableDate (session index 0 excluded) if the book is IN after taking the latest decision made before that session into account; if the book is OUT after that, nothing is traded and the new basket merely becomes the basket in force (`skipped_baskets.csv`). Two events on one session are one event. At an event the state used is that of the latest decision made before the trade session, and the targets use the basket in force at the trade session.

## Checks
1. **C-data.** Every `inputs.csv` row is an APPROVED row of the registry with a matching sha256 prefix and a through-date that covers 2026-10-30 (exempt: D19, D05, D12, which end earlier by design).
2. **C-dec.** Re-derive the weekly decisions per book and window and compare `decisions.csv`: dates, trade date, three SIGN cells and label dates, pattern, basket_eff, n_held.
3. **C-tag (your own implementation).** From D20-D22 implement the tag rule of `T10.md` §3: cutoff = first D21 snapshot; on or after it the latest D21 snapshot on or before the date, member band by index (Nifty 100 Large, Midcap 150 Mid, Smallcap 250 and Microcap 250 Small and below), non-members fall back to rank; before the cutoff the rank on the latest D20 snapshot on or before the date (ordinary shares, market cap descending, ties by quote_id, 1-100 Large, 101-250 Mid, above 250 Small and below, null market caps not ranked); ISINs resolved through quote_id (D22 `isin` and `new_isin`); not found = `Small and below`, `unranked`. The tag is read **as of the decision date itself** (no snapshot after it). Compare `decision_stocks.csv` (`band`, `tag_source`, `tag_snapshot`, `listed_weight`) for **every row**, and cross-check your lookup against the D23 panels on the V5 sample (below).
4. **C-fs.** From your own tags and signals recompute for every decision: `n_held, n_large, n_mid, n_small, k_on, fs, weight_fs, state_rule` (IN iff 5k ≥ 3n, integers), `state_switch`, `extra`, `tie`, both float readings, `float_differs`, `state_weight_fs`; compare `decisions.csv` on every row. Then: no decision has `state_rule = 0` and `state_switch = 1`; recompute `v4_exact.json`.
5. **C-events.** Re-derive the events and compare `events.csv` (`kind`, `trade_date`, `decision_date_used`, `state`, `basket_eff`, `n_held`, `sleeve_share`) and `skipped_baskets.csv`.
6. **C-nav.** Re-simulate with your own simulator the rule, the switch (IN iff pattern BBB), `parent` (always IN), `sleeve100`, and `constant_mix` (f = the average over all sessions of (value of stocks ÷ NAV) of the rule's path at the same cost; stocks f × w_i, half of (1 − f) in GOLDBEES and half in cash, rebalanced at every event of the rule, T0 free) at 0, 20.8 and 50 bps (full window, lbes), and the rule, switch, parent, sleeve100 and constant mix on the live window and with the LIQUIDCASE cash leg (20.8 bps); compare `daily_nav.csv`, `trades.csv` and `stats.csv` (CAGR, max drawdown, volatility, Ulcer index, days at least 10% below the peak, longest underwater, Calmar, with years = (sessions − 1) ÷ 252; Ulcer = √mean((100·dd)²)). End NAV within a relative 1e-9; daily NAV within 1e-9.
7. **C-thr.** The kinds `thr_2/5`, `thr_1/2`, `thr_7/10`, `thr_4/5` (state IN iff k ÷ n ≥ t as exact fractions) and `weight_fs` (IN iff weight_fs ≥ 0.60 − 1e-12): re-derive the states, events, and the end NAV and CAGR of each in `stats.csv`. `t07_rule` is **not** verified (descriptive; the verifier reports "not covered").
8. **C-t06.** With the state = IN iff all three SIGN BUY, your simulator must equal `AA/tests/T06/evidence/headline/daily_nav.csv` (`run = strategy`, same book and cost) on every shared session up to 2026-10-22 within a relative 1e-12, and your decisions equal T06's `decisions.csv` on every shared date. This validates your own simulator against an independent one.
9. **C-always-in.** Hold every stock at 0 cost: compare with the published NAV (D17) within the G0 tolerance (±0.25 pp/yr overall, ±0.5 pp in every calendar year); compare `g_engine.csv`.
10. **C-r7.** Re-derive `stretches.csv` (the runs of `extra`, the spans, the log returns of the rule and the switch at 20.8 bps over the spans from your own daily NAVs, the CAGR differences with and without the largest-|diff| stretch, as defined in the contract) and compare.
11. **C-boot.** Re-derive `bootstrap.csv` from your own daily NAVs with the seeds and procedure in the contract and compare (point estimate within 1e-9; interval ends within 1e-9).
12. **C-r4.** Re-derive R4 for the first 20 draws and 20 random draws per book (random: `default_rng([13, book_no]).choice(500, 20, replace=False)`) with the procedure and seeds in the contract (your own tags and baskets; the perturbation acts on every weekly tag read of the full window; events and NAV rebuilt), and compare `cagr`, `diff_vs_switch`, `weeks_changed`, `rank_tag_reads`, `flipped` in `r4_tag_error.csv` within 1e-9.
13. **C-v5.** The sample of 500 (stock, week) pairs per book (contract) : the engine's `band`, `tag_source` equal a direct read of the D23 panels by you (the D23 files are `tags/mcap_rank_panel.csv` and `tags/membership_panel.csv`; work out their layout from the files); report agreement. The number of weekly tag reads (`decision_stocks.csv` rows per book and window) equals Σ n_held over the decisions.
14. **C-pit.** Every decision uses Master rows dated on or before the decision date, every tag a snapshot on or before the decision date, every trade is on a later session than its decision (T0 excepted: decision and trade on the same session, counted separately); count and report violations (must be 0 out of a count > 0).
15. **C-corrupt.** Make a corrupted copy of the evidence in scratch space (flip a tag band, flip a `state_rule`, change a trade's cost, scale a NAV, delete an event, change a stretch's span) and require that the relevant checks fail and the script exits non-zero on it.

## Output
`verify_t10.json`: for every check the rows compared, the failures and the worst difference. Exit 0 only if every check compared at least one row and none failed. While no engine evidence exists, test the verifier on a mock evidence set you build yourself in scratch space from your own simulator (as T07's verifier was developed), and say so in your log (`logs/step_3_log.md`). When the engine's `smoke` or `headline` evidence appears under `AA/tests/T10/evidence/`, run on it and report.
