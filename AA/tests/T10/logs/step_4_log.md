## What each does
- **analyse.py** reads only the evidence (plus, for the worked example only, the approved OHLC file D18 and `price_corrections.csv`, read through its own APPROVED-row and sha256 check). Gates: V1 from `verify_t10.json` (missing = not satisfied; needs `exit_ok`, no failures, compared > 0, and every planted corruption `detected`); V2 from `audit.json` and **re-derived** (Master label dates, tag snapshots, trade after decision, event decision before trade; 43,326 checks on smoke4); V3 re-derived (switch / parent / 100% sleeve NAV against T06's evidence at 0, 20.8, 50 bps, relative 1e-12, decisions against T06's, G0 from `g_engine.csv`); V4 re-derived from `decisions.csv` and compared with `v4_exact.json` (rule never OUT while the switch is IN, FS = k/n, state = 5k >= 3n, float-formula disagreements listed); V5 (tag reads = sum of stocks held, engine count, the verifier's C-v5). Consistency checks K1-K5 (stats against daily_nav, bootstrap and stretches against stats, invested_weeks against decisions, stretches against audit, R4 draw count = `--expect-r4`, default 500). Exit 1 on any failed gate, and the log then holds only the gate block and says nothing below may be read. It also compares the evidence's weekly counts with Step 1's frozen counts (`logs/step_1_counts.json`: IN weeks, extra weeks, stretches and lengths, ties, flips; all agree on smoke4). Blocks written to `analysis.log`: 4.0a worked example (SCMO_0003, the first extra week with a state trade: 2019-03-08, pattern SBB, FS 9/10, trade 2019-03-11, every stock's OHLC4 fill, units, cost, the sum of units x close reproducing the NAV after the trade to 0.0e+00, NAV around it with the switch beside it, the path's CAGR and drawdown with peak and trough dates), 4.0b gates and reportability, 4.1 headline C1-C4 per book with the 3-of-4 verdict and every series (rule, switch, parent, constant mix, 100% sleeve, published parent, T07 rule labelled descriptive and unverified) with CAGR, max DD, Calmar, vol, Ulcer, days >= 10% below, longest underwater, trades, cost, share IN, 4.2 bootstrap intervals and per-year returns, 4.3 stretch table (R7, both windows, with the 'without the largest' CAGR difference), signal patterns and FS, tag sources, 4.4 verdict and outcome-map rows, 4.5 R1 (0 and 50 bps, trades and cost), R2, R3, R4, R5, R6, R8. "no chance benchmark was run" is in every headline title; no sentence says the rule beats chance.
- **fill_writeup.py** writes the generated block between the §4 markers from `evidence/<run>/analysis.log`. It loads `check_prereg.py`'s own `region()` function, requires the frozen region's hash to equal the header's, writes only if the hash, every byte before `## 4. Results` and every byte from `## 5. Findings` on are unchanged, then reads the file back and repeats the checks (restoring the original bytes if they fail). Tested on a copy of T10.md: §1-§3 and §5-§8 byte-identical (`diff`), second run idempotent, `check_prereg.py` OK on the real file, refuses (exit 1) when a frozen line is altered, when the heading is missing, or when the log lacks a block or reports a failed gate. `T10.md` itself is untouched (`git status` shows it clean).
- **report.py** writes `evidence/<run>/report.html` (1.1 MB, self-contained, light and dark): answer box with the verdicts and the no-null statement, data (inputs, windows, the five tag caveats read from T10.md §3), the worked example, headline tables, hoverable growth and drawdown charts per book (rule, switch, parent, constant mix, published parent, every curve with its start and end dates), the FS-by-week chart with the 0.60 line, the three signal strips, both states and the shaded extra weeks, the stretch bar chart (rule minus switch per stretch), the R4 histograms, all detail and robustness tables, the gates, definitions, and what the page cannot say. Every number is a span `data-k=PATH data-f=FORMAT` into the embedded `page-data` (= `analysis.json`). It refuses to build if a gate failed.
- **check_page.py** (38,998 checks, 0 failures on smoke4, about 5 s): recomputes `page-data` with its own code from the evidence, T06's evidence, the approved OHLC file and the D23 panels and compares leaf by leaf; checks every span's text against its formatted value; recomputes the chart arrays and decodes every polyline and rectangle in the SVGs against them; re-runs the worked example (OHLC4 fills, units, cost, NAV after the trade, the tag of every example stock against the D23 rank panel); recomputes the 1,000-resample bootstrap of every book from daily_nav with the contract's seeds (equals `bootstrap.csv`), every stretch's dates, weeks, mean FS and log returns and the 'without the largest' CAGR differences; recounts V2-V5; checks every date on the page against the evidence, T10.md and the approved-data file; checks the no-null sentences. `--selftest` plants 11 corruptions (a span text, a page-data number, an example fill, a gate flag, a growth-line hover value, an FS value, a stretch bar, a histogram count, a date, and two corrupted evidence copies: a NAV day scaled, a decision's k changed); all 11 detected (1 to 9 failures each).
- **rerun_draw.py** runs `python -I analysis/t10_engine.py --rerun --book B --id N --out f.json` and compares `cagr`, `diff_vs_switch` (relative 1e-12) and `weeks_changed` (exact) with the row of `r4_tag_error.csv`. Tested: equal (exit 0, draw 3 and 17 of smoke4, difference 0.0), a tampered CAGR (exit 1, DIFFERENT), a draw not in the evidence (exit 2), a missing engine (exit 3). The engine's `--rerun`, untested at Step 2, works.
- **run.sh** (`bash tests/T10/run.sh [RUN]`, default `headline`, log `tests/T10/logs/run_sh.log`): check_approved_data, check_prereg, engine (`--r4 $R4`, default 500), verifier (stops if `analysis/verify_t10.py` is missing unless `T10_ALLOW_NO_VERIFIER=1`, and even then analyse fails V1 without a `verify_t10.json`), analyse (`--expect-r4 $R4`), fill_writeup, check_prereg, report, check_page, rerun_draw (SCMO_0003 draw 17); `set -euo pipefail`, so it stops at the first non-zero exit. ## Places where the frozen text or the contract is silent, and what I did
1. **The worked example's trade, GOLDBEES and cash.** The evidence has no per-instrument GOLDBEES fill or cash level, so the example prints the stock fills (from D18) and states that the sleeve is sold in full (traded = 2V, which the evidence confirms); the GOLDBEES and cash fills are not shown.
2. **`C4` ties.** `>=` is read with a relative tolerance of 1e-12 on the Calmar values; C1-C3 are strict comparisons of the stored floats.
3. **Non-reportable books.** As in T07: a book that fails the 5-stretch gate counts as not passing C3 or C4 and is named.
4. **'R7 says one stretch carries the difference'** has no number in the frozen text. The scripts report both CAGR differences and whether the sign flips when the largest stretch is removed, add an outcome-map line only if the sign flips in 2 or more books, and say so. This is an operational reading, stated as such in the log and on the page; the stretch table is the evidence.
5. **Share of time IN** = IN weekly decisions / weekly decisions (rule, switch, parent, T07 rule); the constant mix shows its f; the 100% sleeve 0.
6. **Per-year returns** use the last session of each calendar year (first year from the base 1.000 at T0, last year to 2026-10-30).
7. **Live-window daily NAVs are not in the evidence** (`daily_nav.csv` is full window only), so R2 is shown from `stats.csv` only and no live-window chart exists; the live-window stretch rows and CAGR differences are taken from `stretches.csv` (the verifier covers them: C-r7). `check_page.py` recomputes the full-window stretches from NAVs and checks the live rows against `stats.csv` only.
8. The page is written to `evidence/<run>/report.html` (as instructed), whereas the T10.md header says `evidence/report.html`; `METHODS.md` (T10.md header) is not written by this step.
9. Engine/contract observations (none blocks a gate): the engine's own event counts exceed Step 1's coarse bound by one for SCMO_0029 (already in the Step 2 log); `trades.csv` has no GOLDBEES/cash rows; `stats.csv` carries `cost_bps` = 20.8 on `published_parent` in the live block and empty on `published_aa` (read correctly by the scripts).
