## 1. Idea and references

**The idea in one sentence.** Holding each real parent when **at least two** of the weekly supertrends of the Nifty 100, Midcap 100 and Smallcap 100 are BUY, and otherwise a GOLDBEES and cash sleeve, (a) chooses *when* to hold the parent better than random in-or-out timings that keep the rule's switch count and weeks invested (with a fixed 50/50 sleeve, Part A, and with a sleeve split by the MAUc1 gold supertrend, Part B), and (b) times the gold-versus-cash split better than random gold timings that keep the gold signal's switch count and BUY weeks (Part B), on 2014-09-01 → 2019-01-02.

**Where it came from.** T11 (COMPLETE 2026-11-17) ran T06's rule (fixed 50/50 sleeve) and T08's rule (MAUc1-timed sleeve) on the four real parents' own stocks over 2014-09-01 → 2019-01-02, holding the parent only when all three index signals were BUY. All six criteria failed and the rule earned far less than holding the parent (it was OUT 59% of weeks through the 2014–2017 small-cap rally). On 2026-11-18 Manu asked for the same test with the entry loosened to **at least two of the three** BUY. Counted before this section was written, the 2-of-3 rule differs from all-3 in 22 of the 228 weeks, all of them weeks in which exactly two indices are BUY (2-of-3 IN, all-3 OUT); there is no week in which all-3 is IN and 2-of-3 OUT.

**The decision this will change.** Whether a looser entry turns T11's verdict on this window (no timing edge over chance) into one, i.e. whether the all-3 requirement, rather than the switch itself, is what failed in 2014–2018. A pass is weak evidence only: the rule variant was chosen after T11's result on the same weeks, the window is short, and the rebuilt parent NAV before 2019 has no published series to check against. A pass would call for a pre-registered run of the 2-of-3 rule on 2019–2026 (where all-3 passed in T06/T08) before anything changes in production.

**What this test is NOT asking.**
- Whether the parents' stock selection helps or hurts (T07's and T12's question), or whether some other rule, signal, sleeve, weighting or threshold would do better. There is one rule per part and no search; 2-of-3 was named by Manu, not chosen among thresholds by a result.
- Whether 2-of-3 beats all-3 in a statistical sense. T11's all-3 rule is run beside it as a descriptive comparator on the same prices and costs; no null is built for the difference.
- Whether the rebuilt parent NAV is the published one. No published NAV exists before 2019-01-02, so nothing in this test can check it (§3 contaminants); the rebuild code is the one that matches the published NAV to 0.3 pp a year over 2019–2026.
- Whether the rule beats holding the parent. That is a comparator (§3), not a null.

- Whether gold goes up when MAUc1 is BUY. C3 and C4 ask whether the rule's particular timing of the split beats random timing with the same number of switches and BUY weeks, as seen through the rule's own OUT weeks.

**Robustness null F5 (both random):** an F1 draw of the in-or-out states with an F3 draw of the gold states, independent. Reported only (R5).

**The rule is inside every allowed set.** Its own lengths lie within the bounds by construction, so every N4.9 draw is one configuration of N4.10 and every N4.9-g draw one configuration of N4.10-g. Gate V3 checks both.

**Which null decides.** F1 decides A1, A2, C1 and C2; F3 decides C3 and C4. F2 and F4 are reported beside them. If a pair disagrees, both are reported in the verdict and the finding's status note says so (AGENT.md N6.8).

**Statistic the null is judged on.** After-cost CAGR for A1, C1 and C3, higher better. Maximum drawdown on daily NAV for A2, C2 and C4, shallower better.
- **Unit:** one realisation is part × book × cost × null. The percentile is computed per realisation against its own draws.
- **Percentile:** (draws worse + 0.5 × draws tied) ÷ draws × 100. Ties are equal within a relative 1e-12.
- **p-value:** (1 + draws at least as good) ÷ (draws + 1). It is reported beside the percentile; the pass mark is on the percentile.
- **Secondary statistics**, each with its own percentile against the same draws: volatility of daily returns × √252 (lower better); Ulcer index (lower better); days at least 10% below the running peak (fewer better); longest time under water (shorter better); Calmar = CAGR ÷ |maximum drawdown|.

**Pass mark and draws.** The percentile must be at least 90. 2,000 draws give a resolution of 0.05 points and a smallest p-value of 1/2001; (D + 1) ≥ m ÷ α is met for the six criteria (m = 6, α = 0.10).

**Construction, in order.** Steps 1 to 12 are T11 §3 unchanged except step 3 (the entry rule), the counts and seeds of step 11, and the all-3 comparator of step 10:

1. **Calendar.** The sessions are D26's dates from T0 = 2014-09-01 to **2019-01-02**. 2. **Decision dates.** T0, then the last D26 session of each ISO week after T0. T0 is the window's base. T0 (2014-09-01) is a Monday: the basket's decision date (2014-08-31) is a Sunday and the first signal read is the last Master row on or before 2014-09-01. The decision at the window's last session (2019-01-02, a Wednesday) is made but not traded.
3. **Signals at decision date d.** Read the latest Master row of each of the three indices dated on or before d (Smallcap 100: dated 2011-11-25 or later). **The decision is IN if SIGN is BUY on at least two of the three, else OUT** (T11: IN only if all three are BUY). The engine also records the all-3 state of every decision, for the comparator and gate V4(a1). Read the latest MAUc1 Master row dated on or before d (D24b); g_d = BUY or SELL. A week whose Master row is dated after its last D26 session uses the previous week's row, one week stale and never ahead of d. Every row is a backfill by the production code.
4. **Targets.** IN: 100% the parent's basket in force, at the listed weights renormalised to sum 1 as T06 and T08 do (raw row sums 0.9996–1.0005). OUT, Part A: 50% GOLDBEES and 50% cash. OUT, Part B: **w_d of the sleeve in GOLDBEES and 1 − w_d in cash, w_d = 0.8 if g_d is BUY and 0.2 if g_d is SELL.** The two parts share the draws of the in-or-out nulls; Part A is the engine's `fixed50` mode.
5. **Initial position.** NAV is 1.000 at T0's close; the book holds at that close what the decision at T0 says (T0's basket is the one effective on 2014-09-01; the rule says IN at T0, so the book holds that basket; Part B: the sleeve at w_{T0} when OUT). No cost is charged for this initial position, in any realisation including the nulls.
6. **Execution.** As T08 step 6 with the D26 calendar: a trade happens only when the target changes, on the **next D26 session after d**, at that session's OHLC4 of each instrument (stocks: Eikon bar with the Polyplex correction; if the bar lacks an open, high or low the close is used; GOLDBEES: the de-spiked OHLC4, high and low clipped to MCX gold's range that day, scaled, plus 0.5%, with the MCX range read from D24b's daily file; cash: the level on that session, as-of). Cost: ow = cost_bps ÷ 2 ÷ 10,000 per side on every value traded (stock, GOLDBEES, cash). The target changes in three ways, as T08: **exit** (IN → OUT), **entry** (OUT → IN), **sleeve rebalance** (Part B, OUT on both sides, g changes). A flip of g while IN is not a trade; the split is read at the next exit decision. A decision that is both an exit and a flip is one exit trade at the new g.
7. **Parent rebalances.** A basket takes effect on the first D26 session on or after its `availableDate`. While IN, at that session's OHLC4 the old basket is sold and the new one bought, with cost on the value traded (18 changes after T0; none skipped). While OUT, no trade happens but the current basket is recorded. The basket effective 2018-12-17 is in force to the window's end. A basket-change session that is also an exit or an entry session is that one trade.
8. **NAV.** Cash account + Σ stock units × close + GOLDBEES units × close + cash units × level. It is 1.000 at T0's close. No sale at the end.
9. **Statistics.** Sessions run from T0 to the last session. years = (sessions − 1) ÷ 252. CAGR = NAV_end^(1/years) − 1. Maximum drawdown, Ulcer index, days at least 10% below the running peak, longest time under water (sessions), volatility = SD of daily returns × √252, Calmar = CAGR ÷ |maximum drawdown|.
10. **Comparators** (benchmarks, not nulls), on the same calendar, cost and T0: hold the parent (the engine with the decision fixed at IN, at 0 cost and at each cost; there is no published NAV to compare with before 2019); the rule with the sleeve fixed at 50/50 (A1's rule); the constant mix with a 50/50 non-stock share and the constant mix with a MAUc1-timed non-stock share (f = invested weeks ÷ weeks held in the parent, rebalanced at each decision date's next session at OHLC4 with the same cost, including after T0's decision); the 100% sleeve at 50/50 held throughout and the 100% sleeve timed by MAUc1; **the all-3 rule (T11's rule: IN only if all three are BUY) with the 50/50 and with the MAUc1-timed sleeve, at each cost.**
11. **Null draws.** From the in-or-out series s_0 … s_n (n = 227), cut maximal spells: 9 in-spells and 9 out-spells. From the gold series g_0 … g_n, cut maximal spells: 10 BUY spells and 9 SELL spells.
    - **N4.9 spell shuffle of the in-or-out series (F2):** shuffle the in-spells among themselves and the out-spells among themselves with `numpy.random.default_rng([16, 3, draw_id])` (draw_id = 0 … 1,999), interleave starting in the rule's first state (IN).
    - **N4.10 free-length draw of the in-or-out series (F1):** k_in = 9, S_in = 115, bounds [2, 30]; k_out = 9, S_out = 113, bounds [2, 33]. Let c(m, t) be the number of ways to write t as m integers in [lo, hi]: c(0, 0) = 1, c(0, t) = 0 for t > 0, c(m, t) = Σ_{x = lo..hi} c(m − 1, t − x). The parts are drawn one at a time: with m parts and t weeks left, P(length = x) ∝ c(m − 1, t − x) for x in [lo, hi]. This is exactly uniform over the c(k, S) ordered tuples, with no draw rejected. *Worked example (k = 3, S = 12, bounds [2, 6], 19 tuples):* the first part is 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 with probabilities 3, 4, 5, 4, 3 over 19. The uniform number comes from `rng.random()` and a cumulative-sum search (never `Generator.choice`), from `numpy.random.default_rng([16, 6, draw_id])`, in-spell lengths first and then out-spell lengths from the same generator. The tuples are exactly 120,432,707,298 (in) and 144,224,745,588 (out); the engine prints the counts for every window.
    - **N4.9-g spell shuffle of the gold series (F4):** shuffle the BUY spells among themselves and the SELL spells among themselves with `numpy.random.default_rng([16, 11, 3, draw_id])`, interleave starting in the real first gold state (BUY).
    - **N4.10-g free-length draw of the gold series (F3):** the same sampler with k_buy = 10, S_buy = 132, bounds [2, 36]; k_sell = 9, S_sell = 96, bounds [2, 19]; BUY lengths first, then SELL lengths, from `numpy.random.default_rng([16, 11, 6, draw_id])`; interleaved starting in BUY. The bounds are the real series' shortest spell (2) and longest BUY and SELL spells (36, 19), computed before any return was read. The tuples are exactly 6,835,192,844,305 (BUY) and 4,976,743,356 (SELL). The gold tables are T11's (same gold series); the seeds differ. The seeds' first element 16 is used by no earlier test.
    - **F5:** an F1 draw (seed `[16, 6, draw_id]`) and an F3 draw (seed `[16, 11, 6, draw_id]`) of the same draw id, run together.
    - The R3 window has its own spells, totals and bounds from the same seeds: in [2, 30] (k 7, S 101) and out [2, 33] (k 8, S 109), starting OUT; BUY [2, 36] (k 9, S 130), SELL [2, 15] (k 9, S 80), starting SELL; the tuples are exactly 271,511,233, 8,485,744,608, 426,468,399,525 and 644,288,463.

For A2, C2 and C4 it is 2.8 × the SD of maximum drawdown. **How the test works, in plain words.**

- **The series.** The book is one of the four real parents. Every Friday three weekly index signals are read. If at least two of the three say BUY, the book holds the parent's basket in force at its listed weights (T11 needed all three); otherwise it holds a sleeve of GOLDBEES and cash. In Part A the sleeve is always half and half. In Part B a fourth signal, gold's own weekly supertrend (MAUc1), sets the split: 80% GOLDBEES and 20% cash when it says BUY, 20% and 80% when it says SELL, changed only when that signal changes. A switch is made on the next session at the (open + high + low + close) ÷ 4 of each instrument, paying 10.4 bps on every rupee traded; while the book holds the parent it also follows each of the parent's 18 basket changes. *[the real signals, trades and NAVs are printed in §4.0a]*
- **One decision.** On a Friday, look up the latest weekly row of the three index Master files and of the MAUc1 Master file. If at least two of the three index SIGN cells are BUY, the target is the parent's basket; otherwise the target is the sleeve at the split the sleeve rule gives. The trade, if the target changed, is on the next session. *[printed in §4.0a]*
- **The result of one path.** Units of each stock, GOLDBEES and cash are fixed between trades. Each day the NAV is Σ units × close. The CAGR and drawdown come from that daily NAV. *[printed in §4.0a]*
- **One draw of the in-or-out nulls (F1, F2).** The real path is 18 spells: 9 in and 9 out, starting in. F2 keeps the 18 lengths and shuffles which in-spell goes where and which out-spell goes where. F1 keeps only the *number* of spells and the *total* weeks in and out, and picks nine new in-spell lengths between 2 and 30 weeks that add up to 115 and nine new out-spell lengths between 2 and 33 weeks that add up to 113, every allowed set equally likely. In Part B the real gold signal is then read in whatever weeks the draw is OUT.
- **One draw of the gold nulls (F3, F4).** The real gold signal is 19 spells: 10 BUY and 9 SELL. F4 shuffles which BUY spell goes where and which SELL spell goes where. F3 keeps the number of spells and the total BUY and SELL weeks, and picks ten BUY lengths between 2 and 36 weeks adding up to 132 and nine SELL lengths between 2 and 19 weeks adding up to 96, every allowed set equally likely, starting BUY. The real in-or-out switch is kept, so the book still holds the parent in exactly the rule's weeks, and only the split of its sleeve moves. The first 20 weeks of a real, an F2, an F1, an F4 and an F3 series are printed in §4.0a.
- **The ranking.** The rule's CAGR is placed among the 2,000 draws' CAGRs: the percentile is the share of draws it beats (ties count half); the p-value is the chance of doing as well by luck. 90 or more is a pass.
- **The comparators.** Holding the parent is what the book would do with no rule. The same rule with the 50/50 sleeve is Part A's rule, so the gap between the two is the sleeve's effect. The constant mixes hold the same average share in the parent all the time: they show how much of any gain is only a different average exposure. The all-3 rule is T11's rule on the same prices and costs: the gap between the two shows what the looser entry changed in money terms; it is not tested against chance.

**Point-in-time rule.** A decision at d reads Master rows (the three indices and MAUc1) dated at or before d only (step 3). A trade is on a session after d. The engine asserts both for every decision, counts them, and writes the counts to `audit.json`. The Master rows were produced afterwards by the production code and are not signals traded at the time, and no MAUc1 row was traded by the desk; this is disclosed, not corrected.

**Execution and cost.** AGENT.md R1 (next-session OHLC4, price return, 10.4 bps a side, weights drift between rebalances) with these specifics: cash trades pay the cost; the first position at T0 is free; no sale at the end; a stock bar without an open, high or low is filled at the close; the GOLDBEES fill is de-spiked as T03; sleeve rebalances and parent rebalances pay the cost on the value traded.

**Effective sample size and power.** 9 round trips of the in-or-out switch and 8 sleeve trades, shared by the four books; 228 weekly decisions over 4.3 years. **What the nulls control for, and what they do not.** F1 and F2 control for the fraction of weeks in the parent and the number of switches (F2 also for every spell's length). F3 and F4 control for the number of gold switches, the fraction of weeks BUY, and the whole in-or-out series. They do not control for how many of the drawn flips fall in OUT weeks, nor for the persistence of the gold signal. No null controls for the backfill, the unvalidated rebuild, or for the 80/20 weights being fixed after earlier looks. R6 and the comparators bound the sleeve's effect.

## 6. What this does not establish

**Tested and inconclusive:** whether the 2-of-3 switch has an edge below about 12.5 to 20.8 pp a year of CAGR on the real parents in 2014-09 → 2019-01; whether the MAUc1 timing of the sleeve has skill below about 3.4 to 3.7 pp.
**Not tested at all:** whether 2-of-3 is better or worse than all-3 in a statistical sense (the gap in §4.2 is descriptive; eight differing stretches); how 2-of-3 does on 2019–2026, where the all-3 rule passed C1 and C2 (T06, T08) and the production FS ≥ 0.60 rule (T10) lagged it; any other threshold or signal combination (no search was run, and running one on this window would overfit it); the oracle, negative-control and planted-effect controls of AGENT.md N6.7.
