# T14 evidence contract (written 2026-11-23, Step 2)

What `analysis/t14_engine.py` writes, so that the verifier (Step 3) and the test scripts (Step 4) can read it without opening the engine. One run folder `tests/T14/evidence/<run>/` holds one sub-folder per window, `w1/` (2014-09-01 → 2019-01-02) and `w2/` (2019-01-02 → 2026-10-30). A short run (`--quick`, `--draws N`) writes the same files with fewer draws (and the first book only). **Names inside a window**: `window` = `full` (the window), `r3` (R3: W1 from 2015-01-02; W2's live window from 2021-11-01, called `live` in T08's stored files); `cash` = `lbes` or `liq` (R2).

**Rules** (T14.md §2.1), run ids used everywhere: `50-orig`, `50-half`, `50-liq`, `80-orig`, `80-half`, `80-liq`. Target `(e, gw)` per decision: e = parent share, gw = GOLDBEES share of the non-parent part. Regime codes: 0 IN, 1 OUT-BUY, 2 OUT-SELL.

## Engine behaviour (readings, also in `audit.json` → `readings`)

1. **Unchanged from T11/T08**: the trade function (cost on the value traded at the pre-cost targets, then every target scaled by `(V − cost)/V`), the samplers (copied verbatim, same seeds, bounds from the series), the statistics, the calendar, signals and fills of each window (T14.md §3 table). A trade is due on the session after decision j (j ≥ 1) iff `e[j] != e[j-1] or (e[j] < 1 and gw[j] != gw[j-1])` and that session exists.
2. **`-half` and `-liq` only**: the trade that moves into OUT-SELL is `regime_in`, out of it `regime_out` (even when e does not change, as in `-liq` from OUT-BUY). A parent rebalance (basket's first session on or after its availableDate, not a session of another trade) while `0 < e < 1` is a **partial** `basket_change` (D1): `stock_after = stock_before − cost`, GOLDBEES and cash units unchanged, cost = bps/2/1e4 × Σ|S·w_new − held| on the stock lines, units_i = (S − cost)·w_i/price_i. While e = 1 it is T08's full reset trade; while e = 0 nothing is traded. The constant-mix comparators always use the full reset.
3. **Initial position** at T0's close, free: the target of the decision at T0; IN holds the basket in force at T0 bought at the OHLC4 of the first D18 session on or after its availableDate, scaled so NAV = 1 at T0's close; OUT holds `gw` GOLDBEES and the rest cash.
4. **W2 R3 (`live`)** uses the book's FULL-window bounds (T08 E13); **W1 R3** its own (T11 E11).
5. **Draw sampling for NAV checks**: the engine stores curves for the ids above only; `draws.csv` has every draw's statistics.
