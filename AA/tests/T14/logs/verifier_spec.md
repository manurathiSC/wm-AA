## 1. What to re-derive, per window, from the raw files (own code)

Windows and inputs are T14.md §3's table. W1: D26 calendar, D24b MAUc1 (`Master_MAUc1.xlsx`, weekly csv, `mauc1_daily_clean_extended.csv` as MCX range), seeds `[14, 3|6, id]`, `[14, 11, 3|6, id]`, T0 2014-09-01, END 2019-01-02, R3 from 2015-01-02 with its own bounds. W2: each book's own D17 dates (extended published NAV) from its first date to END = last row of the three D03 Master files (2026-10-30), D24 MAUc1 (`extended_2026-11-06/MAUc1/Master_MAUc1.xlsx`; weekly csv same folder), MCX range D12 (`analysis/evidence/final_strategy/mauc1/mauc1_daily_clean.csv`; raw OHLC4 where no MCX bar), seeds `[3|6, id]`, `[11, 3|6, id]`, R3 ("live") from 2021-11-01 with its own spells but the full-window bounds, R2 LIQUIDCASE from 2024-01-24 chained onto LBES (see `verify_t08.py`'s reading). Everything else (OHLC4 fills, de-spiked GOLDBEES, Polyplex correction, cash as-of, baskets at listed weights renormalised, skipped baskets dropped, last-close stand-ins, the cost rule) is as in `verify_t11.py` / `verify_t08.py`.

Every row of `draws.csv` against the draw's states (tags, spell counts and lengths, gold columns, `out_sell_weeks`, `invested_weeks`, `sessions_invested`, `trades_due`).
5. **NAVs of draws**: re-simulate the **first 20 and 20 random draw ids** (own seed, not [17, 1]) of every row group (family × cost × cash/window) for **all six rules** in each book and compare every statistic in `draws.csv`; compare the 20.8 bps NAV with `draw_curves_20.8.npz` for the stored ids (key `<book>|<F1|F3>|<rule>|<id>`).

7. **Regime ledger** (`regime_ledger.csv`): recompute spells, spans, parent / GOLDBEES / cash returns and each rule's return.
8. **Counts and reportability**: `logs/step_1_spell_counts.json` against the verifier's own regime, spell, round-trip and trade counts for both windows, every book, R3 windows; tuple counts by inclusion–exclusion vs DP.
9. **V2**: labels ≤ decision date, trades after decisions, basket changes on availableDate sessions, Smallcap 100 rows ≥ 2011-11-25.

12. **Engine flags**: `audit.json` gates, `regression.json` pass flags, V2 counts > 0 and violations 0.

Tolerances: NAVs and statistics relative 1e-9 (V1); V4 regression 1e-12; identities 1e-12; float32-free (the curves are float64 here).

## 2. Self-test (the corrupted copy)

A copy of the evidence with deliberate corruptions, each caught by its own check, the verifier exiting non-zero on the copy: a regime week flipped in `decisions.csv` (a `-half` target changed); a `-half` basket trade in `trades.csv` replaced by a full reset to 50/50 (units changed); a `-liq` regime week with GOLDBEES units > 0 in `positions.csv` / a trade's `gold_after` > 0; a NAV column scaled by 1.001 in `daily_nav.csv`; a draw state out of bounds in `draw_states.npz` (N4.10 and N4.10-g); a draw's `cagr` altered in `draws.csv`; one trade removed from `trades.csv`; a ledger return altered. Keep the T11 verifier's corruption mechanics where they apply.
