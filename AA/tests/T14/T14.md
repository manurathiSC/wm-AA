## 1. Idea and references

**The idea in one sentence.** In the weeks when the weekly three-index switch (Nifty 100, Midcap 100, Smallcap 100 supertrends) is OUT **and** the weekly MAUc1 gold supertrend is SELL — the **OUT-SELL regime** — holding either (variant `-half`) **50% of the parent's basket and 50% GOLDBEES**, the parent half following the parent's rebalances, or (variant `-liq`) **100% of the liquid ETF (cash leg)**, instead of the original sleeve, changes what the three-index switch earns: its CAGR, its worst fall, and the parts of those that are timing rather than exposure; tried on both base sleeves (50/50 = T06/T11 Part A, 80/20 = T08/T11 Part B) on the two windows where the switch has been tested on the real parents.

**Where it came from.** Manu, chat 2026-11-23: "the changes that I want to make are based on the regimes where the three-regime switch is also in sell, and gold is also in sell. In those cases, I want to test two things: 1. To keep 50/50, both in the portfolio and in gold. 2. iteration is basically not keeping both of those and keeping just the liquid ETF." Asked what "50/50" meant, he answered: "[50% parent and 50% GOLDBEES] ... Plus any rebalances that the parent might be having, we will just trap the parent equities with 50% of the weight in this regime." and "Do two iterations here, both 50/50 gold and cash, and 80/20 gold and cash." and asked for a new test folder whose plan follows the framework so any agent can work on it. His "execute T14 plan" (chat 2026-11-23) approved the plan and, with it, the defaults of its decision points D1–D4 (§2).

**The decision this will change.** What the book should hold in the OUT-SELL regime: the original sleeve (50/50 or 20% GOLDBEES / 80% cash), half parent + half GOLDBEES, or the liquid ETF alone. The answer is **descriptive first** (what each choice earned over the same weeks, prices and costs, as a difference from the original rule, with the spell-by-spell ledger) and **then against chance** (criteria E1, E2, G1, G2, and the paired contrast R6). A pass is at most PROVISIONAL: the variants were proposed after seeing the results of the original rule on both windows.

### 2.1 The six rules

A target is `(e, gw)`: `e` = share of the book in the parent's basket, `gw` = share of the non-parent part in GOLDBEES, the rest in cash (the LBES liquid ETF series).

| Rule id | Base sleeve | IN | OUT-BUY | **OUT-SELL** | Is |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `50-orig` | 50/50 | e=1 | e=0, gw=0.5 | e=0, gw=0.5 | T06 / T11 Part A (comparator, regression) |
| `50-half` | 50/50 | e=1 | e=0, gw=0.5 | **e=0.5, gw=1.0** | variant 1, 50/50 base |
| `50-liq` | 50/50 | e=1 | e=0, gw=0.5 | **e=0, gw=0.0** | variant 2, 50/50 base |

### 2.2 Hypotheses, nulls and criteria

For each window w in {W1, W2} and each **new** rule r in {`50-half`, `50-liq`, `80-half`, `80-liq`}, four statements, judged per book on the after-cost (20.8 bps) percentile against the rule's own null draws (the variant target applied in each draw's own OUT-SELL weeks):

| # | Claim about rule r | Statistic | Deciding null | Randomised / real |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | the switch's in-or-out timing beats random in-or-out timing that keeps its switch count and weeks invested | CAGR | F1: N4.10 random in-or-out | in-or-out random; gold real |
| E2 | its worst fall is shallower than those random timings' | maximum drawdown | F1 | as above |
| G1 | placing the regime target in the gold signal's SELL weeks (within the real OUT weeks) beats placing it in random gold weeks that keep the gold signal's switch count and BUY weeks | CAGR | F3: N4.10-g random gold timing | in-or-out real; gold random |
| G2 | worst fall shallower | maximum drawdown | F3 | as above |

**Pass for each criterion:** percentile ≥ 90 in at least 3 of the 4 books. 4 rules × 4 criteria = **16 criteria per window, 32 in all**. Beside, not criteria: F2 (N4.9) next to F1, F4 (N4.9-g) next to F3; F5 (both random) is robustness R5. One null per timing (AGENT.md N2, N4.11). The null tables are T11's (W1) and T08's (W2) with one added row: "target in the OUT-SELL regime: the variant's, held in the rule and in every draw". **The `-orig` rules are not re-judged**: their percentiles are T08's and T11's (reproduced exactly) and are shown for reference.

- **Percentile** = (draws worse + 0.5 × ties) ÷ draws × 100, ties within relative 1e-12 ("worse" = lower CAGR, deeper drawdown); **p** = (1 + draws at least as good) ÷ (draws + 1), printed beside it; 2,000 draws per book, cost, rule and null. Secondary statistics as T08/T11 (volatility, Ulcer index, days ≥ 10% under peak, longest under water, Calmar), each with its own percentile.
- **MDE** = 2.8 × the null's SD of CAGR (or of maximum drawdown), printed beside every FAIL. It is computed after the run from the draws and does not enter any threshold.
- **Reportability:** a realisation is NOT REPORTABLE if its window has fewer than 4 in-or-out round trips (W1 has 8, W2 9) or fewer than 4 OUT-SELL spells (both have 8), or if a gold table cannot be split within its bounds. If Step 1 or the engine finds fewer, stop and ask Manu.
- **Multiplicity (N6.8).** Each criterion is judged at 90, unadjusted, for continuity with T06/T08/T11; the Holm-adjusted p (per book, over the 16 criteria of the window) is printed beside each. The four books share one market and the same draws, so "3 of 4" is a consistency check; E1/E2 of the four rules share draws and are not independent, nor are `-half` on the two bases. **Picking the best of the six rules from these tables is itself a selection on seen data**, and the write-up says so.

### 2.3 The headline the decision needs (descriptive, no pass mark)

- **D-table:** per window, book and cost, for each new rule: CAGR, maximum drawdown, Calmar, volatility, and the **difference from its own original** (`50-half` − `50-orig`, `50-liq` − `50-orig`, `80-half` − `80-orig`, `80-liq` − `80-orig`), with start and end dates; trades and cost paid; weeks and sessions in each regime.
- **R6 paired contrast (the chance check on the difference):** every F1 and F3 draw is run with the original target and with the variant target (common random numbers); the rule's difference (variant minus original, CAGR and drawdown) is ranked among the draws' differences. A difference that is large but ranks near 50 is exposure, not timing.
- **Regime ledger:** for each of the 8 OUT-SELL spells per window: dates, weeks, what the parent, GOLDBEES and cash returned over the spell, and each rule's return over it, so the reader sees which spells carry the difference.
- **Comparators** (benchmarks, not nulls): hold the parent; the two `-orig` rules; the constant mix at each rule's own average parent share with its own average gold share of the rest; the 100% sleeve (50/50 and timed).

### 2.4 Outcome map (frozen)

| Pattern (per window, per base) | Conclusion |
|---|---|
| a variant's D-table difference is positive in ≥ 3 books **and** its R6 paired percentile ≥ 90 | the variant improves the rule on this window **because of when** the gold signal places it; PROVISIONAL (seen window, post-hoc) |
| difference positive in ≥ 3 books, R6 paired percentile < 90 | the improvement is what the variant's holding earned over those weeks, not timing; it would have helped about as much in random weeks |
| difference negative in ≥ 3 books | the variant makes the rule worse on this window |
| the two windows disagree | report both; no general statement; the 2019-2026 window is the one the live product traded |
| E1/E2 pass for a variant where its `-orig` passed (W2) | the change keeps the switch's equity-timing result |
| E1/E2 fail for a variant where its `-orig` passed (W2) | the change weakens the equity-timing result; say by how much (percentile change on common draws) |
| G1/G2 pass | the gold signal picks the right weeks for this regime holding (new: T08/T11's gold timing failed for the 20/80 sleeve) |
| F1/F2 or F3/F4 disagree | both are reported in the verdict and the status note |
| a V gate fails | nothing is read; fix; new run directory |

**Construction, in order.** Steps 1–3, 5, 8, 9 and the unchanged parts of 4, 6, 7 are T11 §3 (W1) and T08 §3 (W2), restated here only where they matter to the change.

1. **Calendar** and **decision dates** per window as above: T0, then the last session of each ISO week after T0; the decision on the last session of the window is made but not traded.
2. **Signals at decision date d.** The latest Master row of each of the three indices and of MAUc1 dated on or before d. IN iff all three SIGN are BUY. g = BUY iff the MAUc1 SIGN is BUY. Regime: IN, OUT-BUY, OUT-SELL as §2.1.
3. **Targets** `(e, gw)` per rule from the §2.1 table; one function `targets(rule, in, g)` is used for the real series and for every draw. IN: the parent's basket in force at its listed weights renormalised to 1 (T06/T08). `gw` while IN is carried in the log but never traded on.
4. **Trades.** A trade is due on the next session after decision j (j ≥ 1, a session exists) iff `e[j] != e[j-1] or (e[j] < 1 and gw[j] != gw[j-1])` (the engines' trigger). So in `-half` and `-liq` every entry into and exit from OUT-SELL trades; a gold flip while IN never trades; in `50-orig` a gold flip never trades. At a trade, with the session's fill prices (stocks OHLC4, GOLDBEES de-spiked OHLC4, cash at its level) and V the value of all holdings at them: the target values are `e·V·w_i` in each stock, `(1−e)·gw·V` in GOLDBEES and `(1−e)·(1−gw)·V` in cash; traded = Σ|target − held| over every line; cost = traded × cost_bps ÷ 2 ÷ 1e4 (10.4 bps a side at the 20.8 primary; 0 and 50 as robustness); every target is then scaled by (V − cost) ÷ V. Entering OUT-SELL from IN in `-half` therefore sells about half of the parent (re-weighted to the basket's listed weights) and buys GOLDBEES with the proceeds; entering from OUT-BUY buys the parent half out of the sleeve; leaving to IN buys back the parent and sells all GOLDBEES.
5. **No reset inside a regime.** While the regime is unchanged the weights drift and nothing is traded weekly.
6. **Parent rebalances.** A basket takes effect on the first session on or after its `availableDate`. While IN the old basket is sold and the new bought at that session's OHLC4 (cost on the value traded). While `e = 0`, nothing is traded. **While `e = 0.5` (the `-half` regime), D1: only the stock part is re-weighted to the new basket at its current value; GOLDBEES and cash units are unchanged; the cost is charged on the stock trades and taken from the stock part.** With e = 1 this is the existing path, so the IN behaviour and the regression gate are unaffected. A basket change on the session of a regime trade is one trade to the new basket at the new target; a gold flip and an exit on the same decision is one exit trade straight to the new target.
7. **Initial position** at T0's close, no cost, in every realisation including the nulls: the target of the decision at T0, with NAV 1 (an IN start holds the basket in force at T0 as drifted since its availableDate; an OUT start holds `gw` GOLDBEES and the rest cash; if T0 were OUT-SELL in `-half`, half the drifted basket plus half GOLDBEES; neither window starts there: W1 starts IN, W2 OUT-BUY).
8. **NAV, statistics:** cash account + Σ units × close; years = (sessions − 1) ÷ 252; CAGR = NAV_end^(1/years) − 1; maximum drawdown, Ulcer index, days ≥ 10% below the running peak, longest time under water, volatility = SD of daily returns × √252, Calmar = CAGR ÷ |maximum drawdown|.
9. **Null draws: T11's (W1) and T08's (W2) samplers, seeds and bounds unchanged.** The in-or-out series is cut into spells; **F1** = N4.10 free-length draw (seed `[6, id]` W2, `[14, 6, id]` W1) with the real gold series; **F2** = N4.9 spell shuffle (`[3, id]`, `[14, 3, id]`); **F3** = the real in-or-out series with an N4.10-g draw of the gold series (`[11, 6, id]`, `[14, 11, 6, id]`); **F4** = N4.9-g (`[11, 3, id]`, `[14, 11, 3, id]`); **F5** = an F1 and an F3 draw of the same id. 2,000 draws; the same draw ids for every cost and every book (the four books' decision series are identical). Each draw is run through the same engine, so each draw's regime is derived from its own in-or-out and gold states and each of the six rules is run on it: the `-orig` rows are the regression, the variants are judged, and each F1 and F3 draw gives the pair (original, variant) for R6. **Draws are reused on purpose (D2):** so a change of percentile between `80-orig` and `80-half` is due to the OUT-SELL target alone, and the originals' percentiles reproduce T08's and T11's stored ones exactly. This departs from the project habit of a fresh seed prefix per test; the free prefix **17** is reserved for anything new T14 needs to draw (none is planned).
10. **MDE** as §2.2, from the draws, after the run.
11. **R6 pairing.** For each F1 and F3 draw d and rule pair (orig, variant): Δ_d = statistic(variant, draw d) − statistic(orig, draw d), CAGR and maximum drawdown. The real difference Δ_real is ranked among the Δ_d with the percentile formula of §2.2.

**How the test works, in plain words.**

- **The series.** The book is one of the four real parents. Every Friday three weekly index signals and gold's own weekly signal are read. If all three index signals say BUY, the book holds the parent's basket. Otherwise it is in a "sleeve" and the original rule says what the sleeve is: half GOLDBEES and half cash (50/50), or 80% GOLDBEES and 20% cash when gold is BUY and 20/80 when gold is SELL (80/20). T14 changes only the weeks when the three-index signal is OUT and gold is SELL: there the book holds either half the parent and half GOLDBEES, or only the liquid ETF. Every price and level comes from the approved files of §3's table; the 2014-2018 parent is rebuilt from the parent's baskets and stock prices as in T11 (no published NAV exists to check it).
- **One decision.** On a Friday, look up the latest row of the three index files and of the gold file. Work out the regime (IN, OUT-BUY or OUT-SELL), read off the target for each of the six rules, and if it differs from last Friday's the trade is on the next session at the average of open, high, low and close. *[one real row printed in §4.0a]*
- **The result of one path.** Units of each stock, GOLDBEES and cash are fixed between trades; each day the NAV is the sum of units × price; CAGR and drawdown come from that NAV. *[printed in §4.0a]*
- **One draw.** A draw replaces one timing with a random one and keeps the other real. In the in-or-out draw (F1) the number of in-spells and out-spells and the total weeks in and out stay, and the lengths are drawn at random within the bounds; the gold signal stays real. The regime in the draw is then whatever its random in-or-out weeks and the real gold signal imply, and each of the six rules is run on it. In the gold draw (F3) the in-or-out weeks stay real and the gold SELL weeks are drawn at random. The draws are the same 2,000 T11 and T08 used. *[one draw's first 20 weeks printed in §4.0a]*
- **The ranking.** The rule's CAGR is placed among the 2,000 draws' CAGRs of the same rule: the percentile is the share of draws it beats (ties count half); the p-value is the chance of doing as well by luck; 90 or more in three of four books is a pass.
- **The headline difference and R6.** The D-table says what each variant earned over the original rule on the same weeks. R6 asks whether that difference is larger than the same change would have made in random weeks; if the difference ranks near 50 among the draws' differences, it is exposure (what the extra parent or gold happened to earn), not timing.
- **The comparators.** Holding the parent is what the book would do with no rule. The constant mix holds the variant's own average parent share all the time: it shows how much of any gain is just a different average exposure.

**Point-in-time rule.** A decision at d reads Master rows dated at or before d only; a trade is on a session after d. The engine asserts both for every decision, counts them, and writes the counts to `audit.json` (V2). The Master rows were produced afterwards by the production code and are not signals traded at the time, and no MAUc1 row was traded by the desk; disclosed, not corrected.

**Execution and cost.** AGENT.md R1 (next-session OHLC4, price return, 10.4 bps a side, weights drift between rebalances); the first position at T0 is free; no sale at the end; a stock bar without open, high or low is filled at the close; the GOLDBEES fill is T03's de-spike; every regime trade and parent rebalance pays on the value traded.

**Effective sample size and power.** 8 OUT-SELL spells per window (the independent unit for the change), 8 (W1) and 9 (W2) in-or-out round trips, 228 and 410 weekly decisions; the four books share one market, so they are one sample seen four times. MDE: §2.2, computed after the run.

**What the nulls control for, and what they do not.** F1 and F2 control for the fraction of weeks IN and the number of in-or-out switches; they do not control for which weeks the book is in the OUT-SELL regime (a draw's OUT-SELL weeks are its random OUT weeks that meet the real SELL signal). F3 and F4 control for the number of gold switches and the BUY weeks; they do not control for how the real SELL weeks coincide with the real OUT weeks. A `-half` variant is exposed to the parent in OUT-SELL weeks, and the nulls cannot say whether the parent happened to earn well in them; R6 and the constant mix are the controls for that.
