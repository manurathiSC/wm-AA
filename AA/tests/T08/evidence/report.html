Does the three-index switch with a MAUc1-timed GOLDBEES sleeve still choose when to hold the parent better than chance, and does the gold timing itself beat random gold timing?

2019-01-02 → 2026-10-30 (SCMO_0007 from 2019-01-04) · four parents of the AA books · decisions every Friday, trades next session at OHLC4 · 10.4 bps a side · 2,000 draws per book, cost and null · every number is computed from evidence/headline/

The answer

The equity switch beats chance; the MAUc1 timing of the sleeve is not distinguishable from random gold timing. Holding the parent only when the Nifty 100, Midcap 100 and Smallcap 100 are all BUY, and otherwise a sleeve of GOLDBEES and cash split 80/20 when the weekly MAUc1 gold supertrend is BUY and 20/80 when it is SELL, against random timings, on 2019-01-02 → 2026-10-30, after 20.8 bps. C1 (return) and C2 (worst fall) are judged against F1, random in-or-out timing with the real gold signal: 4 of 4 books and 3 of 4 at the 90th percentile (pre-registered pass: 3 of 4 each); F2 beside: 4 and 3. C3 and C4, the new claim, are judged against F3, random gold timing with the real in-or-out switch: 0 of 4 and 0 of 4; F4 beside: 0 and 0. Holm-adjusted p-values are in the table.

The catch: on the live decisions only (2021-11-01 → 2026-10-30) return beats the 90th percentile in 4 of 4 books against F1 and 0 of 4 against F3; the decisions before 2021-11 are a backfill by the production code, and so is every MAUc1 row; the rule, its 80/20 weights and the MAUc1 signal were chosen after seeing 2014-2026 results; the weeks added since T06 (2026-10-23, 2026-10-30) hold one MAUc1 flip and no switch of the index rule; 22 GOLDBEES fills after 2026-09-28 use the raw OHLC4. What to be careful about

  • Each null randomises one timing and holds the other real. F1 and F2 move the in-or-out switch and read the real gold signal in whatever weeks they are OUT; F3 and F4 keep the real switch and move the gold signal. A pass says the rule's timing beats random placement of that one ingredient, not that it beats holding the parent. Both random together is only a robustness check (R5).
  • Only C3 and C4 are the new claim. C1 and C2 replicate T06 under a different sleeve. The gold nulls move only the sleeve's split, so their spread is narrower than F1's (see the power tables).
  • Nothing is out of sample. The rule, the 80/20 weights and MAUc1 were chosen after seeing 2014-2026 results. Decisions before 2021-11 and every MAUc1 row are the production code run backwards. On the live decisions only, return is 4 of 4 at the 90th percentile against F1 and 0 of 4 against F3.
  • 9 round trips and 9 sleeve trades per book is all the evidence there is. The smallest return edge detectable at 80% power against F1 is 16.1 to 20.4 pp a year; against F3 4.3 to 4.7 pp.
  • The books are one market. SCMO_0007's return percentile against F1 is 94.2 and against F3 61.1.

8. How one F1 draw is made. It keeps the 9 in-spells and 10 out-spells, the first state (out) and the totals (201 weeks in, 209 out), and draws every length as an integer from 2 to 46 (in) or 2 to 40 (out; the bounds are the rule's longest spells), every allowed set of lengths equally likely. With c(m, t) the number of ways to write t weeks as m lengths in the bounds, c(0, 0) = 1 and c(m, t) = Σ over x of c(m − 1, t − x), the parts are drawn one at a time: with m parts and t weeks left, P(length = x) = c(m − 1, t − x) ÷ c(m, t). The uniform number u is rng.random() and the length is the first x whose cumulative weight exceeds u × c(m, t). In-spell parts come first, then out-spell parts, from the same generator. The real MAUc1 signal is then read in whatever weeks the draw is OUT.

Definitions

  • Percentile: (draws worse + 0.5 × draws tied) ÷ draws × 100, ties equal within a relative 1e-12. p-value: (1 + draws at least as good) ÷ (draws + 1). Holm p: the Holm step-down adjustment of the four deciding p-values of a book.
  • CAGR: NAV_end^(252 ÷ (sessions − 1)) − 1, daily NAV, after cost. Worst fall (maximum drawdown): the most negative NAV ÷ running peak − 1.
  • MAUc1: the weekly supertrend (period W, multiplier 2) of MCX gold futures, recomputed with the production code (D24). Gold signal g: its SIGN, BUY or SELL, from the latest weekly row dated on or before the decision date. Sleeve: while the rule is OUT, GOLDBEES and cash, 80/20 when g is BUY and 20/80 when SELL, changed only when g changes.
  • F1 in-or-out N4.10 with the real gold signal (seed default_rng([6, id])); F2 in-or-out N4.9 spell shuffle ([3, id]); F3 gold N4.10-g with the real switch ([11, 6, id]); F4 gold N4.9-g spell shuffle ([11, 3, id]); F5 an F1 and an F3 draw together (robustness only).
  • N4.10 free-length random timing: the same number of spells of each state and the same totals of weeks in each, every length an integer between the bounds, uniform over all allowed ordered tuples, drawn exactly by a dynamic programme, no rejection. N4.9 spell shuffle: the real series' spells of each state reordered among themselves. All nulls start in the real series' first state.
  • Weeks versus sessions: the in-or-out nulls hold invested weeks exactly; a week has 3 to 5 sessions, so invested sessions differ by draw and are reported.
  • MDE: 2.8 × the SD of the draws' CAGR (or maximum drawdown). Live window: 2021-11-01 → 2026-10-30.
