# Verifier spec for T08 (`AA/analysis/verify_t08.py`)

Written 2026-11-12 by the Step 3 agent, in its own words, from `T08.md` §1-§3 (frozen, wins), `PLAN.md`, `logs/evidence_contract.md`, and the T06 verifier (`analysis/verify_t06.py`, forked) with its spec (`tests/T06/logs/verifier_spec.md`). The verifier imports nothing from the engine, from `approved_data.py` or from any other AA module (only numpy, pandas, and scipy for one chi-square p-value). It parses `data/APPROVED_DATA.md` with its own code and opens a file only if that row is `APPROVED`. It never saw `t08_engine.py` or any T08 evidence produced by it. Where this spec says "mine" it means the verifier's own derivation.

## 1. Inputs (all `APPROVED` rows; nothing after END is read)
- **END** = the last row of the three index Masters (D03 `extended_2026-10-30/Master/Master_{Nifty100,MC100,SC100}.xlsx`), which must agree (2026-10-30).
- **Calendar** = the dates of the EXTENDED published NAV `Historical Data/extended_2026-11-06/published_nav/SCMO_<p>.csv` (columns `_id.scid, _id.date, index`, `YYYY/MM/DD`, newest first, so sorted ascending), from the book's first date T0 to END. The old pinned D17 files are read only for V4(e).
- **Signals.** Index SIGN as in T06. **Gold signal:** `Historical Data/extended_2026-11-06/MAUc1/Master_MAUc1.xlsx` (`Date`, `SIGN`); cross-check `mauc1_supertrend_weekly_extended.csv` (`review`, `SIGN`, `signal`; signal 1 = BUY). Prices and cash as T06: D18 `holdings_matrix/<p>_ohlc.csv` + `price_corrections.csv`, D19 weights, `_holding_weight_sources.csv` (skipped baskets), D09 GOLDBEES extended parquet, D12 MCX range (de-spike), D11 LBES extended and `liquidEtfFinal_extended`.
- Through-date rule (C-data): every row the verifier or the engine's `inputs.csv` lists must be APPROVED, its sha256 must start with the registry prefix and equal the file on disk, and its through-date must cover END, except D12, D19, D05 (earlier by design, last basket stays in force; GOLDBEES after 2026-09-28 uses the raw fill) and the old pinned D17 files (through 2026-10-22, used only for the overlap check).

## 2. Conventions (T08.md §3 steps 1-10)
- **Decision dates.** T0, then the last session of each ISO week after T0. The decision at the last session is made, not traded. State `s_j` = 1 (IN) iff all three SIGNs are BUY on the latest Master row dated on or before the decision date; gold `g_j` = 1 (BUY) iff the latest MAUc1 row dated on or before the decision date is BUY. Gold weight `gw_j` = 0.8 if `g_j` = 1, else 0.2 (`fixed50`: 0.5 always).
- **Position and trades.** NAV 1.000 at T0's close; the initial position (free) is the parent if `s_0` = 1, else the sleeve split `gw_0` GOLDBEES / `1 - gw_0` cash. A trade at decision j happens on the next session at each instrument's OHLC4 (stocks with the Polyplex correction, blank open/high/low replaced by the close; GOLDBEES the de-spiked OHLC4 where the MCX file has the session, the raw OHLC4 after; cash the as-of level) iff `s_j` differs from the current equity target, or the target is 0 and `gw_j` differs from the current sleeve weight. Kinds: `switch_in` (OUT→IN), `switch_out` (IN→OUT, sleeve bought at `gw_j`), `sleeve_rebalance` (OUT on both sides, g changed), `basket_change` (parent rebalance while IN; skipped while OUT). A flip of g while IN is not a trade; an exit on a flip decision is one exit at the new weight.
- **Trade arithmetic (T06's `trade()`).** `V` = value of every holding at the fill; targets `e·V·w` for stocks, `(1-e)·gw·V` GOLDBEES, `(1-e)(1-gw)·V` cash; `traded` = sum of absolute changes (stocks, GOLDBEES, cash); `cost` = `traded · bps/2/10000`; all targets scaled by `(V - cost)/V`. So after a sleeve rebalance GOLDBEES value = `gw·(V - cost)`, cash = `(1-gw)(V - cost)`, and `traded = |ΔGOLDBEES| + |Δcash|` (twice the gold change).
- **NAV and statistics** as T06: Σ units × close; years = (sessions - 1)/252; CAGR, max drawdown, volatility (SD of daily returns × √252), Ulcer (√mean of squared percent drawdowns), days ≥ 10% below the peak, longest under water, Calmar. Percentile = (worse + 0.5 tied)/draws × 100, tie within a relative 1e-12; p = (1 + at least as good)/(draws + 1); implemented twice (vectorised, loop).
- **Runs the verifier simulates** (all costs 0 / 20.8 / 50 bps, full window, LBES): `strategy` (timed sleeve), `strategy_fixed50`, `hold_parent`, `constant_mix` (50/50 non-stock, f = invested weeks / weeks, rebalanced to e = f at every decision's next session and at every basket change), `constant_mix_timed` (the non-stock share split `gw_j` at each decision; at a basket change the weight in force), `sleeve_only` (50/50, no trade), `sleeve_only_timed` (trades only when `gw` changes, kind `sleeve_rebalance`). Plus `strategy` at 20.8 on LIQUIDCASE cash and on the live window (T0 = first session on or after 2021-11-01).
- **Closed form** for the strategy: end NAV = product over fixed-unit segments of (trade-day jump × segment growth); compared at 1e-9.

## 3. Nulls and counting
- **Families.** F1 = in-or-out N4.10 + real gold; F2 = N4.9 + real gold; F3 = real in-or-out + gold N4.10-g; F4 = real in-or-out + gold N4.9-g; F5 = N4.10 in-or-out + N4.10-g gold, same draw id. A draw's gold weights come from its gold states (timed) or are 0.5 (`fixed50`, F1 and F2 for R6).
- **Structure per draw** (saved states, every draw): weeks, first state, spell counts, in/BUY weeks equal the real series; N4.9 / N4.9-g sorted lengths equal; N4.10 lengths in [2, 46] (in) and [2, 40] (out), summing to the window's totals; N4.10-g BUY in [2, 68], SELL in [2, 28], summing to the totals. Bounds are derived from the real series (longest spells, shortest 2) and must equal the numbers the spec fixes.
- **Regeneration from seeds.** N4.9 `default_rng([3, id])` and N4.9-g `[11, 3, id]`: permute the 1-spells, then the 0-spells, interleave from the real first state. N4.10 `[6, id]` and N4.10-g `[11, 6, id]`: exact-uniform sampler (DP counts c(m, t), parts one at a time, `rng.random()` and a cumulative-sum search), 1-spell lengths first, then 0-spell lengths, interleaved from the real first state. The verifier's sampler reproduces T06's 100 saved N4.10 draws per book exactly (a gate).
- **Counts.** Allowed tuples by inclusion-exclusion Σ_j (−1)^j C(k, j) C(S − k·lo − j(hi − lo + 1) + k − 1, k − 1) for every book, window and state (in-or-out and gold), compared with `count_table.json`, with a plain DP and with the pre-registered `step_1_spell_counts.json`.
- **Sampler exactness.** Own sampler on k = 3, S = 12, [2, 6] (19 tuples): 200,000 samples, chi-square p > 0.001; every position's mean over the saved N4.10 / N4.10-g draws within 4 standard errors of S/k (exact variance from the counts).
- **Per-row bookkeeping** (every row of `draws.csv`): invested weeks, in/out spell counts and lengths, sessions invested (coverage = next decision's session index − this one's, last week 0), gold BUY weeks, BUY/SELL spell counts and lengths, `sleeve_trades` (decisions j = 1..D−2 that are OUT after an OUT decision with g changed; 0 in `fixed50`), family tags.
- **Sampled re-simulation** (`C-draw-nav`): for every group of `draws.csv` (see the contract's row list) the first 20 and 20 seeded random draw ids, each at the group's cost; the eight statistics at 1e-9 relative; for F1 and F3 at 20.8 bps, 20 + 20 curves against `draw_curves_20.8.npz` (float32, 1e-5).

## 4. T06 regression on the overlap (V4)
(a) decisions equal T06's on every shared decision (T06's last, 2026-10-22, is read against 2026-10-23); (b) with 0.5 throughout the verifier's simulator reproduces T06's strategy, hold_parent, sleeve_only and constant-mix NAVs (T06's own f), and the published NAV, to 1e-12 relative on dates ≤ 2026-10-22; (c) T06's first 20 N4.10 draws with the rule's states appended reproduce T06's saved float32 curves (1e-5); (d) always-in at 0 cost against the published NAV within ±0.25 pp/yr overall and ±0.5 pp per calendar year; (e) the extended D17 equals the old D17 on its rows (1e-12); (f) the gold series equals the weekly csv signal at every decision date, and Master SIGN equals the weekly csv SIGN on every shared date.

## 5. The sleeve (V6)
`C-sleeve`: every exit and sleeve rebalance of the verifier's strategy (all costs) against `sleeve_trades.csv`: date, kind, decision date, g and weight after, GOLDBEES and cash value after, traded, cost, raw-fill flag; and for rebalances g and weight before and the values before; completeness both ways. `C-sleeve-identity`, on the file's own numbers: cost = traded·bps/2/10⁴; GOLDBEES + cash after = before − cost; post-trade split = `gw_after` of the value after cost (1e-12 of the value); `traded` = |ΔGOLDBEES| + |Δcash| against the targets; a rebalance requires a change of g; the raw-fill flag is true exactly after the MCX range's last date (2026-09-28); at 0 cost the value is continuous.

## 6. Tolerances and exit rule
NAVs, statistics, trades, units: relative 1e-9; the T06 overlap: 1e-12; float32 curves: 1e-5; percentiles, spell structure, counts: exact. Exit 0 only if rows were compared, no gate check failed or is missing, no expected evidence file is missing, and, in the default run, every corruption of the self-test (decisions flipped, a NAV scaled, an in-or-out draw and a gold draw out of bounds, three gold weeks flipped, a sleeve rebalance removed, an F3 draw's end NAV scaled) is applied and detected by its own check. `verify_t08.json` carries the counts.

## 7. Readings chosen where the contract is silent (verified against T06 where possible)
1. `gw_after` in `trades.csv` is compared only on trades that leave the book OUT; what an IN trade carries is undefined.
2. `sleeve_trades` (stats and draws) counts `sleeve_rebalance` trades only, not exits.
3. In `constant_mix_timed` a basket-change rebalance uses the sleeve weight in force from the latest decision.
4. `sleeve_trades.csv`: `g_before`/`g_after` may be BUY/SELL or 0/1; values-before and `g_before`/`gw_before` are compared on rebalances only (an exit has no sleeve before it); `gold_value_after` = units × the fill price.
5. `spell_sig` equals `strategy_sig` on every F2 row and `g_spell_sig` equals `real_g_spell_sig` on every F4 row (informational; signatures are otherwise not defined by the contract).
6. `cash` may be written `liq` or `liquidcase`; both are read as `liq`.
