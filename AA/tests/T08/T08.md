## 1. Idea and references

**The idea in one sentence.** Holding the parent only when the Nifty 100, Midcap 100 and Smallcap 100 weekly supertrends are all BUY, and otherwise a sleeve of GOLDBEES and cash split 80/20 when the MAUc1 gold supertrend is BUY and 20/80 when it is SELL, (a) chooses *when* to hold the parent better than random in-or-out timings that keep the rule's switch count and weeks invested, and (b) times the gold-versus-cash split better than random gold timings that keep the gold signal's switch count and BUY weeks, on the latest approved data.

**Where it came from.** T03 and T06 tested the equity switch with a fixed 50/50 sleeve and found it beats random timings (F-AA-S04, F-AA-S07, both PROVISIONAL). The desk's rule also times the sleeve: 80% GOLDBEES when gold's weekly supertrend (MAUc1, MCX gold futures) is BUY and 20% when it is SELL. T06 §7, item 6 asked whether that adds anything. The earlier `final_strategy` study backtested the whole rule on the AA books with no null at all (§2).

**The decision this will change.** Whether the MAUc1-timed sleeve can be said to time gold better than chance, separately from whether the equity switch still beats chance with this sleeve in place. A pass on C1 and C2 only replicates T06. Only C3 and C4 bear on the new part of the rule.

**Robustness null F5 (both random):** an F1 draw of the in-or-out states with an F3 draw of the gold states, independent. Reported only (R5).

**The rule is inside every allowed set.** Its own lengths lie within the bounds by construction, so every N4.9 draw is one configuration of N4.10 and every N4.9-g draw one configuration of N4.10-g. Gate V3 checks both.

**Which null decides.** F1 decides C1 and C2; F3 decides C3 and C4. F2 and F4 are reported beside them. If a pair disagrees, both are reported in the verdict and the finding's status note says so (AGENT.md N6.8).

**Statistic the null is judged on.** After-cost CAGR for H1 and H3, higher better. Maximum drawdown on daily NAV for H2 and H4, shallower better.
- **Unit:** one realisation is book × cost × null. The percentile is computed per realisation against its own draws.
- **Percentile:** (draws worse + 0.5 × draws tied) ÷ draws × 100. Ties are equal within a relative 1e-12.
- **p-value:** (1 + draws at least as good) ÷ (draws + 1). It is reported beside the percentile; the pass mark is on the percentile.
- **Secondary statistics**, each with its own percentile against the same draws: volatility of daily returns × √252 (lower better); Ulcer index (lower better); days at least 10% below the running peak (fewer better); longest time under water (shorter better); Calmar = CAGR ÷ |maximum drawdown|.

**Pass mark and draws.** The percentile must be at least 90. 2,000 draws give a resolution of 0.05 points and a smallest p-value of 1/2001; (D + 1) ≥ m ÷ α is met for the four criteria (m = 4, α = 0.10).

The calendar is D17's dates up to the window end 2026-10-30, so no price after it is read, although D17, D18, GOLDBEES, cash and D24 run later.

**Construction, in order.** Steps 1 to 10 are T06 §3 (steps 1–10) unchanged except where stated:

1. **Calendar.** The sessions are D17's dates, per book, from its first date T0 (2019-01-02; 0007 2019-01-04) to **2026-10-30**. The sessions D18 has and D17 lacks are never read (F-AA-D15).
2. **Decision dates.** T0, then the last D17 session of each ISO week after T0. T0 is the window's base. The decision at the window's last session (2026-10-30, a Friday) is made but not traded.
3. **Signals at decision date d.** Read the latest Master row of each of the three indices dated on or before d. The decision is IN if SIGN is BUY on all three, else OUT. **Read the latest MAUc1 Master row dated on or before d; g_d = BUY or SELL.** A week whose Master row is dated after its last D17 session uses the previous week's row, one week stale and never ahead of d. Rows before 2021-11 are a backfill by the production code, and every MAUc1 row is.
4. **Targets.** IN: 100% parent. OUT: **w_d of the sleeve in GOLDBEES and 1 − w_d in cash, where w_d = 0.8 if g_d is BUY and 0.2 if g_d is SELL.** The 50/50 sleeve of T06 is kept as a mode of the engine (`fixed50`) for comparators and R6; it is not the rule.
5. **Initial position.** NAV is 1.000 at T0's close; the book holds at that close what the decision at T0 says, with the sleeve at w_{T0} when OUT. No cost is charged for this initial position, in any realisation including the nulls.
6. **Execution.** A trade happens only when the target changes, on the **next D17 session after d**, at that session's OHLC4 of each instrument (stocks: Eikon bar with the Polyplex correction; if the bar lacks an open, high or low the close is used; GOLDBEES: the de-spiked OHLC4, high and low clipped to MCX gold's range that day, scaled, plus 0.5%, and the raw OHLC4 after the MCX file ends; cash: the level on that session, as-of). Cost: ow = cost_bps ÷ 2 ÷ 10,000 per side on every value traded (stock, GOLDBEES, cash). The target changes in three ways:
   - **Exit** (IN → OUT): sell the basket; buy GOLDBEES and cash at the split w_d of the proceeds after cost, with g_d the signal read at the exit decision.
   - **Entry** (OUT → IN): sell the sleeve; buy the basket in force at its listed weights.
   - **Sleeve rebalance** (OUT on both sides, g changes): sell and buy GOLDBEES and cash from their drifted values to the split w_d of the sleeve value after cost. Cost on the value traded (the sum of the absolute changes in GOLDBEES and cash). Between events the sleeve is not rebalanced and its weights drift, as T06's did.
   - A flip of g while IN is not a trade; the split is read at the next exit decision. A decision that is both an exit and a flip is one exit trade at the new g.
7. **Parent rebalances.** A basket takes effect on its `availableDate`. While IN, at that session's OHLC4 the old basket is sold and the new one bought, with cost on the value traded. While OUT, no trade happens but the current basket is recorded. The last approved basket is in force to the window's end (2026-10-30); the next rebalance is expected about mid-December 2026 and is not in the data.
8. **NAV.** Cash account + Σ stock units × close + GOLDBEES units × close + cash units × level. It is 1.000 at T0's close. No sale at the end.
9. **Statistics.** Sessions run from T0 to the last session. years = (sessions − 1) ÷ 252. CAGR = NAV_end^(1/years) − 1. Maximum drawdown, Ulcer index, days at least 10% below the running peak, longest time under water (sessions), volatility = SD of daily returns × √252, Calmar = CAGR ÷ |maximum drawdown|.
10. **Comparators** (benchmarks, not nulls), on the same calendar, cost and T0: hold the parent (the engine with the decision fixed at IN, at 0 cost and at each cost, plus the published NAV rebased to 1.000); **the rule with the sleeve fixed at 50/50** (T06's rule on this window); the constant mix with a 50/50 non-stock share (the strategy's average equity share f = invested weeks ÷ weeks held as f in the parent and (1 − f)/2 each in GOLDBEES and cash, rebalanced at each decision date's next session at OHLC4 with the same cost, including after T0's decision) **and the constant mix with a MAUc1-timed non-stock share** (f in the parent and (1 − f)·w_d in GOLDBEES, rebalanced the same way); the 100% sleeve at 50/50 held throughout, and **the 100% sleeve timed by MAUc1** (w_d in GOLDBEES, trading only when g changes).
11. **Null draws.** From the in-or-out decision series s_0 … s_n (n = 409; SCMO_0007 408), cut maximal spells: 9 in-spells and 10 out-spells. From the gold series g_0 … g_n, cut maximal spells: 9 BUY spells and 9 SELL spells.
    - **N4.9 spell shuffle of the in-or-out series (F2, T06's function unchanged):** shuffle the in-spells among themselves and the out-spells among themselves with `numpy.random.default_rng([3, draw_id])` (draw_id = 0 … 1,999), interleave starting in the rule's first state (OUT).
    - **N4.10 free-length draw of the in-or-out series (F1, T06's function unchanged):** k_in = 9, S_in = 201, HI_in = 46; k_out = 10, S_out = 209 (SCMO_0007: 208), HI_out = 40; lower bound 2. Let c(m, t) be the number of ways to write t as m integers in [lo, hi]: c(0, 0) = 1, c(0, t) = 0 for t > 0, c(m, t) = Σ_{x = lo..hi} c(m − 1, t − x). The parts are drawn one at a time: with m parts and t weeks left, P(length = x) ∝ c(m − 1, t − x) for x in [lo, hi]. This is exactly uniform over the c(k, S) ordered tuples, with no draw rejected. *Worked example (k = 3, S = 12, bounds [2, 6], 19 tuples):* the first part is 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 with probabilities 3, 4, 5, 4, 3 over 19. The uniform number comes from `rng.random()` and a cumulative-sum search (never `Generator.choice`), from `numpy.random.default_rng([6, draw_id])`, in-spell lengths first and then out-spell lengths from the same generator. The tuples are exactly 7,107,131,351,925 (in) and 89,834,825,481,830 (out) for SCMO_0003 (SCMO_0007 out: 89,734,761,224,680); the engine prints the counts for every book and window. The live window's constants are the same bounds; its totals are 124 (5 in-spells) and 138 (6 out-spells).
    - **N4.9-g spell shuffle of the gold series (F4):** shuffle the BUY spells among themselves and the SELL spells among themselves with `numpy.random.default_rng([11, 3, draw_id])`, interleave starting in the real first gold state (BUY).
    - **N4.10-g free-length draw of the gold series (F3):** the same sampler (the functions of F1, with different arguments) with k_buy = 9, S_buy = 265 (SCMO_0007: 264), bounds [2, 68]; k_sell = 9, S_sell = 145, bounds [2, 28]; BUY lengths first, then SELL lengths, from `numpy.random.default_rng([11, 6, draw_id])`; interleaved starting in BUY. The bounds are the real series' shortest spell (2) and longest BUY and SELL spells (68, 28), computed before any return was read. The tuples are exactly 129,645,864,230,832 (BUY) and 117,482,550,471 (SELL) for SCMO_0003 (SCMO_0007 BUY: 127,812,203,830,350); the live window's totals are 177 (6 BUY spells) and 85 (7 SELL spells), the same bounds, with 595,441,694 and 127,978,767 tuples. The in-or-out seeds `[3, draw_id]` and `[6, draw_id]` are T06's, so the in-or-out samplers are unchanged; the gold seeds begin with 11, a first element no earlier test uses (3 to 10 all appear in T03 to T07).
    - **F5:** an F1 draw (seed `[6, draw_id]`) and an F3 draw (seed `[11, 6, draw_id]`) of the same draw id, run together.
    - The same draw ids are used for every cost and for books whose series are identical (SCMO_0003, 0010, 0029); SCMO_0007 draws from its own totals with the same seeds.
    - Each draw runs through the same engine (steps 5 to 9) with the same cost: in F1, F2 and F5 the draw's in-or-out states with the real (F1, F2) or drawn (F5) gold states; in F3 and F4 the real in-or-out states with the drawn gold states. The draws' stock trades use the real baskets and real parent rebalances; only the states differ. Every F1 and F2 draw is also run with the sleeve fixed at 50/50 (R6). The states of every draw are saved (`draw_states.npz`, in-or-out and gold), and `rerun_draw.py` reproduces any draw from its id.
12. **MDE.** For C1 and C3, the smallest timing edge detectable at 80% power and 5% two-sided is 2.8 × null_sd, where null_sd is the SD of after-cost CAGR across the draws of a realisation (per null). For C2 and C4 it is 2.8 × the SD of maximum drawdown. Both are printed beside every FAIL. **Disclosed:** the numbers did not exist when this section was written; the thresholds in §2 do not depend on them. T06's N4.10 values (null SD of CAGR 5.4 to 6.9 pp, MDE 15 to 19 pp) are the prior for C1; for C3 and C4 there is no prior, because the sleeve is held only in OUT weeks and the gold nulls move only the sleeve's split, so their spread is probably much narrower and the same edge may be easier to detect.

**How the test works, in plain words.**

- **The series.** Each parent's NAV is built every day from the stocks it holds. Every Friday, three index signals are read. If all three say BUY, the book holds the parent's stocks. Otherwise it holds a sleeve of GOLDBEES and cash, and the split is set by a fourth signal, gold's own weekly supertrend (MAUc1): 80% GOLDBEES and 20% cash when it says BUY, 20% and 80% when it says SELL. The split is changed only when that signal changes. A switch is made on the next session at the (open + high + low + close) ÷ 4 of each instrument, paying 10.4 bps on every rupee traded. *[the real signals, trades and NAVs are printed in §4.0a]*
- **One decision.** On a Friday, look up the latest weekly row of the three index Master files and of the MAUc1 Master file. If the three index SIGN cells are all BUY, the target is the parent; otherwise the target is the sleeve at the split the gold signal gives. The trade, if the target changed, is on the next session. *[printed in §4.0a]*
- **The result of one path.** Units of each stock, GOLDBEES and cash are fixed between trades. Each day the NAV is Σ units × close. The CAGR and drawdown come from that daily NAV. *[printed in §4.0a]*
- **One draw of the in-or-out nulls (F1, F2).** The real path is 19 spells: 9 in and 10 out. F2 keeps the 19 lengths and shuffles which in-spell goes where and which out-spell goes where. F1 keeps only the *number* of spells and the *total* weeks in and out, and picks nine new in-spell lengths between 2 and 46 weeks that add up to 201 and ten new out-spell lengths between 2 and 40 weeks that add up to 209, every allowed set equally likely. The real gold signal is then read in whatever weeks the draw is OUT.
- **One draw of the gold nulls (F3, F4).** The real gold signal is 18 spells: 9 BUY and 9 SELL. F4 shuffles which BUY spell goes where and which SELL spell goes where. F3 keeps the number of spells and the total BUY and SELL weeks, and picks nine BUY lengths between 2 and 68 weeks adding up to 265 and nine SELL lengths between 2 and 28 weeks adding up to 145, every allowed set equally likely, starting BUY. The real in-or-out switch is kept, so the book still holds the parent in exactly the rule's weeks, and only the split of its sleeve moves. The first 20 weeks of a real, an F2, an F1, an F4 and an F3 series are printed in §4.0a.
- **The ranking.** The rule's CAGR is placed among the 2,000 draws' CAGRs: the percentile is the share of draws it beats (ties count half); the p-value is the chance of doing as well by luck. 90 or more is a pass.
- **The comparators.** Holding the parent is what the book would do with no rule. The same rule with the 50/50 sleeve is T06's rule on this window, so the gap between the two is the sleeve's effect. The constant mixes hold the same average share in the parent all the time: they show how much of any gain is only a different average exposure.

**Point-in-time rule.** A decision at d reads Master rows (the three indices and MAUc1) dated at or before d only (step 3). A trade is on a session after d. The engine asserts both for every decision, counts them, and writes the counts to `audit.json`. The Master rows before 2021 were produced afterwards by the production code and are not signals traded at the time, and no MAUc1 row was traded by the desk; this is disclosed, not corrected.

**Execution and cost.** AGENT.md R1 (next-session OHLC4, price return, 10.4 bps a side, weights drift) with these specifics: cash trades pay the cost; the first position at T0 is free; no sale at the end; GOLDBEES uses the de-spiked fill (X02) where the MCX file covers the session and the raw OHLC4 after 2026-09-28; sleeve rebalances pay the cost on the value traded.

**Effective sample size and power.** 9 round trips of the in-or-out switch per book on the full window and 5 on the live window; 9 sleeve trades on the full window and 6 on the live window; 410 weekly decisions. The independent unit for timing is the round trip (equity) and the gold flip that reaches the sleeve (gold). MDE: step 12, computed after the run from the draws.

**What the nulls control for, and what they do not.** F1 and F2 control for the fraction of weeks in the parent and the number of switches (F2 also for every spell's length). They do not control for which weeks the sleeve is held (gold's 2024-26 rally lifts any draw that sits in a BUY sleeve in those years), nor for the real gold signal's state in the draw's OUT weeks. F3 and F4 control for the number of gold switches, the fraction of weeks BUY, and the whole in-or-out series (so the equity part and the cost of it are identical to the rule's). They do not control for how many of the drawn flips fall in OUT weeks, nor for the persistence of the gold signal. No null controls for the stale-week and backfill issues below, nor for the 80/20 weights being fixed after earlier looks. R6 and the comparators bound the sleeve's effect.
