## Readings and decisions (where the frozen text or contract is silent or inaccurate); all are in `audit.json['readings']` (E1..E24)
- **E1 / E6 / E8 (stand-ins):** the frozen text asserts a GOLDBEES bar and D18 bars; where a session lacks them (2016-10-30 GOLDBEES; 2017-10-19 all stocks) the previous close is valuation and fill (for GOLDBEES `gf.fillna(gc)`: previous close, not the previous de-spiked fill, T08's behaviour; §3 says "previous close and fill"; no trade falls on that session, so the choice is moot) and each use is counted.
- **E4 (initial position):** §3 step 5 says the book "holds at that close" the basket. The engine, as T08 E4, buys the basket at T0's OHLC4 (its availableDate session) and rescales NAV to 1.000 at T0's close. The difference is the OHLC4-to-close move of 2014-09-01 on the weights; free of cost either way. Silent in the frozen text.
- **E12 (R7):** "return set to zero (the previous close carried)" is implemented as a return-preserving splice (the day's return exactly 0, later prices of that stock scaled, other returns unchanged); carrying the previous close for one day only would put the jump on the next day. "A stock held by the parent": weight > 0 in the basket in force on session t-1 or t. The manual-export contribution is reported as a counterfactual (always-in 0-cost NAV with the held export stock-days' returns set to zero, against the real one) plus the held-session counts; §2 does not define "contribution". The plan's eight R7 days do not exist in D18's 20% rule (one day is listed instead).
- **E13:** F1 and F2 are run with the 50/50 sleeve at all three costs (§3 says only "also run"); R2 and R3 cells also run F1 fixed50 (A1 is in R2 and R3).
- **E18 (V4(a)):** constant-mix comparators use f from the stored decisions.csv (f depends on the whole stored window); the stored T06 `strategy` is compared with the engine's `strategy_fixed50`; the engine's last decision (2019-12-31) has no stored counterpart (T08/T06 decide it on 2020-01-03) and is skipped; every session of the window must be compared (`dates_compared == sessions`).
