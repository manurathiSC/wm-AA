# T11 independent verifier: what it checks and how (gate V1)

Written 2026-11-17 by the Step 3 agent, **before** reading `logs/step_2_log.md`, from `T11.md` §1-§3 (frozen), `logs/evidence_contract.md`, the smoke evidence's files (as files to compare against) and the earlier verifiers `verify_t08.py` / `verify_t09.py` (models). The verifier is `AA/analysis/verify_t11.py`. It imports only numpy, pandas, scipy and the standard library. It never opened `t11_engine.py`, `t08_engine.py`, `t09_engine.py`, `t06_engine.py`, `approved_data.py` or any other module of `analysis/`; it parses `data/APPROVED_DATA.md` itself.

Run: `python analysis/verify_t11.py --run smoke` (or `--run headline`; `--evidence DIR --draws N` for another folder; `--no-selftest` skips the corrupted copy). Exit 0 only if rows were compared, none failed, none were missing, no evidence file is missing and every corruption of the self-test was detected. It writes `evidence/<run>/verify_t11.json` (`exit_ok`, `gate_compared`, `gate_failures_and_missing`, `n_checks`, `checks`, `criteria`, `percentiles`, `selftest.detected`, `selftest.detected_by`, ... the keys T09's `analyse.py` and `check_page.py` rely on).

## 1. Inputs: what the verifier reads, and how it refuses the rest

- `rd(path, first_date, gate_only)` is the only way a file is opened. It raises unless the path is a row of the `approved-table` in `APPROVED_DATA.md` with status `APPROVED`; a row carrying `[usable-from: d]` needs a first read date and raises if it is earlier than `d` (standing rule 7). The Smallcap 100 Master is read from 2011-11-25 and the rows before are dropped at load.
- Files read: D26 calendar; D03 Masters (3); D24b `Master_MAUc1.xlsx`, `mauc1_supertrend_weekly_extended.csv`, `mauc1_daily_clean_extended.csv`; D09 GOLDBEES; D11 LBES and liquidEtfFinal; D18 `*_ohlc.csv` (4) and `price_corrections.csv`; D19 weight matrix (weights), weight sources (cross-check of D05's `skipped`) and columns (registered); D05 rebalance history (`skipped`, `availableDate`); D17 extended published NAVs (V4(a)/(f) only). **Gate-only read:** D24 `Master_MAUc1.xlsx`, for V4(e) only (T11.md §3 lists D24 as "Not read" by the test; the engine could not do V4(e); the verifier may, and the read is marked `gate_only` and excluded from the "every file the verifier read is listed in inputs.csv" rule).
- Test evidence read (not data): `tests/T06/evidence/headline/{daily_nav,decisions}.csv`, `tests/T08/evidence/headline/{daily_nav,decisions}.csv` (V4(a)), `tests/T11/logs/step_1_spell_counts.json` (Step 1's counts), the evidence folder itself. Nothing unapproved is opened.

**C-data** (V1). For every file the verifier read: APPROVED row, sha256 prefix equals the file's on disk, the row's through-date covers the window's end (2019-01-02; the published NAVs 2019-12-31), first read date not before `usable-from`. For every row of `inputs.csv`: the path is an APPROVED row; its sha256 starts with the registry prefix and equals the disk's; `bytes` equals the disk's size; `through` equals the registry's; `approved_row` equals `APPROVED <id>`; `usable_from` equals the registry's marker; the row's id is not one T11.md §3 lists as "Not read" (D25, D20-D23, D20b/D23b, D09b, D12, D03b, D15, D24). Every file the verifier needed (gate-only excepted) is listed in `inputs.csv`.
**C-sc100-restriction**: `rd` refuses a Smallcap 100 Master read from 2011-11-24, from 2004-01-01, and without a first date; the registry carries the 2011-11-25 marker; the verifier's own first read is 2011-11-25; no decision (the verifier's, the evidence's) reads a Smallcap 100 row dated before 2011-11-25.

## 2. Re-derivation from the raw files (own code, nothing shared with the engine)

- **Calendar**: D26's dates 2014-09-01 -> 2019-01-02 (1,070 sessions); r3 window starts at the first session on or after 2015-01-02 (989 sessions).
- **Decisions** (every book): T0, then the last D26 session of each ISO week after T0; the decision is IN iff the latest Master row on or before the date is BUY in Nifty 100, Midcap 100 and Smallcap 100; `g` is the SIGN of the latest D24b Master row on or before the date; the last decision (2019-01-02) is made, not traded; trade date = next session. Also the r3 window's decisions.
- **Books**: D18 bars (Polyplex correction applied), reindexed on the calendar (D18 dates off the calendar are ignored), a session without a bar carries the previous close for valuation and fill, a bar without an open, high or low is filled at its close; OHLC4 fills. Baskets from D19 (listed weights renormalised to sum 1) with D05's skipped flag, effect session = first calendar session on or after availableDate (19 baskets effective 2014-09-01 -> 2018-12-17 per book; the first is the initial one).
- **Gold and cash**: GOLDBEES OHLC (D09) de-spiked as T03/T08 (open, high, low clipped to the MCX range of D24b's daily file plus 0.5% around the close; raw OHLC4 where there is no MCX bar), previous close on a session with no GOLDBEES bar (2016-10-30); cash = LBES level as-of (R2: liquidEtfFinal).
- **Simulation** (own code, T08's arithmetic): initial position at T0 bought at the basket's first-session OHLC4, scaled to NAV 1.000 at T0's close, free of cost; trades only when the target changes (exit, entry, sleeve rebalance OUT-OUT with g changed) on the next session; basket changes while IN at the availableDate session at OHLC4 on old-sold/new-bought; cost = traded x bps / 2 / 1e4, every target scaled by (V - cost) / V; NAV = Σ units x close. Runs: `strategy` (timed sleeve, Part B), `strategy_fixed50` (Part A), `hold_parent`, `constant_mix`, `constant_mix_timed` (f = invested weeks / weeks, rebalanced after every decision including T0's), `sleeve_only`, `sleeve_only_timed`, at 0 / 20.8 / 50 bps; `liq` (strategy and Part A, 20.8) and `r3` (20.8).
- **R7**: flagged stock-days (|one-day close return| >= 20% of a stock with weight > 0 in the basket in force on t-1 or t), the return-preserving splice (every later close and fill of the stock multiplied by close[t-1]/close[t]), and the six variants `*_r7_flagged` / `*_r7_noexport` (0 and 20.8 bps); the export-priced held stock-sessions per stock against T11.md §3 (574 / 123 / 556 / 122).
- **Counts**: inclusion-exclusion `Σ_j (-1)^j C(k,j) C(S - k·lo - j·(hi-lo+1) + k - 1, k - 1)` for each of the 4 tables x 2 windows, against a plain DP and against T11.md §3 step 11 (4,024,559,144; 19,406,343,528; 6,835,192,844,305; 4,976,743,356; r3 7,654,290; 659,773,128; 426,468,399,525; 644,288,463).
- **Samplers**: N4.9 (`rng.permutation` of the 1-spells then of the 0-spells) and N4.10 (parts one at a time, P(x) ∝ c(m-1, t-x), `rng.random()` and a cumulative-sum search, 1-spell lengths first), from seeds `[14, 3, id]`, `[14, 6, id]`, `[14, 11, 3, id]`, `[14, 11, 6, id]`, with bounds `lo = min(2, shortest spell)`, `hi = longest spell` per state computed from the series.

## 4. Self-test (the corrupted copy)

`corrupt_copy` writes a copy of the evidence with nine corruptions and re-runs every check on it (the real evidence files are the fallback). The copy must make the verifier exit non-zero **and** each corruption must be caught by its own check: 5 decisions flipped (C-dec); 3 gold weeks flipped in gold_signal.csv (C-gold); the strategy 0-cost NAV x 1.001 (C-nav0); an N4.10 in-or-out draw with an in-spell longer than the bound, states and draws.csv made consistent (C-states-lengths); an N4.10-g draw with a BUY spell above its bound (C-gold-states); a sleeve rebalance removed from sleeve_trades.csv (C-sleeve); a basket trade removed from trades.csv (C-trades); the Smallcap 100 `usable_from` of inputs.csv set to 2004-01-01 (C-data); an F3 draw's end NAV x 1.001 (C-draw-nav). Exit 0 requires `selftest.detected` true.

## 5. Not covered by the verifier (stated, not hidden)

The percentiles' interpretation and the verdicts (Step 4's scripts); the MDE; the results page; the hand-worked examples of §4.0a; the oracle / negative-control / planted-effect runs (not run in T11, §2). The verifier checks a sample of draws' NAVs (first 20 and 20 random per book and row group), but the **structure** of every draw and the regeneration of every saved state from its seed.

## 6. Readings where the frozen text or the contract is silent (decided by the verifier; see `step_3_log.md`)

1. **`sessions_invested`, week 0.** The contract says "daily returns earned while the parent is held (T06-1)". T06 and T08 never needed week 0 (their rule starts OUT). Here the rule starts IN at T0. Reading: a week's sessions run from its trade session (decision session + 1) to the next decision session, so cover_j = dec_idx[j+1] - dec_idx[j]; week 0's trade session is T0 itself (the initial position is struck at T0's OHLC4), so its cover is dec_idx[1] - dec_idx[0] + 1 (the first week holds 5 sessions, like a full Monday-to-Friday week; T11.md §2 says "weeks hold 3 to 5 sessions"). The other reading (returns only: session 0 earns none) is one lower for every draw and for the rule (442 against 443 for the rule). Reported only; it enters no criterion. Found when the first smoke run of the verifier disagreed with the engine by exactly one session in every draw that starts IN; the engine's column is the "+1" reading.
2. **Baskets table**: baskets with availableDate on or after T0 whose effect session (first D26 session on or after availableDate) lies inside the window; `basket_no` counts from 0; `role` initial = effect session 0.
3. **D18 dates not on the calendar are ignored** (contract §Engine behaviour 1): closes and fills are reindexed on the calendar before the forward fill. The initial position's fill is the OHLC4 on the first D18 date on or after the basket's availableDate (T0 itself here).
4. **An exit's `gw_before`** is the previous gold weight; when the exit decision also flips g it differs from `gw_after` (one exit trade at the new g, T11.md §3 step 6). The verifier requires only that the sleeve be empty before an exit.
5. **`invested_weeks` of the comparators** = Σ e over the decisions (228 for hold-the-parent, f x 228 for the constant mixes, 0 for the sleeve-only runs); of the strategy runs = weeks IN.
6. **`count_table.json` bounds** for `r3` may be flat (`in`, `out`, `buy`, `sell`) or nested like `full`; both are accepted.
7. **The sample of draws**: the first 20 ids plus 20 ids drawn from the rest with `default_rng([20261117, book_index, crc32(group)])` (a seed that is not one of T11's), per book and row group (25 groups per book).
8. **V4(f)'s gap** = (hold-the-parent NAV on the last session ÷ its first) minus (published NAV on the last session ÷ its first), in percentage points, over D17's 2019 sessions of each book.
9. **V4(c)** is not re-done (the verifier may not read the engines); the engine's own result is read from `regression.json` and must show no unexpected change.
