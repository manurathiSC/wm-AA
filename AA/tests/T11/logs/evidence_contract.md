## Engine behaviour the verifier and the scripts must match (`T11.md` §3 steps 1-11)

1. **Calendar** = D26's dates from T0 = 2014-09-01 to END = 2019-01-02 (1,070 sessions). Every session needs a cash level (as-of), a GOLDBEES close (the session 2016-10-30 has none of its own: the previous close is used for valuation and fill, counted; no trade falls on it in any realisation, and the engine counts it) and D18 bars for held stocks (the session 2017-10-19 has no D18 bar for any stock: each stock's previous close is valuation and fill; counted; no trade falls on it in the real rule or any draw; thin Muhurat sessions 2016-10-30 and 2018-11-07 leave some held stocks without a bar: the same stand-in, counted). D18 dates not on the D26 calendar are ignored (the 41 sessions before T0 are not read into any window).
2. **Decisions:** T0, then the last D26 session of each ISO week after T0; IN iff the latest Master row on or before the date is BUY in all three indices (Smallcap 100 rows from 2011-11-25 only); `g` = SIGN of the latest D24b `Master_MAUc1.xlsx` row on or before the date. The decision at END (2019-01-02) is made, not traded. First decision 2014-09-01 (a Monday; the first Master rows read are the last ones dated on or before it).
3. **Initial position at T0's close, free of cost (all realisations incl. nulls):** IN = the basket effective on 2014-09-01 at its listed weights renormalised to sum 1, bought at **that session's OHLC4** (T0 is the basket's own availableDate session, so there is no drift) and the whole book scaled so NAV = 1 at T0's close; OUT (r3 window): `gw` GOLDBEES and `1 - gw` cash of NAV 1 at the close (gw from T0's g; 0.5 for `fixed50`); the r3 window's IN start would be the basket in force drifted from its availableDate (not used by the real series, which starts OUT, but used by draws that start IN: none, draws keep the first state).
4. **Trades** exist on the next session after decision j iff `e` changed or (`e` = 0 and `gw` changed): `switch_in`, `switch_out`, `sleeve_rebalance`. A flip of g while IN is no trade. A decision that is both an exit and a flip is one exit at the new gw. **Basket changes:** a basket takes effect on the first D26 session on or after its availableDate; while IN it is a `basket_change` trade (old basket sold, new bought at OHLC4 with cost on the value traded); while OUT nothing trades; a basket-change session that is also an entry or an exit session is that one trade (`switch_in` buys the basket in force that session; `switch_out` sells). Arithmetic (T06/T08 `trade`, unchanged): V at the fill prices (stocks OHLC4, GOLDBEES de-spiked OHLC4, cash level); targets `e*V*w_i`, `(1-e)*gw*V`, `(1-e)*(1-gw)*V`; `traded = sum |target - held|`; `cost = ow * traded`; every target scaled by `(V - cost)/V`. Weights while IN right after an entry or a basket change equal the basket's weights (renormalised) to 1e-12.
5. **Fills:** stock OHLC4 = (open+high+low+close)/4, the close if any of open/high/low is blank; stocks with no bar that session use the last close; Polyplex (SCMO_0007) prices dated before 2021-02-17 times 1.123274 (constant in this window, changes no return). GOLDBEES: close from D09; fill = OHLC4 of open/high/low clipped to MCX gold's range that day (band = (high-low)/close of D24b's daily file + 0.5% of close, high and low then re-bounded by open and close), raw OHLC4 on a session without an MCX bar (counted in `audit.data`); cash: LBES level as-of (R2 `liq`: liquidEtfFinal as-of).
6. **Statistics** (`stats`): sessions T, years = (T-1)/252, CAGR = NAV_end^(1/years) - 1, MDD, vol = SD of daily returns x sqrt(252), Ulcer, days at least 10% below the peak, longest underwater (sessions), Calmar. Percentile, p-value, MDE: `T11.md` §2-§3.
7. **R7 flagged-day rule (a reading, E-number in audit.json):** a stock-day (i, t), t = 1..T-1 of the full window, is listed iff the ffilled D18 close return `close[t]/close[t-1] - 1` has absolute value >= 0.20 and the stock has weight > 0 in the basket in force on session t-1 or on session t. The re-run replaces each flagged stock's price path by a return-preserving splice (every later price of that stock, closes and OHLC4 fills, multiplied by `close[t-1]/close[t]`, cumulatively over flags), so that day's return is exactly 0 and all other returns are unchanged. `_noexport` does the same for every (i, t) with `src == 'export'` on session t and w > 0 in the basket in force on t-1 or t.
8. **Parent NAV file:** per book, the four NAV series are the corresponding runs of `daily_nav.csv` (same numbers, to 1e-15).
