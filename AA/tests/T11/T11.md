## 1. Idea and references

**The idea in one sentence.** Holding each real parent only when the weekly supertrends of the Nifty 100, Midcap 100 and Smallcap 100 are all BUY, and otherwise a GOLDBEES and cash sleeve, (a) chooses *when* to hold the parent better than random in-or-out timings that keep the rule's switch count and weeks invested (with a fixed 50/50 sleeve, Part A, and with a sleeve split by the MAUc1 gold supertrend, Part B), and (b) times the gold-versus-cash split better than random gold timings that keep the gold signal's switch count and BUY weeks (Part B), on 2014-09-01 → 2019-01-02.

**Where it came from.** T06 (fixed 50/50 sleeve) and T08 (MAUc1-timed sleeve) run on the real parents but only from 2019-01-02, when the published NAVs begin. T09 ran the same two rules on a proxy of the three raw indices for 2011-11-25 → 2019-01-02, because it took it that no holdings exist before 2019. That premise is wrong: the parents' baskets exist from 2014-03 (D05, D19), and the approved stock prices (D18) and GOLDBEES (D09) from 2014-07-01 (the Eikon table `ohlcv_eikon` itself starts that day; read-only check of the whole table and of UPL, Aarti Drugs and Emami on 2026-11-17, no earlier row). So the rules can be run on the real parents' own stocks from 2014-09-01. The result can then be set beside T09 (same weeks, proxy) and T06/T08 (real parents, 2019–2026).

**The decision this will change.** Whether the F-AA-S04 / S07 finding (the switch beats chance, PROVISIONAL) and the open question of T08 (does the MAUc1 timing of the sleeve beat random gold timing) hold up on years the parents' real-stock backtests never covered, and whether T09's failure on the proxy over these same weeks was due to the proxy (no stock selection) or to the weeks. A pass is supporting evidence only: the window is seen, short, and the rebuilt parent NAV before 2019 has no published series to check against.

**What this test is NOT asking.**
- Whether the parents' stock selection helps or hurts (T07's question; it needs market-cap tags, and the approved tags start 2018-01-01), or whether a different rule, signal, sleeve or weighting would do better. There is one rule per part and no search.
- Whether the rebuilt parent NAV is the published one. No published NAV exists before 2019-01-02, so nothing in this test can check it (§3 contaminants); the rebuild code is the one that matches the published NAV to 0.3 pp a year over 2019–2026.
- Whether the rule beats holding the parent. That is a comparator (§3), not a null.
- Whether the rule has skill on weeks nobody has seen. There are none.
- Whether gold goes up when MAUc1 is BUY. C3 and C4 ask whether the rule's particular timing of the split beats random timing with the same number of switches and BUY weeks, as seen through the rule's own OUT weeks.

**Robustness null F5 (both random):** an F1 draw of the in-or-out states with an F3 draw of the gold states, independent. Reported only (R5).

**The rule is inside every allowed set.** Its own lengths lie within the bounds by construction, so every N4.9 draw is one configuration of N4.10 and every N4.9-g draw one configuration of N4.10-g. Gate V3 checks both.

**Which null decides.** F1 decides A1, A2, C1 and C2; F3 decides C3 and C4. F2 and F4 are reported beside them. If a pair disagrees, both are reported in the verdict and the finding's status note says so (AGENT.md N6.8).

**Statistic the null is judged on.** After-cost CAGR for A1, C1 and C3, higher better. Maximum drawdown on daily NAV for A2, C2 and C4, shallower better.
- **Unit:** one realisation is part × book × cost × null. The percentile is computed per realisation against its own draws.
- **Percentile:** (draws worse + 0.5 × draws tied) ÷ draws × 100. Ties are equal within a relative 1e-12.
- **p-value:** (1 + draws at least as good) ÷ (draws + 1). It is reported beside the percentile; the pass mark is on the percentile.
- **Secondary statistics**, each with its own percentile against the same draws: volatility of daily returns × √252 (lower better); Ulcer index (lower better); days at least 10% below the running peak (fewer better); longest time under water (shorter better); Calmar = CAGR ÷ |maximum drawdown|.

**Pass mark and draws.** The percentile must be at least 90. 2,000 draws give a resolution of 0.05 points and a smallest p-value of 1/2001; (D + 1) ≥ m ÷ α is met for the six criteria (m = 6, α = 0.10).

**Construction, in order.** Steps 1 to 10 are T08 §3 (steps 1–10) unchanged except where stated:

1. **Calendar.** The sessions are D26's dates from T0 = 2014-09-01 to **2019-01-02**. The engine asserts every session has a GOLDBEES bar (or, for 2016-10-30, which has none of its own, the previous close and fill, counted) and a cash level (as-of), and a D18 bar for the held stocks or the stand-in above.
2. **Decision dates.** T0, then the last D26 session of each ISO week after T0. T0 is the window's base. T0 (2014-09-01) is a Monday: the basket's decision date (2014-08-31) is a Sunday and the first signal read is the last Master row on or before 2014-09-01. The decision at the window's last session (2019-01-02, a Wednesday) is made but not traded.
3. **Signals at decision date d.** Read the latest Master row of each of the three indices dated on or before d (Smallcap 100: dated 2011-11-25 or later). The decision is IN if SIGN is BUY on all three, else OUT. Read the latest MAUc1 Master row dated on or before d (D24b); g_d = BUY or SELL. A week whose Master row is dated after its last D26 session uses the previous week's row, one week stale and never ahead of d. Every row is a backfill by the production code.
4. **Targets.** IN: 100% the parent's basket in force, at the listed weights renormalised to sum 1 as T06 and T08 do (raw row sums 0.9996–1.0005). OUT, Part A: 50% GOLDBEES and 50% cash. OUT, Part B: **w_d of the sleeve in GOLDBEES and 1 − w_d in cash, w_d = 0.8 if g_d is BUY and 0.2 if g_d is SELL.** The two parts share the draws of the in-or-out nulls; Part A is the engine's `fixed50` mode.
5. **Initial position.** NAV is 1.000 at T0's close; the book holds at that close what the decision at T0 says (T0's basket is the one effective on 2014-09-01; the rule says IN at T0, so the book holds that basket; Part B: the sleeve at w_{T0} when OUT). No cost is charged for this initial position, in any realisation including the nulls.
6. **Execution.** As T08 step 6 with the D26 calendar: a trade happens only when the target changes, on the **next D26 session after d**, at that session's OHLC4 of each instrument (stocks: Eikon bar with the Polyplex correction; if the bar lacks an open, high or low the close is used; GOLDBEES: the de-spiked OHLC4, high and low clipped to MCX gold's range that day, scaled, plus 0.5%, with the MCX range read from D24b's daily file; cash: the level on that session, as-of). Cost: ow = cost_bps ÷ 2 ÷ 10,000 per side on every value traded (stock, GOLDBEES, cash). The target changes in three ways, as T08: **exit** (IN → OUT), **entry** (OUT → IN), **sleeve rebalance** (Part B, OUT on both sides, g changes). A flip of g while IN is not a trade; the split is read at the next exit decision. A decision that is both an exit and a flip is one exit trade at the new g.
7. **Parent rebalances.** A basket takes effect on the first D26 session on or after its `availableDate`. While IN, at that session's OHLC4 the old basket is sold and the new one bought, with cost on the value traded (18 changes after T0; none skipped). While OUT, no trade happens but the current basket is recorded. The basket effective 2018-12-17 is in force to the window's end. A basket-change session that is also an exit or an entry session is that one trade.
8. **NAV.** Cash account + Σ stock units × close + GOLDBEES units × close + cash units × level. It is 1.000 at T0's close. No sale at the end.
9. **Statistics.** Sessions run from T0 to the last session. years = (sessions − 1) ÷ 252. CAGR = NAV_end^(1/years) − 1. Maximum drawdown, Ulcer index, days at least 10% below the running peak, longest time under water (sessions), volatility = SD of daily returns × √252, Calmar = CAGR ÷ |maximum drawdown|.
10. **Comparators** (benchmarks, not nulls), on the same calendar, cost and T0: hold the parent (the engine with the decision fixed at IN, at 0 cost and at each cost; there is no published NAV to compare with before 2019); the rule with the sleeve fixed at 50/50 (A1's rule); the constant mix with a 50/50 non-stock share and the constant mix with a MAUc1-timed non-stock share (f = invested weeks ÷ weeks held in the parent, rebalanced at each decision date's next session at OHLC4 with the same cost, including after T0's decision); the 100% sleeve at 50/50 held throughout and the 100% sleeve timed by MAUc1.
11. **Null draws.** From the in-or-out series s_0 … s_n (n = 227), cut maximal spells: 8 in-spells and 8 out-spells. From the gold series g_0 … g_n, cut maximal spells: 10 BUY spells and 9 SELL spells.
    - **N4.9 spell shuffle of the in-or-out series (F2):** shuffle the in-spells among themselves and the out-spells among themselves with `numpy.random.default_rng([14, 3, draw_id])` (draw_id = 0 … 1,999), interleave starting in the rule's first state (IN).
    - **N4.10 free-length draw of the in-or-out series (F1):** k_in = 8, S_in = 93, bounds [1, 28]; k_out = 8, S_out = 135, bounds [2, 34]. Let c(m, t) be the number of ways to write t as m integers in [lo, hi]: c(0, 0) = 1, c(0, t) = 0 for t > 0, c(m, t) = Σ_{x = lo..hi} c(m − 1, t − x). The parts are drawn one at a time: with m parts and t weeks left, P(length = x) ∝ c(m − 1, t − x) for x in [lo, hi]. This is exactly uniform over the c(k, S) ordered tuples, with no draw rejected. *Worked example (k = 3, S = 12, bounds [2, 6], 19 tuples):* the first part is 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 with probabilities 3, 4, 5, 4, 3 over 19. The uniform number comes from `rng.random()` and a cumulative-sum search (never `Generator.choice`), from `numpy.random.default_rng([14, 6, draw_id])`, in-spell lengths first and then out-spell lengths from the same generator. The tuples are exactly 4,024,559,144 (in) and 19,406,343,528 (out); the engine prints the counts for every window.
    - **N4.9-g spell shuffle of the gold series (F4):** shuffle the BUY spells among themselves and the SELL spells among themselves with `numpy.random.default_rng([14, 11, 3, draw_id])`, interleave starting in the real first gold state (BUY).
    - **N4.10-g free-length draw of the gold series (F3):** the same sampler with k_buy = 10, S_buy = 132, bounds [2, 36]; k_sell = 9, S_sell = 96, bounds [2, 19]; BUY lengths first, then SELL lengths, from `numpy.random.default_rng([14, 11, 6, draw_id])`; interleaved starting in BUY. The bounds are the real series' shortest spell (2) and longest BUY and SELL spells (36, 19), computed before any return was read. The tuples are exactly 6,835,192,844,305 (BUY) and 4,976,743,356 (SELL). The seeds' first element 14 is used by no earlier test.
    - **F5:** an F1 draw (seed `[14, 6, draw_id]`) and an F3 draw (seed `[14, 11, 6, draw_id]`) of the same draw id, run together.
    - The R3 window has its own spells, totals and bounds from the same seeds: in-or-out in [2, 28] (k 6, S 85) and out [2, 34] (k 7, S 125), starting OUT; BUY [2, 36] (k 9, S 130), SELL [2, 15] (k 9, S 80), starting SELL; the tuples are exactly 7,654,290, 659,773,128, 426,468,399,525 and 644,288,463.
    - The same draw ids are used for every cost and every book (the four books' series are identical). Each draw runs through the same engine (steps 5 to 9) with the same cost: in F1, F2 and F5 the draw's in-or-out states with the real (F1, F2) or drawn (F5) gold states; in F3 and F4 the real in-or-out states with the drawn gold states. The draws' stock trades use the real baskets and real parent rebalances; only the states differ. Every F1 and F2 draw is also run with the sleeve fixed at 50/50 (the null of A1 and A2, and R6). The states of every draw are saved (`draw_states.npz`, in-or-out and gold), and `rerun_draw.py` reproduces any draw from its id.
12. **MDE.** For A1, C1 and C3, the smallest timing edge detectable at 80% power and 5% two-sided is 2.8 × null_sd, where null_sd is the SD of after-cost CAGR across the draws of a realisation (per null). For A2, C2 and C4 it is 2.8 × the SD of maximum drawdown. Both are printed beside every FAIL. **Disclosed:** the numbers did not exist when this section was written; the thresholds in §2 do not depend on them. T06's N4.10 values (null SD of CAGR 5.4 to 6.9 pp, MDE 15 to 19 pp) and T09's (null SD 4.2 to 4.9 pp) are the priors for A1; this window is 4.3 years, so the spread is expected to be wider.

**NAV output.** Besides the evidence set, the run writes `evidence/headline/parent_nav_2014-09-01_2019-01-02.csv`: per parent and session, the hold-the-parent NAV (always IN) at 0 and 20.8 bps and the Part A and Part B strategy NAV at 20.8 bps, base 1.000 at T0, with the caption "rebuilt from the parent's baskets and Eikon prices, not validated against a published NAV before 2019-01-02". The results page draws one growth chart per parent with the same caption. **How the test works, in plain words.**

- **The series.** The book is one of the four real parents. Every Friday three weekly index signals are read. If all three say BUY, the book holds the parent's basket in force at its listed weights; otherwise it holds a sleeve of GOLDBEES and cash. In Part A the sleeve is always half and half. In Part B a fourth signal, gold's own weekly supertrend (MAUc1), sets the split: 80% GOLDBEES and 20% cash when it says BUY, 20% and 80% when it says SELL, changed only when that signal changes. A switch is made on the next session at the (open + high + low + close) ÷ 4 of each instrument, paying 10.4 bps on every rupee traded; while the book holds the parent it also follows each of the parent's 18 basket changes. *[the real signals, trades and NAVs are printed in §4.0a]*
- **One decision.** On a Friday, look up the latest weekly row of the three index Master files and of the MAUc1 Master file. If the three index SIGN cells are all BUY, the target is the parent's basket; otherwise the target is the sleeve at the split the sleeve rule gives. The trade, if the target changed, is on the next session. *[printed in §4.0a]*
- **The result of one path.** Units of each stock, GOLDBEES and cash are fixed between trades. Each day the NAV is Σ units × close. The CAGR and drawdown come from that daily NAV. *[printed in §4.0a]*
- **One draw of the in-or-out nulls (F1, F2).** The real path is 16 spells: 8 in and 8 out, starting in. F2 keeps the 16 lengths and shuffles which in-spell goes where and which out-spell goes where. F1 keeps only the *number* of spells and the *total* weeks in and out, and picks eight new in-spell lengths between 1 and 28 weeks that add up to 93 and eight new out-spell lengths between 2 and 34 weeks that add up to 135, every allowed set equally likely. In Part B the real gold signal is then read in whatever weeks the draw is OUT.
- **One draw of the gold nulls (F3, F4).** The real gold signal is 19 spells: 10 BUY and 9 SELL. F4 shuffles which BUY spell goes where and which SELL spell goes where. F3 keeps the number of spells and the total BUY and SELL weeks, and picks ten BUY lengths between 2 and 36 weeks adding up to 132 and nine SELL lengths between 2 and 19 weeks adding up to 96, every allowed set equally likely, starting BUY. The real in-or-out switch is kept, so the book still holds the parent in exactly the rule's weeks, and only the split of its sleeve moves. The first 20 weeks of a real, an F2, an F1, an F4 and an F3 series are printed in §4.0a.
- **The ranking.** The rule's CAGR is placed among the 2,000 draws' CAGRs: the percentile is the share of draws it beats (ties count half); the p-value is the chance of doing as well by luck. 90 or more is a pass.
- **The comparators.** Holding the parent is what the book would do with no rule. The same rule with the 50/50 sleeve is Part A's rule, so the gap between the two is the sleeve's effect. The constant mixes hold the same average share in the parent all the time: they show how much of any gain is only a different average exposure.

**Point-in-time rule.** A decision at d reads Master rows (the three indices and MAUc1) dated at or before d only (step 3). A trade is on a session after d. The engine asserts both for every decision, counts them, and writes the counts to `audit.json`. The Master rows were produced afterwards by the production code and are not signals traded at the time, and no MAUc1 row was traded by the desk; this is disclosed, not corrected.

**Execution and cost.** AGENT.md R1 (next-session OHLC4, price return, 10.4 bps a side, weights drift between rebalances) with these specifics: cash trades pay the cost; the first position at T0 is free; no sale at the end; a stock bar without an open, high or low is filled at the close; the GOLDBEES fill is de-spiked as T03; sleeve rebalances and parent rebalances pay the cost on the value traded.

**Effective sample size and power.** 8 round trips of the in-or-out switch and 9 sleeve trades, shared by the four books; 228 weekly decisions over 4.3 years. The independent unit for timing is the round trip (equity) and the gold flip that reaches the sleeve (gold). MDE: step 12, computed after the run from the draws.

**What the nulls control for, and what they do not.** F1 and F2 control for the fraction of weeks in the parent and the number of switches (F2 also for every spell's length). They do not control for which weeks the sleeve is held (gold's path in the weeks a draw sits in the sleeve), nor for the real gold signal's state in the draw's OUT weeks. F3 and F4 control for the number of gold switches, the fraction of weeks BUY, and the whole in-or-out series. They do not control for how many of the drawn flips fall in OUT weeks, nor for the persistence of the gold signal. No null controls for the backfill, the unvalidated rebuild, or for the 80/20 weights being fixed after earlier looks. R6 and the comparators bound the sleeve's effect.
