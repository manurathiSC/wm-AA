## 1. Idea and references

**The idea in one sentence.** Holding the parent only when the Nifty 100, Midcap 100 and Smallcap 100 weekly supertrends are all BUY, and a 50/50 GOLDBEES and cash sleeve otherwise, chooses *when* to hold the parent better than the same in and out spells placed at random.

**Where it came from.** It is the production rule of the four AA books (`analysis/final_strategy.py`, `evidence/final_strategy/`), simplified to a fixed 50/50 sleeve. The production sleeve splits 80/20 or 20/80 on a gold supertrend (MAUc1); that gold-timing step is removed so the test is about the equity timing only. `evidence/why_n100sc100/` is the earlier study of why the triple of indices beats single-index rules.

**The decision this will change.** Whether the equity-timing half of the AA books' rule can be called skill, or only a way of being out of the market for about half the weeks.

**What is already known.**

| Ref | What it says | Relationship |
|---|---|---|
| `evidence/final_strategy/` | The triple rule with the 80/20 gold-timed sleeve: live 2021-26 26.6% CAGR, 2014-21 18.4% | tests: same rule, simplified sleeve, a null added |
| `evidence/why_n100sc100/` | The triple beats single-index rules on drawdown; its return edge depends on the regime | depends on |
| the 0029 demo (index level, earlier descriptive work) | The rule's CAGR ranked at the 87th or 88th percentile of random timings | depends on: already seen, see §2 |
| T01 (COMPLETE, 2026-10-01) | A parent's **own** 65-day percentile does not time weakness (F-AA-S01, S02) | contrasts: own series vs market indices |
| F-AA-M01 (T01) | With four books, a timing edge of 12-18 pp/yr is the smallest detectable | depends on: §3 computes the MDE for this test |

**What this test is NOT asking.**
- Whether the 80/20 gold timing adds anything. It is removed.
- Whether a different rule would do better. There is one rule and no search.
- Whether the rule beats holding the parent. That is a comparator (§3), not a null: lower drawdown from lower exposure is expected.

## 2. Hypothesis and pre-registered criteria

**Written before any run, 2026-11-05.** What was already known:
- The rule was chosen after seeing 2014-2026 results. This is not out of sample.
- The 0029 demo (87th and 88th percentile in earlier descriptive work) has been seen. The final-strategy CAGRs on 2021-26 and 2014-21 have been seen.
- The weekly decision series, which depends only on the three index signals, was computed before this section was written, to count spells and set the null's draw count: **399 weekly decisions after the first, 9 in-spells and 9 out-spells (17 switches), about 50% of weeks invested, identical in all four books.** No return of any kind was computed.
- **Expectation, declared:** H2 passes in most books, because the rule was out of equities in the 2020 and 2022-24 falls. H1 is uncertain: the earlier percentiles sit near the 90 mark, so a FAIL at 90 is the more likely result.

**Hypotheses** (after cost at 20.8 bps round trip; one rule; judged per book):
- **H1, timing.** The rule's exits earn more than randomly timed exits at the same exposure. Judged on CAGR. Falsified if C1 fails.
- **H2, protection.** The rule's worst fall is shallower than that of randomly timed exits. Judged on maximum drawdown. Falsified if C2 fails.

**Null (primary): N4.9 spell shuffle.**

| Dimension | Randomised / held / not matched | How | Measured in §4.0 as |
|---|---|---|---|
| when the in-spells and out-spells fall relative to the market | **randomised** | the 9 in-spells are shuffled among themselves and the 9 out-spells among themselves, then interleaved starting in the rule's first state (OUT). 9! × 9! ≈ 1.3 × 10^11 orderings, so 2,000 seeded draws (§3) | — |
| fraction of weeks invested | held | the same spell lengths | strategy vs every draw: invested weeks (must be identical) |
| number of entries and exits | held | the same number of spells | spell counts (must be identical) |
| every spell's length | held | the same multiset | sorted in-spell and out-spell lengths (must be identical) |
| book, stocks, parent rebalances, calendar, sleeve, cost, execution | held | constant for the run | — |
| what the sleeve earned in the weeks it is held | **not matched** | a draw is out in different weeks, so gold's and cash's path is sampled differently | null CAGR spread |
| persistence of the signal | held | the spells are the rule's own | — |
| the first state | held | OUT, as the rule starts on 2019-01-02 | — |

**Null (secondary): circular shift** of the weekly in-or-out series (N4.4). The 400-point series is rotated by k weeks for every k with 46 ≤ k ≤ n − 46, where 46 weeks is the longest spell, as AGENT.md N4.9 requires. It is run for every such k, so it has no seed. It keeps the exposure and spell structure except at the wrap.

**Which null decides.** N4.9. If the two disagree, the conclusion is the N4.9 one, and the disagreement is reported in the verdict.

**Statistic the null is judged on.** After-cost CAGR for H1, higher better. Maximum drawdown on daily NAV for H2, shallower better.
- **Unit:** one realisation is book × cost. The percentile is computed per realisation against its own draws.
- **Percentile:** (draws worse + 0.5 × draws tied) ÷ draws × 100. Ties are equal within a relative 1e-12.
- **p-value:** (1 + draws at least as good) ÷ (draws + 1). It is reported beside the percentile; the pass mark is on the percentile.
- **Why CAGR answers H1:** the null holds exposure and switching fixed and moves only *when* the exits fall. If the rule's exits land on the parent's weak stretches, it earns more than the same spells placed at random.
- **Secondary statistics**, each with its own percentile against the same draws: volatility of daily returns × √252 (lower better); Ulcer index (lower better); days at least 10% below the running peak (fewer better); longest time under water (shorter better); Calmar = CAGR ÷ |maximum drawdown|.
- Total return is rank-equivalent to CAGR within a realisation and is not reported separately.

**Pass mark and draws.** The percentile must be at least 90. 2,000 draws give a resolution of 0.05 points and a smallest p-value of 1/2001; (D + 1) ≥ m ÷ α is met for the two primary criteria (m = 2, α = 0.10).

**Validity gates. If any fails, nothing below may be read.**

**Construction, in order.**

1. **Calendar.** The sessions are D17's dates, per book, from its first date T0 (2019-01-02; 0007 2019-01-04) to 2026-08-21. The 8 sessions D18 has and D17 lacks are never read (F-AA-D15).
2. **Decision dates.** T0, then the last D17 session of each ISO week after T0. T0 is the window's base. The decision at the window's last session (2026-08-21) is not traded.
3. **Signal at decision date d.** Read the latest Master row of each of the three indices dated on or before d. The decision is IN if SIGN is BUY on all three, else OUT. A week whose Master row is dated after its last D17 session (five weeks: those ending 2019-10-25, 2020-11-13, 2021-11-03, 2024-05-17 and 2024-10-31, where a Muhurat or special session sits after D17's last) uses the previous week's row, one week stale and never ahead of d. The decision at T0 uses the latest row on or before T0. Rows before 2021-11 are a backfill by the production code, not signals traded at the time.
4. **Targets.** IN: 100% parent. OUT: 50% GOLDBEES, 50% LBES cash.
5. **Initial position.** NAV is 1.000 at T0's close. The book holds at that close what the decision at T0 says: the basket in force at T0 (the baskets in D19 that have taken effect, skipped baskets dropped) at the close prices, or the sleeve at close and LBES level. No cost is charged for this initial position, in any realisation including the nulls; this is the same starting point as the published NAV and the Step 1 rebuild.
6. **Execution.** A trade happens only when the decision changes the state. It is on the **next D17 session after d**, at that session's OHLC4.
   - Stocks: OHLC4 of the Eikon bar, with the Polyplex correction applied to bars dated before 2021-02-17. If the bar lacks an open, high or low, the close is used.
   - GOLDBEES: the de-spiked OHLC4 of `final_strategy.despiked_goldbees` (high and low clipped to MCX gold's range that day, scaled, plus 0.5%), re-implemented in the engine from D09 and D12.
   - LBES: its level on that session (as-of).
   - **Exit:** sell the whole basket at OHLC4; buy GOLDBEES and cash so that each is half of the proceeds after cost.
   - **Entry:** sell the sleeve; buy the basket in force at its listed weights at OHLC4.
   - **Cost:** ow = cost_bps ÷ 2 ÷ 10,000 per side, on every value traded (stock, GOLDBEES, LBES).
7. **Parent rebalances.** A basket takes effect on its `availableDate`. While IN, at that session's OHLC4 the old basket is sold and the new one bought, with cost on the value traded. While OUT, no trade happens, but the engine records which basket is current, so that re-entry buys the right basket. The sleeve drifts while OUT: its units do not change.
8. **NAV.** Cash account + Σ stock units × close + GOLDBEES units × close + LBES units × level. It is 1.000 at T0's close. No sale at the end: the last NAV is marked at the close of 2026-08-21.
9. **Statistics.** Sessions run from T0 to the last session. years = (sessions − 1) ÷ 252. CAGR = NAV_end^(1/years) − 1. Maximum drawdown, Ulcer index, days at least 10% below the running peak, longest time under water (sessions), volatility = SD of daily returns × √252, Calmar = CAGR ÷ |maximum drawdown|.
10. **Comparators** (benchmarks, not nulls), all on the same calendar, cost and T0:
    - hold the parent: the engine with the decision fixed at IN, at 0 cost and at each cost, plus the published NAV (D17) rebased to 1.000;
    - the constant mix: the average equity share f of the strategy (invested weeks ÷ weeks), held as f in the parent and (1 − f)/2 each in GOLDBEES and cash, rebalanced at each decision date's next session at OHLC4 with the same cost;
    - the 100% sleeve: 50% GOLDBEES and 50% cash, held throughout.
11. **Null draws.**
    - **N4.9 spell shuffle:** from the decision series s_0 … s_n (n = 399), cut maximal spells. Shuffle the in-spells among themselves and the out-spells among themselves with `numpy.random.default_rng([3, draw_id])` (draw_id = 0 … 1,999; the same draws for the four books and three costs). Interleave starting OUT. 9! × 9! = 131,681,894,400 > 50,000, so a seeded sample is used. Each draw runs through the same engine (steps 5 to 9) with the same cost. `rerun_draw.py` reproduces any draw from its id.
    - **Circular shift:** s rotated by k positions for each k with 46 ≤ k ≤ n − 46, each through the same engine.
    - The draws' stock trades use the real baskets and real parent rebalances; only the in-or-out state differs.
12. **MDE.** For C1, the smallest timing edge detectable at 80% power and 5% two-sided is 2.8 × null_sd, where null_sd is the SD of after-cost CAGR across the draws of a realisation. For C2 it is 2.8 × the SD of maximum drawdown. Both are printed beside every FAIL. **Disclosed:** the numbers did not exist when this section was written; the thresholds in §2 do not depend on them. The circular-shift SD is printed beside the N4.9 one as a pilot.

**How the test works, in plain words.**

- **The series.** Each parent's NAV is not a series that is read: it is built every day from the stocks it holds. Every Friday, three index signals are read. If all three say BUY, the book holds the parent's stocks; otherwise it holds half GOLDBEES and half cash. A switch is made on the next session at the (open + high + low + close) ÷ 4 of each instrument, paying 10.4 bps on every rupee traded. *[the real signals, trades and NAVs are printed in §4.0a]*
- **One decision.** On a Friday review, look up the latest weekly row of the three Master files. If the three SIGN cells are all BUY, the target is the parent. The trade, if the target changed, is on the next session. *[one real decision, its three signs and its trade are printed in §4.0a]*
- **The result of one path.** Units of each stock, GOLDBEES and cash are fixed between trades. Each day the NAV is Σ units × close. The CAGR and drawdown come from that daily NAV. *[printed in §4.0a]*
- **One null draw.** The real path is 18 spells: 9 in and 9 out, with fixed lengths. A draw keeps the 18 lengths and shuffles which in-spell goes where, and which out-spell goes where. The book then holds the parent in those new weeks, with the same stocks and costs. 2,000 draws per book and cost. *[the first 20 weeks of a real and a shuffled series are printed in §4.0a]*
- **The ranking.** The rule's CAGR is placed among the 2,000 draws' CAGRs: the percentile is the share of draws it beats (ties count half); the p-value is the chance of doing as well by luck. 90 or more is a pass.
- **The comparators.** Holding the parent is what the book would do with no rule. The constant mix holds the same average share in the parent, all the time: it shows how much of any drawdown benefit is only less exposure. The 100% sleeve is the other end.

**Point-in-time rule.** A decision at d reads Master rows dated at or before d only (§3 step 3). A trade is on a session after d. The engine asserts both for every decision, counts them, and writes the counts to `audit.json`. The Master rows before 2021 were produced afterwards by the production code and are not signals traded at the time; this is disclosed, not corrected.

**Execution and cost.** AGENT.md R1 (next-session OHLC4, price return, 10.4 bps a side, weights drift) with these specifics: LBES trades pay the cost; the first position at T0 is free; no sale at the end; GOLDBEES uses the de-spiked fill (X02).

**Effective sample size and power.** 9 round trips per book, identical across books; ~400 weekly decisions. The independent unit for timing is the round trip. MDE: §3 step 12, computed after the run from the draws, with the circular-shift SD as a pilot. T01's MDE of 12 to 18 pp/yr per book (F-AA-M01) is the prior; this test has more draws but the same number of independent books.

**What the null controls for, and what it does not.** The null controls for the fraction of time in the parent and the number of switches, and nothing else. It does not control for which weeks the sleeve is held, so gold's 2024-26 rally lifts any draw that sits in the sleeve in those years (not matched, in §2); R3 and the comparators bound this. It does not control for the stale-week and backfill issues below.

### F-AA-S04 — The three-index switch times the parents better than the same weeks placed at random, by 12.0, 7.4, 10.7 and 7.8 pp a year of CAGR (percentiles 96.0, 86.4, 95.0, 92.4) and by shallower worst falls (percentiles 97.5, 94.7, 97.5, 97.6), 2019 → 2026-08, after 20.8 bps

**Status:** PROVISIONAL. All gates V1-V5 pass; C1 (3 of 4 books at the 90th percentile) and C2 (4 of 4) pass as pre-registered. It rests on one window that was seen before the test, and it does not survive R4.
**Evidence:** `evidence/headline/` (`draws.csv`, `stats.csv`, `audit.json`), `analysis.log`, §4.1 and §4.4 above.
**Claim:** against 2,000 random reorderings of its own 9 in-spells and 9 out-spells (the same weeks invested, the same number of switches), the rule's CAGR beats 96.0%, 86.4%, 95.0% and 92.4% in SCMO_0003, 0007, 0010 and 0029; SCMO_0007 misses the pre-registered 90. The rule's worst fall (−15% to −20%) is shallower than 94.7% to 97.6% of the draws (their median fall is −27% to −32%). The result holds at 0 and 50 bps (R2), against the circular-shift null (R1, all four books ≥ 93), and with LIQUIDCASE instead of LBES (R3). The constant mix at the same average exposure earns 12.6% to 19.4% and falls 21% to 27%, so the gain is not exposure alone.
**Falsified by:** a pre-registered rerun on decisions that were not seen. On the live decisions alone (2021-11-01 → 2026-08-21, R4, own spells and own null) CAGR percentiles are 85.7, 80.1, 92.0 and 84.9: 1 of 4 reaches 90, so the 2019-21 backfilled decisions carry part of the result. The smallest edge detectable at 80% power is 15 to 19 pp a year of CAGR and 20 to 30 pp of worst fall (§4.3, 9 round trips), which is large next to the edges observed (7 to 12 pp of CAGR).
