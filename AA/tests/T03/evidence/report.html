Does the three-index switch choose when to hold the parent better than chance?

2019-01-02 → 2026-08-21 (SCMO_0007 from 2019-01-04) · four parents of the AA books · decisions every Friday, trades next session at OHLC4 · 10.4 bps a side · every number is computed from evidence/headline/

The answer

Yes on the full 2019 → 2026 window, with a catch. Holding the parent only when the Nifty 100, Midcap 100 and Smallcap 100 are all BUY, and a 50/50 GOLDBEES and cash sleeve otherwise, beat randomly timed versions of itself in 3 of 4 books on return (pre-registered pass: 3 of 4 at the 90th percentile) and in 4 of 4 on the depth of the worst fall.

The catch: judged only on the decisions that were live in production (2021-11 → 2026-08), return beats the 90th percentile in 1 of 4 books, and the decisions before 2021 are a backfill by the production code. The rule was also chosen after seeing 2014-2026 results, so every result here is in-sample and PROVISIONAL.

What to be careful about

  • The null is "same weeks in the market, placed at random". A pass says the rule's timing is better than random placement, not that it is better than holding. It was out of the market in about half the weeks, so compare with the constant mix below.
  • Nothing is out of sample. The rule was chosen after seeing 2014-2026. Decisions before 2021-11 are the production code run backwards, not signals traded at the time. On the live decisions only, return is 1 of 4 at the 90th percentile.
  • Nine round trips per book is all the evidence there is. The smallest return edge detectable at 80% power is 15 to 19 pp a year.
  • The books are one market. SCMO_0007 misses the 90th percentile on return (86.4).

state(d) = IN if SIGN_Nifty100 = SIGN_MC100 = SIGN_SC100 = BUY (latest row dated on or before d), else OUT IN → 100% parent basket OUT → 50% GOLDBEES + 50% cash

Definitions

  • Percentile: (draws worse + 0.5 × draws tied) ÷ draws × 100. p-value: (1 + draws at least as good) ÷ (draws + 1).
  • CAGR: NAV_end^(252 ÷ (sessions − 1)) − 1. Worst fall: the most negative NAV ÷ running peak − 1.
  • Random timing (N4.9): the rule's in-spells and out-spells reordered; same weeks invested, same number of switches, same spell lengths. 2,000 seeded draws per book and cost.
  • Live window: 2021-11-01 → 2026-08-21, the decisions that were in production.
