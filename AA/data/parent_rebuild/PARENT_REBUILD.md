## What was done

For each parent (SCMO_0003, 0007, 0010, 0029), the index was rebuilt from its own baskets and stock prices, then compared day by day with `parentIndices.csv`.

| Input | File | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Baskets | `Historical Data/frozen_2026-09-25/Rebalance_history/Rebalance_history_by_scid_<parent>.csv` | ISIN, weight, `date` (decision), `availableDate` (effective); rows with `skipped=true` are not rebalances |
| Stock prices | `analysis/cache/keyed_ohlcv_eikon_v2.parquet` (Eikon, per ISIN, 2021-06-01 → 2026-08-21) | open, high, low, close; split/bonus/demerger adjusted, not dividend adjusted |
| Second price source | `analysis/cache/keyed_ohlcv_v2.parquet` (Kite) | cross-check only |
| Calendar | `Historical Data/frozen_2026-09-25/Data/calendar.csv` plus published dates plus days most stocks trade | sessions |
| Published NAV | `Historical Data/parentIndices.csv` | the comparison |

**Formulas.**

- Switch day R = the basket's `availableDate`. Switch price of stock i: P_i = (open + high + low + close) ÷ 4 on R.
- Value of the old basket at the switch: V = Σ old shares_i × P_i.
- New shares: shares_i = V × w_i ÷ P_i, with w_i the listed weights renormalised to sum to 1.
- Every other day: level(t) = Σ shares_i × close_i(t). A stock with no close carries its last close.
- The rebuild starts at the basket in force on 2021-11-01 from that basket's own switch, and is scaled to 100 on 2021-11-01 like the published series.
- No costs, no dividends: the published index is price return (the gap on non-rebalance days is about 0 pp/yr).
- CAGR = (level_end ÷ level_start)^(365.25 ÷ calendar days) − 1. Tracking error = std(daily return difference) × √252.
