## 6. Issues

### F-AA-D01 — OPEN
**Inputs:** D06, X01

In the 2014-21 Eikon price file, 13 ISINs' histories end on 2016-06-30 although the stocks kept trading (for example Lakshmi Vilas Bank to 2020, JMC Projects to 2023, Piramal Enterprises to 2025), and 2 held ISINs (INE202B01038, INE093A01041) have no rows at all. The 13 share one end date, which points to the fetch or the table, not the stocks.

**Effect if ignored.** Part of the parent baskets from mid-2016 to 2018 has no price (see Stock prices). The harness carries the last close or moves the weight to other names, so the 2014-21 parent rebuild, and every 2014-21 result built on it (final strategy, T01's 2015-21 window), is affected by an amount not yet measured.

**Handling.** Re-query ohlcv_eikon for these quote_ids with the VPN on. If the table has the rows, rebuild the file with a script; if not, find another source. Rerun the 2014-21 results.

### F-AA-D02 — ACCEPTED
**Inputs:** D01

The NSE calendar built from Refinitiv .NSEI lacks five real sessions since 2014: 2020-02-01 (Saturday, Budget), 2023-11-12 (Sunday, Muhurat), 2024-03-02 (Saturday, special session), 2025-02-01 (Saturday, Budget) and 2026-02-01 (Sunday, Budget). GOLDBEES from Kite has a row on all five; the published NAVs and GOLDBEES from Eikon on the two Budget days of 2025 and 2026. Yet the same calendar holds other special sessions (2016-10-30, 2019-10-27, 2020-11-14, 2024-01-20, 2024-05-18), so its coverage of special sessions is inconsistent.

**Effect if ignored.** An engine on this calendar folds those days into the next session. Negligible for multi-year returns; day-level comparisons are off by one day on those dates.

**Handling.** Accepted and noted. A project-built calendar would add the two days.

### F-AA-D03 — HANDLED
**Inputs:** D14, D15

The published NAV files skip 5 NSE sessions inside 2021-11-01 to 2026-08-21: the Muhurat sessions 2021-11-04, 2022-10-24, 2024-11-01 and 2025-10-21, and the special session 2024-05-18.

**Effect if ignored.** A comparison rebased on a session one series lacks gains or loses a day's return.

**Handling.** Compare on common dates only; rebase on a session both series hold.

### F-AA-D04 — HANDLED
**Inputs:** D06, D07, D09

Eikon lacks special weekend and Muhurat sessions: GOLDBEES lacks 2016-10-30, 2019-10-27, 2020-11-14, 2024-01-20 and 2024-05-18; stocks lack 2024-01-20 and 2024-05-18 (and the 2014-21 sessions listed in sessions_absent_from_stock_file.csv).

**Effect if ignored.** Prices are carried from the previous session on those days.

**Handling.** Carry the last close; count the days.

### F-AA-D05 — CONVENTION SET
**Inputs:** D11

The cash file is posted on its own calendar: many dates that are not NSE sessions (most of them Sundays) and some NSE sessions absent. Past its last date (2026-08-09) the engines extend it two ways: flat (T01, final strategy 2014-21) and at the trailing rate (final strategy 2021-26).

**Effect if ignored.** Tiny on returns (cash moves about 0.02% a day), but two tests could disagree on the same days.

**Handling.** One convention for the project (see the component map). T02 follows it.

### F-AA-D06 — ACCEPTED
**Inputs:** D02, D04

Index OHLC: Nifty Smallcap 250 lacks 7 sessions, 5 in a row from 2022-02-24 to 2022-03-03; SC100's open, high and low are frozen at 3584.17 from 2011-04-01 to 2011-04-07 while its close moves; Nifty 100 lacks 2006-01-24 and SC100 lacks 3 sessions in 2005 and 2011.

**Effect if ignored.** The Smallcap 250 signal (comparator only) is computed on gapped data. The SC100 problems are before 2014, outside every backtest window, but they enter the supertrend's history.

**Handling.** Accepted for now; re-pull Smallcap 250 for 2022 if it is ever used in a test.

### F-AA-D07 — OPEN
**Inputs:** D05

Whole parent baskets are flagged skipped=True (0007 on 2020-03-16 and 2021-09-16; 0029 on some dates). Their weights are drifted, not equal, which suggests 'reviewed, not traded'. The harness trades to them as if they were new baskets.

**Effect if ignored.** Extra turnover and a small re-weighting on those dates in the rebuilt parents.

**Handling.** Decide whether a skipped basket should trade; if not, change the harness in a new run.

### F-AA-D08 — OPEN
**Inputs:** D07

The 2021-26 Eikon price file was saved by a one-off command on 2026-09-25; no script in the project rebuilds it.

**Effect if ignored.** The file cannot be reproduced if lost or questioned.

**Handling.** Add data/build_prices_2021_26.py that re-creates it from ohlcv_eikon and checks its sha256 against the inventory.

### F-AA-D09 — OPEN
**Inputs:** D11, D12, D14, D15, D16

No record of who exported the cash file, the published NAV files and the AA weight files, from which system, or when; the MAUc1 export date is not recorded and the file sits in Downloads.

**Effect if ignored.** A number built on these cannot be traced to its source.

**Handling.** Manu to name the sources (open questions); copy the MAUc1 export into Historical Data with its sha256.

### F-AA-D10 — OPEN
**Inputs:** X01

Over 2021-11-03 to 2026-08-21 the rebuilt parents run below the published NAVs on all four parents (see Reconciliation), most on 0029.

**Effect if ignored.** Levels of anything built on the rebuild are understated; rule-versus-hold comparisons on the same rebuild are not.

**Handling.** Before quoting a level, say it is the rebuild. Find out the published series' dividend basis (F-AA-D09).
