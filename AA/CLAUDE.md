- **Data boundary (read first; standing rule, 2026-11-06).** The only data you may read is what [`data/APPROVED_DATA.md`](data/APPROVED_DATA.md) lists as `APPROVED`. It gives each file's path and through-date and the latest usable date per role. Everything else on disk is off-limits: newer-looking files, `PENDING` and `SUPERSEDED` rows, anything in Downloads. Do not edit, extend, rebuild or re-fetch data on your own initiative. If the approved data is stale for your test, a file you need is missing or `PENDING`, or anything is unclear, **stop and ask Manu**; do not work around it. Run `python data/check_approved_data.py` before a test's Step 1 and at the top of its `run.sh`; a non-zero exit means stop and ask.
