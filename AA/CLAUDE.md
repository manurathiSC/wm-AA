- **Glossary of rule and run ids (T06, T08, T11, T14-T16).**
  - *States, read every Friday:* **IN** = Nifty 100, Midcap 100 and Smallcap 100 weekly supertrend SIGNs all BUY; otherwise the three-index switch is **OUT**. **OUT-BUY** = switch OUT and the weekly MAUc1 gold SIGN BUY; **OUT-SELL** = switch OUT and MAUc1 SELL. **B** = the indices whose own SIGN is BUY in an OUT week, **k** = how many (0, 1 or 2; 3 would be IN).
  - *Base sleeve* (what the book holds when OUT, before any variant): **50** = 50% GOLDBEES / 50% cash (T06, T11 Part A); **80** = 80% GOLDBEES / 20% cash when gold is BUY and 20% / 80% when gold is SELL (T08, T11 Part B). Cash = the LBES liquid-ETF level. IN always holds the parent's basket.
  - *Rule ids* (`<base>-<OUT-SELL holding>`; they differ only in the OUT-SELL weeks): **`-orig`** the base sleeve unchanged (T06/T08/T11); **`-liq`** 100% liquid ETF (T14); **`-half`** 50% parent basket + 50% GOLDBEES (T14); **`-idx`** the liquid ETF plus each index in B, 1/(k+1) each, rebalanced when B changes (T15); **`-all`** 25% each of Nifty 100, Midcap 100, Smallcap 100 and the liquid ETF whatever their SIGNs, set on entry and left to drift until the book leaves OUT-SELL (T16). Index legs are the price indices (no dividends, no fund cost) filled at their own OHLC4.
  - *Windows:* **W1** 2014-09-01 → 2019-01-02 (rebuilt parents, T11's inputs); **W2** 2019-01-02 → 2026-10-30 in T14/T15 (published-NAV calendar, T08's inputs), extended to 2026-11-20 in T16; **live** = W2's decisions from 2021-11-01.
  - *Robustness runs (T14-T16):* **R1** costs 0 and 50 bps round trip instead of 20.8; **R2** LIQUIDCASE as the cash leg; **R3** later start (W1 from 2015-01-02, W2 live from 2021-11-01); **R4** leave one OUT-SELL spell out (the new rule holds cash in that spell only); **R5** a 0.5 pp/yr fund-cost drag on each index leg. C1, C2, ... are per-test comparisons and are defined in each test's §2.
